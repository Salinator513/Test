# Branch B001 — Current Accepted State

## Research Objective

Under a fixed inference-compute budget, which design features of a role-separated multi-agent research loop most reduce unsupported claims and cumulative reasoning errors compared with repeated single-agent prompting?

## Completed Cycle

C0002 is complete and accepted through Cycle Archivist review.

C0001 established that the current evidence does not support general superiority of fresh role-separated or multi-agent workflows under fixed compute, that the reviewed literature does not isolate fresh role separation from added or differently allocated compute, and that evaluation must be claim-level, coverage-sensitive, and capable of tracing error lineage.

C0002 investigated informational equivalence and state transfer for the proposed persistent-versus-fresh contrast. It corrected the estimand from an undefined invocation-boundary label to observable factors involving prior-trace access, package representation, predeclared retention, dependency and relation preservation, transformation and compression, information geometry, write and retrieval loss, prompts and permissions, sampling and selection, and full resource allocation. C0002 added a fourteen-source primary-research ledger but no branch-local controlled estimate of an independent fresh-role effect.

## Current Hypotheses

- **C0001-EXP-01 — Independent fresh-role effect:** Unresolved and weakened. A conditional residual effect after full controls remains logically possible, but no accepted evidence establishes its direction or magnitude and C0002 shows that the treatment is not yet operationally defined.
- **C0001-EXP-02 — External memory and provenance:** Qualified. Structured external records are active information and cost interventions in some long-horizon settings (`C0001-SRC-12`; `C0002-SRC-04`, `C0002-SRC-05`, `C0002-SRC-14`). Whether explicit provenance, typed state, or external memory improves B001 outcomes at matched information and compute remains unresolved.
- **C0001-EXP-03 — Structured verification:** Qualified. Staged verification and privileged error-location information can improve some outcomes (`C0001-SRC-10`, `C0001-SRC-11`), while generic intrinsic self-correction can fail (`C0001-SRC-09`). Equal-compute superiority over other workflows is unresolved.
- **C0001-EXP-04 — Context-partition benefit:** Narrow hypothesis. Trace restriction or partitioning may help under severe corruption, interference, mismatched history, redundancy, or poor information position (`C0001-SRC-01`; `C0002-SRC-02`, `C0002-SRC-03`, `C0002-SRC-08`), but fresh invocation is not isolated.
- **C0001-EXP-05 / C0002-EXP-03 — Interface-loss risk:** Qualified supported risk. Package construction, permissions, compression, and handoffs may omit, mutate, retype, or separate valid claims, qualifications, support links, temporal or causal relations, objections, and unresolved dependencies. The risk is strongest in dependency-dense tasks, but the independent contribution of freshness is unmeasured.
- **C0001-EXP-07 — Function-specific replacement:** Qualified hypothesis. Trained, deterministic, privileged, or human checks may replace particular weak verification functions. Replacement of complete critic, evidence, or integration roles remains unsupported.
- **Verifier-quality hypothesis:** Independent, privileged, deterministic, trained, heterogeneous-model, or human verification signals may outperform a generic same-model critic at comparable cost.
- **Diversity-quality hypothesis:** Role prompts or multiple samples may help only when they produce nonredundant supported facts or independent correct error detections, not merely stylistic or semantic variation.
- **C0002-EXP-01 — Residual-boundary null:** Unresolved. Package-only persistent and package-only fresh execution may have no stable difference after a verified noncollapsed residual manipulation and full controls. Current non-identification and possible treatment collapse do not establish a zero effect.
- **C0002-EXP-02 — Conditional prior-trace interference:** Qualified hypothesis. Excluding prior trace may help when earlier material is misleading, mismatched, redundant, prematurely committed, or poorly positioned, provided valid dependencies remain available and gains survive coverage and compute controls.
- **C0002-EXP-05 — Qualified structured-state hypothesis:** Typed, selective, relational, or retrieved state may outperform raw accumulated trace in some regimes, but comparative dominance over freshness or other factors is not established because components, information, and compute are bundled.
- **C0002-EXP-07 — Compression and transformation as active factors:** Summarization, deletion, normalization, prioritization, reordering, retyping, and bounded relay size can change retained information, salience, and cost. Direction is unresolved and no single compression metric or monotonic effect is accepted.
- **State-write bottleneck hypothesis:** Write-time promotion, omission, classification, and relation preservation may explain more downstream failure than retrieval in some regimes. Exact-package replay and oracle-versus-stored-versus-retrieved comparisons can discriminate this mechanism.
- **Context-salience and retrieval-geometry hypothesis:** Order, position, adjacency, redundancy, and surrounding context volume may change effective use even when the same reference inventory is retained.
- **Relation-preservation hypothesis:** Packages preserving claim-support, qualification, contradiction, temporal, causal, and unresolved-question relations may outperform flat packages with identical atomic-item coverage.
- **Task × model × retention interaction hypothesis:** Stronger models and dependency-dense tasks may benefit more from full context and suffer more from lossy packages, while separable or interference-heavy tasks may benefit from filtering. This interaction is plausible but not quantified.
- **Fresh-context-by-state hypothesis, superseded wording:** The earlier scalar claim that freshness may help when state is “complete” is retained only as separate interaction hypotheses involving measured trace access, item retention, dependency and relation coverage, salience, write and retrieval loss, task structure, and model capability.
- **C0002-EXP-08 — Stage-specific sensitivity:** Deferred speculation. Some transitions may benefit more from reset or trace restriction, but no accepted evidence identifies a sensitive stage after controlling the primary factors.

## Alternative Explanations

- **Functional scaffolding, external memory, and verifier information:** Mandatory generation, challenge, evidence, and revision stages, together with structured state and higher-quality verification signals, may account for useful effects; fresh invocation boundaries may add little once information and compute are equalized.
- **Budget allocation, sampling, and selection:** Candidate count, repeated sampling, selector quality, prompt specialization, tools, actual consumed compute, saved-budget policy, and favorable metric or condition selection may explain much apparent architecture gain.
- **Prior-trace interference:** Earlier turns may cause anchoring, premature commitment, task interference, redundancy, or retrieval competition. A fresh stage may help because it excludes particular history, not because a new container is intrinsically beneficial.
- **Context position and retrieval geometry:** Full trace can preserve all required information but bury or separate it; a concise package can improve usability by repositioning content without changing factual inventory.
- **State-write versus retrieval failure:** A final failure can result from evidence never written into state, evidence written but not retrieved, or evidence retrieved but misused. These mechanisms require different interventions.
- **Structured-state mechanisms:** Condensation, typed ledgers, temporal graphs, append-only state, conflict handling, and selective retrieval can improve outcomes in some settings but also add information, tools, calls, and compute.
- **Minimal null and treatment collapse:** After all observable factors are matched, no stable residual boundary effect may remain. Alternatively, the comparison may implement no distinct treatment at all; package-matched equality would then be an implementation-equivalence result.
- **Decomposition plus centralized verification:** Parallel evidence gathering may help on separable tasks when a central validation bottleneck prevents propagation, while the same structure may be harmful on sequential or dependency-dense tasks.
- **Omission and coverage artifact:** A workflow can lower unsupported-claim counts by making fewer factual claims, reducing coverage, omitting difficult dependencies, or failing the task.
- **Metric and evaluator choice:** Reported factuality or correction gains can vary with generator, error type, coding scheme, evaluator, shared-model bias, and adjudication procedure.
- **Hybrid interaction account:** Harmful-trace exclusion, valid-dependency loss, write quality, salience, reader capability, task structure, prompts, and resource allocation may jointly determine outcomes and reverse sign across regimes.

## Known Objections and Contradictions

- Same-model agents can produce correlated or redundant judgments; agreement is not independent validation.
- Added roles or stages can consume fixed budget, duplicate work, create handoff overhead, and introduce additional error-propagation paths.
- Apparent gains can come from added sampling, revision, retrieval, external memory, privileged verifier information, prompt tuning, tools, selector quality, or saved-budget reallocation.
- Fixed token caps, stored-state budgets, or maximum steps can conceal unequal prompt processing, context processing, package construction, completion, final-answer, orchestration, tool, candidate, and selector costs.
- A workflow can lower unsupported-claim counts by making fewer claims, reducing factual coverage, omitting qualifications, or failing task requirements.
- Same-model or LLM-as-judge evaluation may share the tested system’s biases; factuality metrics vary by generator and error type.
- **Positive debate versus budget-aware neutral or negative evidence:** `C0001-SRC-05` reports added-compute gains, while `C0001-SRC-01`, `C0001-SRC-03`, and `C0001-SRC-04` report ties, losses, or simpler-baseline competitiveness under tighter accounting. Differences in inference, tuning, models, and tasks explain part but not all of the tension.
- **Self-refinement versus weak intrinsic self-correction:** `C0001-SRC-08` reports gains, while `C0001-SRC-09` reports failures or degradation. `C0001-SRC-10` suggests error-location and verifier information may be moderators.
- **Context protection versus interface loss:** `C0001-SRC-01`, `C0002-SRC-02`, `C0002-SRC-03`, and `C0002-SRC-08` support narrow contamination, interference, or salience mechanisms, while `C0001-SRC-02`, `C0001-SRC-07`, `C0002-SRC-01`, `C0002-SRC-05`, `C0002-SRC-06`, and `C0002-SRC-07` support dependency-loss and propagation risks.
- **Compression efficiency versus interface loss:** `C0002-SRC-04` reports efficiency and outcome gains or preservation from condensation, while `C0002-SRC-01` reports reversals under insufficient relays and `C0002-SRC-05` reports write-side loss. Compression quality, retention policy, model capability, and compute are moderators.
- **Structured-state gains versus component attribution:** `C0002-SRC-14` reports strong benchmark results, but graph construction, append-only state, retrieval, tools, conflict handling, and summaries are bundled. Positive performance does not identify which component caused it.
- **More context versus usable context:** Full trace can preserve evidence while reducing salience; concise packages can foreground evidence while deleting dependencies. Inventory retention and information geometry must be measured separately.
- **Scalar completeness versus multidimensional retention:** A package can fill every field and omit a critical relation, or be concise while preserving every predeclared dependency. Completeness inferred from successful outcomes is circular.
- **Null result versus treatment collapse:** Package-matched equality can mean a true zero effect, no distinct residual treatment, weak manipulation, task insensitivity, opposing subgroup effects, or unreliable measurement.
- **Traceability versus support:** Claim IDs, citations, or provenance links improve traceability but do not by themselves establish correct or complete support.
- **Semantic similarity versus epistemic preservation:** A claim can remain textually similar while its status, qualification, support, contradiction relation, or uncertainty changes.
- **Evidence volume versus independence:** Benchmark reuse and aggregated measurement datasets limit independent replication counts. Repeated same-model interpretation adds no evidential weight.
- **Transfer tension:** Findings from short-answer reasoning, multi-turn generation, task switching, memory QA, summarization, navigation, and agentic benchmarks may not transfer to open-ended multi-source research synthesis.

## Rejected or Superseded Claims

- **Rejected:** General superiority of multi-agent, seven-role, or fresh role-separated workflows over repeated or staged single-model prompting under fixed compute.
- **Rejected:** An intrinsic or general fresh-conversation-container benefit from the current evidence.
- **Rejected:** The C0001 Explorer’s four-condition ladder as a clean causal isolation design.
- **Rejected:** The C0002 Explorer’s original 2 × 2 boundary-by-state design as a causal factorial unless prior-trace access is identically controlled or represented as a separate factor and state dimensions are independently predefined.
- **Rejected:** `C0002-EXP-04`, a scalar state-completeness threshold, as framed. Nonlinear effects across separately measured retention, dependency, relation, geometry, and cost variables remain possible.
- **Rejected:** Package-only persistent versus package-only fresh equality as proof of a boundary null when no nontrivial residual manipulation is defined and verified.
- **Rejected:** Full-trace persistent versus package-only or fresh differences as evidence for freshness; such contrasts estimate access, context position, retention, retrieval opportunity, and associated cost.
- **Rejected:** Same-model role agreement as independent corroboration.
- **Rejected:** Positive added-compute debate, refinement, verification, sampling, memory, condensation, predictive-writing, or graph-retrieval results as direct proof of an independent fresh-role effect or equal-compute superiority.
- **Rejected:** Citation presence, provenance-link counts, Markdown field completion, raw unsupported-claim counts, or semantic similarity as sufficient epistemic evaluation or state-retention evidence.
- **Rejected:** Performance loss after role deletion as proof of unique role value unless handling of freed compute is declared.
- **Rejected:** Broad deterministic replacement of whole critic, evidence, or integration roles.
- **Rejected:** Lower unsupported-claim counts as improvement without claim count, factual coverage, citation correctness and completeness, task completion, usefulness, and omission severity.
- **Rejected:** Counting studies that reuse LongMemEval, LoCoMo, or aggregated factuality datasets as fully independent benchmark replications.
- **Superseded wording:** “Role separation may reduce cumulative error by limiting objectives and context” is retained only through the narrowed contamination, context-partition, and interface-loss hypotheses with the controls and objections above.
- **Superseded wording:** “Externalized memory and explicit provenance may matter more than agent count” is replaced by qualified structured-state, write-retention, and provenance hypotheses; comparative dominance remains unresolved.
- **Superseded wording:** “Fresh execution may help when state is complete” is replaced by separate, preregistered interaction hypotheses involving trace access, item retention, dependency and relation preservation, salience, write and retrieval loss, task structure, model capability, and resource allocation.
- **Reclassified:** `C0002-EXP-06` hidden-context privilege is retained only as prior-trace access asymmetry, an observable design factor and confound rather than a substantive empirical effect claim.

## Current Research Status

C0002 is completed and archived. The branch now has a qualified thirty-source external record across C0001 and C0002, with explicit benchmark-overlap limits, but no branch-local controlled experiment has established an independent fresh-role or residual conversation-container effect.

The principal accepted direction is a qualified observable information-access and relay-fidelity tradeoff. Prior trace can interfere, anchor, distract, or become hard to retrieve, and structured or selective state can improve outcomes in some represented regimes. The same filtering and state-writing operations can discard valid dependencies, qualifications, relations, objections, and support. No universal direction is accepted.

The branch’s immediate problem is operational, not another general search for multi-agent gains: define a canonical typed package, a predeclared information and dependency inventory, a noncollapsed access contrast, valid manipulation and outcome measures, and a full compute and saved-budget rule.

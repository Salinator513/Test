# Branch B001 — Current Accepted State

## Research Objective

Under a fixed inference-compute budget, which design features of a role-separated multi-agent research loop most reduce unsupported claims and cumulative reasoning errors compared with repeated single-agent prompting?

## Completed Cycle

C0006 is complete and accepted through Cycle Archivist review.

- **C0001:** The reviewed evidence did not support general superiority of fresh role-separated or multi-agent workflows under fixed compute, did not isolate fresh role separation from added or differently allocated compute, and required claim-level, coverage-sensitive, lineage-aware evaluation.
- **C0002:** “Fresh versus persistent” was decomposed into observable prior-trace access, state representation and retention, transformation, information geometry, write and retrieval loss, prompts, permissions, tools, sampling, selection, and full resources. No residual fresh-boundary effect was isolated.
- **C0003:** Relation or metadata availability was separated from equivalent-content representation. Exact replay was limited to package-construction control. Reference inventory, written package, recorded selection, and final-output checkpoints were separated.
- **C0004:** The branch narrowed to prospective apparatus validation for a direct claim–support object at the External Evidence Researcher → Alternative Theorist handoff. Selection was recognized as behaviorally non-neutral; authority-cue, reader-bottleneck, role-aligned scoring, and narrow-codebook requirements were strengthened.
- **C0005:** No pair-level B001 audit was executed. “Semantic use” was replaced by observable downstream relation-preserving incorporation. Development and held-out audit separation became mandatory; the one-to-one primary object was restricted to an eligible subset within a broader many-to-many schema; reliability became coverage-, prevalence-, clustering-, role-, and metric-sensitive.
- **C0006:** No development or held-out audit was executed. A proposed denominator hierarchy and immediate gate were replaced by a linked denominator-and-observability lattice and a gate deferred until the downstream decision, population or stratum, sampling frame, inferential unit, codebook, role-relevance rubric, resource rule, uncertainty target, and false-passage/failure consequences are frozen.

The retained external ledger across C0001–C0006 contains ninety-five sources. This is a source count, not an independence count.

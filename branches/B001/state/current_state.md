# Branch B001 — Current Accepted State

## Research Objective

Under a fixed inference-compute budget, which design features of a role-separated multi-agent research loop most reduce unsupported claims and cumulative reasoning errors compared with repeated single-agent prompting?

## Completed Cycle

C0005 is complete and accepted through Cycle Archivist review.

C0001 established that the reviewed evidence does not support general superiority of fresh role-separated or multi-agent workflows under fixed compute, does not isolate fresh role separation from added or differently allocated compute, and requires claim-level, coverage-sensitive, lineage-aware evaluation.

C0002 replaced the undefined fresh-versus-persistent label with observable factors: prior-trace access, package representation and retention, transformation and compression, dependency and relation preservation, information geometry, write and retrieval loss, prompts and permissions, tools, sampling and selection, and full resource allocation. It found no branch-local controlled estimate of a residual fresh-role effect.

C0003 separated metadata availability from metadata representation, limited exact-package replay to control of package-construction variance, required an explicit selected-subset checkpoint before retrieval claims, and recast a reference package as a separately costed diagnostic upper bound. It found no direct corrected metadata experiment in open-ended multi-source research synthesis under matched full resources.

C0004 narrowed the immediate frontier to validation of a minimal direct claim–support apparatus at the External Evidence Researcher → Alternative Theorist handoff. It established that evidence selection and presentation must be treated as behaviorally consequential, strengthened authority-cue and selection–incorporation objections, and required role-aligned epistemic scoring.

C0005 did not execute a pair-level B001 audit and did not establish that the selected handoff is measurable. It added a sixteen-source methodological ledger and replaced latent “semantic use” language with observable downstream relation-preserving incorporation. It requires a frozen development/audit design, coverage- and metric-sensitive reliability, explicit observational boundaries for selection and write loss, and predeclared local failure actions.

The retained external ledger across C0001–C0005 contains seventy-five sources. This is a source count, not an independence count.

## Current Hypotheses

- **Independent fresh-role effect (`C0001-EXP-01`):** Unresolved and weakened. A conditional residual effect remains logically possible, but no accepted evidence establishes direction or magnitude and no residual treatment is operationally defined.
- **External memory and provenance (`C0001-EXP-02`):** Qualified. Structured external records are active information and resource interventions; matched B001 effects remain unresolved.
- **Structured verification (`C0001-EXP-03`):** Qualified. Staged verification and privileged error-location information can help in some settings; equal-resource superiority remains unresolved.
- **Context-partition benefit (`C0001-EXP-04`):** Narrow. Trace restriction may help under corruption, interference, mismatched history, redundancy, or poor information position if valid dependencies remain available.
- **Interface-loss risk (`C0001-EXP-05` / `C0002-EXP-03`):** Qualified supported risk. Packages can omit, mutate, retype, or separate claims, qualifications, links, objections, and dependencies.
- **Function-specific replacement (`C0001-EXP-07`):** Qualified. Trained, deterministic, privileged, heterogeneous-model, or human checks may replace specific weak functions; replacement of complete roles is unsupported.
- **Verifier-quality hypothesis:** Independent, privileged, deterministic, trained, heterogeneous-model, or human verification may outperform a generic same-model critic at comparable cost.
- **Diversity-quality hypothesis:** Multiple roles or samples may help only when they produce nonredundant supported facts or independent correct error detections.
- **Residual-boundary null (`C0002-EXP-01`):** Unresolved. Package-only persistent and fresh execution may have no stable difference after a verified noncollapsed manipulation and full controls; non-identification does not establish zero.
- **Conditional prior-trace interference (`C0002-EXP-02`):** Qualified. Excluding prior trace may help when earlier material is misleading, mismatched, redundant, prematurely committed, or poorly positioned.
- **Qualified structured-state hypothesis (`C0002-EXP-05`):** Typed, selective, relational, or retrieved state may outperform raw trace in some regimes; comparative dominance is unestablished.
- **Compression and transformation (`C0002-EXP-07`):** Summarization, deletion, normalization, prioritization, reordering, retyping, and bounded relays change retained information, salience, and cost; direction is unresolved.
- **State-write bottleneck (`C0003-EXP-03`):** Unresolved without dominance wording. Writing, forced selection, and downstream incorporation may each contribute, and one final error may have multiple causes.
- **Context-salience and retrieval geometry (`C0003-EXP-04` / `C0004-ALT-01`):** Qualified evidence-backed mechanism hypothesis. Order, position, adjacency, redundancy, context volume, serialization, stopping, and format familiarity can affect use.
- **Relation-information hypothesis (`C0003-EXP-01` / `C0004-EXP-01`):** Correct direct claim–support or qualification relations may reduce reconstruction error when relations are dispersed, ambiguous, or dependency-dense. No effect is accepted.
- **Representation-equivalence hypothesis (`C0004-EXP-02`):** Controlled prose may reproduce typed-field performance when relation content and geometry are approximately matched. No superiority direction is accepted.
- **Epistemic-status preservation (`C0003-EXP-02`):** Deferred separate treatment. Labels may help preserve status, but correctness, stability, transitions, and fixed-resource effects are unresolved.
- **Task × model × retention × relation × ambiguity interaction:** Reader capability, retention, relation quality, ambiguity, dispersion, qualification density, package length, and complexity may moderate effects.
- **Local package sufficiency (`C0003-EXP-05`):** Retained only as task-, stage-, reader-, and outcome-relative; universal minimality is rejected.
- **Unresolved-question continuity (`C0003-EXP-06`):** Deferred speculation.
- **Authority-cue hypothesis (`C0004-ALT-02`):** Qualified evidence-backed risk in adjacent settings. Structured links or labels may alter behavior through perceived validation or evaluator alignment; direct transfer remains untested.
- **Apparatus-induced deliberation and selection (`C0004-ALT-03`):** Evidence-backed alternative explanation, not an accepted cause of any B001 result.
- **Reader-bottleneck null (`C0004-ALT-04`):** Unresolved. Correct metadata may have little stable average benefit when readers do not select or incorporate it.
- **Recorded-selection versus observable-incorporation correspondence:** Relation information may change recorded selection without changing observable incorporation, or incorporation may appear without recorded selection. Neither pattern identifies causal use.
- **Qualification-preservation hypothesis:** A relation benefit may appear primarily through preserved scope and modifiers rather than fewer unsupported claims.
- **Coverage–noise interaction:** Correct-link coverage and false or irrelevant relation exposure may have separable, sign-reversing effects.
- **Writer–validator privilege:** Reference or curated gains may be caused by correction, adjudication, validation labor, or privileged information rather than schema.
- **Prompt-substitution:** Strong relation-seeking instructions may recover some effects without typed fields while adding deliberation and compute.
- **Evaluator alignment:** Structured outputs may score better because treatment and evaluator formats align rather than because support is better.
- **Stage-specific sensitivity (`C0002-EXP-08`):** Deferred speculation; the selected handoff remains pragmatic only.
- **Prospective small-core feasibility (`C0005-EXP-01`, `C0005-EXP-06`):** A frozen atomic direct-support subset may pass a local audit on reliability, retained coverage, identity, role relevance, and uncertainty. No current B001 prevalence or reliability result exists.
- **Qualification-centered future apparatus (`C0005-EXP-02`):** Qualification may prove a better primary relation, but only through a new prospective protocol and held-out audit.
- **Identity-and-observability bottleneck (`C0005-EXP-03`, `C0005-ALT-02`):** Upstream support may be clear while downstream multidimensional lineage is unstable; causal use remains unidentified.
- **Sparse-reuse minimal null (`C0005-EXP-05`, `C0005-ALT-04`):** The selected handoff may legitimately contain too few role-relevant factual incorporation events.
- **Predeclared task-stratum interaction (`C0005-EXP-07`):** A factual stratum may be measurable even if the handoff as a whole is not; post hoc favorable strata are exploratory.
- **Coverage-failure and role-mismatch (`C0005-ALT-01`):** A reliable residue may cover too little qualification-rich, multi-source, synthesis-heavy, or nonfactual work.
- **Resource, prompt, filtering, and metric dependence (`C0005-ALT-03`):** Feasibility may depend on full-source access, expertise, repeated coding, adjudication, prompts, filtering, and metric construction.
- **Hybrid C0005 account:** Small-core feasibility, low coverage, identity instability, resource privilege, sparse reuse, and metric sensitivity may coexist.

# C0003 Dense Summary

## Research objective and frontier

C0003 pursued the operational frontier left by C0002: define a testable stage-package intervention before returning to persistent-versus-fresh or broad role-separated comparisons. The initial Explorer proposal combined a typed relational package, epistemic-status preservation, exact-package replay, write-versus-retrieval diagnostics, information geometry, package minimality, and unresolved-question continuity. The Critic narrowed this bundle to one primary metadata type at one stage transition and separated two estimands. A relation-absent versus relation-present comparison tests **metadata availability**, because the explicit relation is substantive information. A controlled-prose versus typed-field comparison carrying approximately equivalent relation content tests **metadata representation**, subject to geometry and resource checks.

## Work performed by each role

The Explorer proposed a minimal typed package preserving claim identity, status, support, qualifications, objections, unresolved questions, and relation links. It proposed oracle-versus-agent writing, exact replay, field ablations, geometry tests, write–retrieve–use checkpoints, dependency-density comparisons, coverage gates, and compute accounting.

The Critic identified two fatal problems: relation-present versus relation-absent is not information matched, and the proposal lacked one primary estimand. It required one relation or status type, separated availability from representation, recast the oracle as a predeclared independently validated reference package, limited exact replay to control of package-construction variance, and required explicit selected-subset logging before retrieval claims. It also rejected undefined write dominance, bundled metadata types, circular task strata, and false token matching.

The Internal Evidence Researcher mapped the proposal to accepted branch concepts. Existing state already supports claim IDs, epistemic categories, support and provenance, scope and limitations, objections, contradictions, rejected claims, unresolved questions, multidimensional state-transfer measures, coverage-sensitive lineage, exact replay as a proposed diagnostic, and full resource accounting. It found no internal causal evidence that explicit links or status fields improve downstream performance, that writing is dominant, or that a validated schema, hash, selected-subset object, lineage codebook, reliability threshold, or budget rule exists.

The External Evidence Researcher added fourteen primary sources. It searched narrowly for direct metadata-availability and representation studies, write–retrieve–use diagnostics, relation annotation, geometry confounds, uncertainty labels, and long-form transfer. No direct corrected experiment was found.

The Alternative Theorist preserved the relation-information account as the strongest experimental hypothesis while developing representation/geometry, authority-cue/evaluator-alignment, reader-bottleneck, resource-allocation, writer–validator privilege, and coverage–noise alternatives. It proposed corrupted-link, inert-placebo, framing, evaluator-blinding, selected-subset, prompt-equivalence, budget-reallocation, and task-strata tests.

The Integrator accepted the methodological split and diagnostic requirements, rejected any established typed-package benefit, and proposed six branch claims. The Archivist accepted those claims with two wording corrections: metadata and representation can change behavior in represented settings rather than establishing one general “material” benefit, and the listed mechanisms are live competing and potentially interacting explanations rather than proven coexisting causes in every setting.

## New evidence and source IDs

`C0003-SRC-01` provides full-paper claim–evidence annotations and reports difficult long-range link recovery. `C0003-SRC-02` shows that swapped webpage metadata can redirect answers while conflicting text remains fixed, without showing epistemic benefit. `C0003-SRC-03` and `C0003-SRC-04` show representation, serialization, order, delimiters, and model-format preferences affect outcomes, with geometry and resource confounds. `C0003-SRC-05` separates retrieval coverage and precision from generator utilization, noise sensitivity, hallucination, and faithfulness.

`C0003-SRC-06` through `C0003-SRC-08` provide claims linked to evidence sets, rationales, intermediate questions, or justifications with reported annotation agreement. `C0003-SRC-09` through `C0003-SRC-11` show that uncertainty representations are feasible but distribution-dependent and that positive uncertainty-alignment results bundle training, sampling, reward modeling, and PPO. `C0003-SRC-12` and `C0003-SRC-13` are transfer-relevant long-form writing studies that expose source-bias transfer, fabricated associations, new-information incorporation failures, and weak self-evaluation without isolating metadata or fixed compute. `C0003-SRC-14` shows that agreement for complex structured annotations depends on the selected distance function.

The sources are not fourteen independent demonstrations of one effect. Claim-verification, uncertainty, representation, and long-form-writing families overlap in method or purpose.

## Main and alternative interpretations

The main interpretation is a non-identified relation-information hypothesis: a correct explicit support or qualification relation may reduce reconstruction burden because it supplies substantive information. This is experimentally justified but not established.

Alternative accounts remain coequal. Typed formats may work through adjacency, compactness, delimiters, order, or model familiarity. Metadata may operate as authority or evaluator-alignment cues. Readers may fail to select or use correct relations. Reference packages may benefit from correction and validation privilege. Added calls, prompts, training, retrieval, candidates, selectors, or budget reallocation may explain apparent gains. Greater relation coverage may improve recall while increasing false links and noise.

## Claim-status changes

`C0003-INTG-01` is accepted as the availability-versus-representation methodological correction. `C0003-INTG-02` is accepted as a search-bounded direct-evidence gap. `C0003-INTG-03` is accepted as a qualified conclusion that metadata and representation can change behavior in represented settings. `C0003-INTG-04` is accepted as the observable write–retrieve–use requirement. `C0003-INTG-05` is accepted as a qualified competing-mechanism synthesis. `C0003-INTG-06` reinforces the existing role-separation and full-compute non-isolation gap.

`C0003-EXP-01` remains an unresolved relation-information hypothesis. `C0003-EXP-02` is deferred as a separate status-metadata treatment. `C0003-EXP-03` loses its dominance wording. `C0003-EXP-04` remains a general representation/geometry mechanism. `C0003-EXP-05` is rejected as universal minimality and retained only as local, task-relative sufficiency. `C0003-EXP-06` remains deferred speculation.

## Contradictions, reasoning changes, and rejected claims

The cycle preserves tensions between added relation information and pure representation, compact geometry and epistemic meaning, useful links and authority cues, coverage and noise, reference quality and privilege, exact replay and effective access, and writing versus selection versus use. A positive package result would not establish role separation. A null could reflect no effect, treatment collapse, weak annotations, reader non-use, task or model interactions, unreliable measurement, or insufficient power.

Rejected claims include an established typed-package benefit, information matching in relation-present comparisons, package-writing dominance, hash equality as retrieval proof, reference-package gains as schema effects, universal package size or serialization, monotonic benefit from more links, lower unsupported-claim counts without coverage gates, and generalization to fresh invocation or role separation.

## Unresolved questions and suggested next direction

The branch still needs one relation type, one stage transition, a predeclared inventory and relation graph, controlled-prose and typed encodings, geometry and framing controls, corrupted and inert links, explicit selected-subset logging, item-level multi-cause lineage, a validated annotation and adjudication scheme, coverage and usefulness thresholds, and a complete end-to-end budget and saved-budget rule.

The next direction is apparatus validation, not a broad effectiveness test. The first pilot should verify manipulation and measurement with relation-absent, correct, corrupted, and inert-placebo conditions; equivalent-content prose and typed forms; package and context hashes; selected-subset logging; blinded evaluation; reliability reporting; equal candidate and selector opportunities; and declared resource accounting. Only after those objects work reliably should the branch estimate a package effect or revisit persistent-versus-fresh execution.

# C0001 Shallow Summary

## Frontier investigated

C0001 examined how to isolate fresh role-separated execution from structured external memory, provenance rules, staged critique and verification, context partitioning, sampling, and added compute. The intended comparison is a persistent staged workflow versus fresh staged invocations with the remaining factors held constant.

## Most important evidence

No source in the sixteen-source C0001 ledger tested that exact contrast. The closest matched reasoning-token study (`C0001-SRC-01`) usually found single-agent systems best or tied on clean multi-hop reasoning, with a narrow partitioning benefit under some severe context corruption. A controlled architecture benchmark (`C0001-SRC-02`) found gains on decomposable or dynamic tasks but large losses on sequential planning. Budget-aware studies (`C0001-SRC-03`, `C0001-SRC-04`) showed that simpler sampling baselines and protocol sensitivity can explain debate gains. Verification, external memory, diversity, interface failures, and factuality metrics supplied component evidence rather than proof of role separation.

## Main state change

The branch now accepts conditional architecture fit and rejects any general independent role-separation advantage. It also accepts a compute-isolation gap and the need for coverage-sensitive, claim-level, stage-by-stage evaluation.

## Strongest unresolved objection

Fresh and persistent workflows have not been made informationally and computationally equivalent; observed differences may still come from prompts, samples, state, verifier information, handoffs, or overhead.

## Current direction

Define the compute and measurement protocols, then run a fresh-boundary isolation test with identical stages, artifacts, evidence, tools, samples, verifier inputs, and integration access.

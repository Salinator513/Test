# C0006 Dense Summary

## Research objective and frontier

The branch objective remains to determine which features of a role-separated multi-agent research loop reduce unsupported claims and cumulative reasoning errors relative to repeated single-agent prompting under fixed inference compute. C0006 did not test that architecture question directly. Its frontier was an apparatus dependency: how to represent and count work at the External Evidence Researcher → Alternative Theorist handoff so that a later direct-support measurability audit cannot hide pair inflation, non-independent observations, qualification loss, low retained coverage, or role mismatch.

The Explorer initially proposed a prospective denominator hierarchy and retained-coverage/role-relevance gate. The Critic preserved the coverage problem but rejected the implied clean hierarchy and immediate gate. The corrected frontier became a linked denominator-and-observability system now, with a numerical gate deferred until the downstream decision, claim scope, population, sampling frame, inferential unit, and error costs are frozen.

## Work performed by each role

The Explorer identified raw-pair inflation, many-to-many relations, qualification loss, clustered sparsity, factual-stratum concentration, checkpoint contamination, and the risk of a reliable but trivial residue. It proposed multiple coverage views and development-only sensitivity analyses.

The Critic found two fatal problems. First, no gate can be specified without stating what later experiment a pass would authorize and what population or stratum the resulting claim would cover. Second, the Explorer’s phrase that final content “depends on” eligible units violated the accepted boundary between observable incorporation and latent causal use. The Critic also replaced the proposed hierarchy with a linked lattice, required named numerator/denominator mappings, separated factual-stratum coverage from whole-role relevance, and required codebook and denominator co-development.

The Internal Evidence Researcher mapped branch-local constraints. It distinguished task instances, role outputs, sections, role-function units, upstream factual units, evidence units, several procedure-defined candidate sets, relation edges, bounded groups, eligible primary units, downstream units, lineage links, clusters, and exclusion records. It classified objects as ordinary-artifact, offline-annotation, or checkpoint-created and drafted allowed-ratio constraints. It reported no branch-local counts or validated schema.

The External Evidence Researcher retained twenty component-method sources. Crossed and multiple-membership modeling supports non-nested mappings. brat, MASI, gamma, CEAF, and LEA support complex spans, sets, links, groups, and boundary-sensitive agreement while showing that metrics encode different identity theories. TREC pooling supports procedure-bounded candidate universes and challenge testing, with shallow-pool bias and conditional deep-pool robustness. Reject-option and selective-classification methods expose quality–coverage tradeoffs. Pseudoreplication and mixed-effects methods require target-aligned dependence treatment. Neyman and Horvitz–Thompson require a sampling frame and known inclusion design. Walter et al. and decision-curve analysis support decision-linked sizing and operating policies. CONSORT provides exclusion-flow reporting by analogy. Turpin et al. show that generated explanations can omit experimentally induced influences.

The Alternative Theorist retained a linked-denominator/conditional-gate interpretation and developed three principal alternatives: apparatus-created measurability (`C0006-ALT-01`), construct and metric underdetermination (`C0006-ALT-02`), and a factual-stratum sparse-reuse null (`C0006-ALT-03`). It also preserved deep-curation, qualification-loss, checkpoint-contamination, and resource-privilege hybrids.

The Integrator proposed ten new claims. The Archivist accepted them with scope corrections: the proposed cross-cutting objects should not be forced into one hierarchy; TREC challenge testing is a local methodological precedent rather than a universal prerequisite; role-importance weighting remains unvalidated; selective prediction and CONSORT transfer only by analogy; and `C0006-SRC-20` supports rationale unfaithfulness, not all checkpoint or citation claims by itself.

## New evidence and source IDs

C0006 adds `C0006-SRC-01` through `C0006-SRC-20` and external evidence items `C0006-EXT-01` through `C0006-EXT-17`. The source families cover non-nested modeling, structured annotation, pooling, selective prediction, dependence, sampling, decision analysis, reporting, and explanation faithfulness. The branch ledger rises from seventy-five to ninety-five retained sources, but the count is not an independence count.

The accepted source-reported additions are: non-nested and multiple memberships can be modeled explicitly; structured objects can retain edges, groups, sets, discontinuous spans, and alternative alignments; practical candidate universes can be procedure-bounded; retained quality can improve as coverage falls; nominal observations sharing higher-level causes are not automatically independent; probability weighting requires a frame and known inclusion probabilities; reliability planning and operating decisions are consequence-dependent; exclusion flow should remain visible; and model explanations can omit causal influences. Each finding retains its transfer limitation.

## Main and alternative interpretations

The main accepted interpretation is methodological. For the currently proposed objects, B001 needs a linked denominator-and-observability lattice or multi-table system with explicit mappings and an allowed-ratio table. Every prevalence or coverage result must identify its object universe, numerator, denominator, identity rules, mapping direction, scope, and candidate-generation procedure. Edge and group views must remain parallel.

The main alternatives remain unresolved. The apparatus itself may create the measured core through candidate generation, source access, splitting, filtering, repeated coding, adjudication, and checkpoints. Plausible atomicity, grouping, boundary, weighting, metric, and cluster definitions may yield different local constructs. Direct support may be measurable inside a factual stratum but peripheral to the Alternative Theorist’s whole workload. Deep curation may create a locally adequate but expensive candidate universe.

## Claim-status changes

`C0006-INTG-01` through `C0006-INTG-10` are accepted as methodological requirements, clarifications, evidence-gap conclusions, competing-hypothesis sets, or reinforcement of non-isolation. No new empirical effect claim is accepted.

`C0006-EXP-01` remains an unsupported nontrivial-core hypothesis. `C0006-EXP-02`, `C0006-EXP-05`, and `C0006-EXP-06` remain live adverse risks without B001 magnitude estimates. `C0006-EXP-03` is accepted only as a pair-inflation and dependence risk, not a current magnitude claim. `C0006-EXP-04` is limited to prospectively defined factual strata. `C0006-EXP-07` supports the need for multidimensional reporting, while the exact gate remains unresolved. `C0006-EXP-08` is accepted in narrowed form as an observational-boundary conclusion.

## Contradictions and reasoning changes

C0006 preserves edge inflation versus group erasure, quality improvement versus ecological loss, procedure-bounded adequacy versus completeness, strict versus relaxed identity, maximal versus parsimonious clustering, probability weights versus substantive weights, ordinary versus checkpoint observability, factual-stratum feasibility versus whole-role mismatch, and transparent exclusions versus construct validity.

The key reasoning change is that “denominator hierarchy and gate” is superseded by “linked denominator map now; conditional gate later.” Denominator rules cannot be validated independently of the codebook that creates their units. A candidate-relation universe is always tied to a procedure and source-access rule. Coverage is not one scalar. A factual-stratum result cannot support a whole-role claim without separate role-relevance evidence.

## Rejected claims

C0006 does not establish prevalence, measurability, reliability, retained coverage, role relevance, a representative core, a complete candidate universe, a preferred metric, a preferred group object, an effective sample size, or a numerical threshold. A lattice does not validate its semantic mappings. Multiple edges are not automatically inflation, and grouped counts are not automatically superior. Weighting cannot repair an undefined frame. A checkpoint or rationale does not establish causal use. The twenty sources and current-agent agreement are not independent validations. Nothing in the cycle supports role separation, fresh invocation, or seven-agent superiority.

## Unresolved questions and suggested direction

The branch still lacks a one-sentence target decision, authorized development and audit corpora, target population, sampling frame, linked schema, allowed-ratio table, role-function taxonomy, candidate-generation and challenge procedures, direct-support and grouping codebooks, primary cluster model, role-relevance rubric, exclusion ledger, resource ledger, and decision-linked threshold policy.

The next direction is to freeze the later experiment and claim scope, serialize the linked object system, authorize a development corpus and untouched audit corpus, and co-develop the minimal codebook. Development work should expose sensitivity to candidate procedures, atomicity, grouping, metrics, clustering, qualification loss, role coverage, checkpoints, and resources. It must still not be treated as a held-out feasibility or effectiveness result.

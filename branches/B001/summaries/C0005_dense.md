# C0005 Dense Summary

## Research objective and frontier

C0005 examined a pre-effect apparatus question: whether the External Evidence Researcher → Alternative Theorist handoff contains enough stable, role-relevant, narrowly defined direct claim–support relations to justify a later relation-availability or representation experiment. The cycle did not test whether explicit relations improve output, whether typed fields outperform prose, whether the selected handoff is uniquely sensitive, or whether role separation or fresh invocation is beneficial.

The empirical measurability question remains unanswered. The authorized internal corpus did not include raw prior External Evidence Researcher and Alternative Theorist outputs suitable for pair counting or duplicate coding. No reference inventory, held-out audit, fixed denominator hierarchy, independently coded reliability sample, or branch-specific feasibility threshold existed.

## Work performed by each role

The Explorer proposed a measurability screen centered on candidate direct-support pairs, identity preservation, qualification, write–select–output checkpoints, and role-aligned coding. It kept sparse reuse, qualification dominance, identity instability, and a small reliable core as possibilities rather than findings.

The Critic preserved the apparatus frontier but made two decisive corrections. First, final text, citations, selected-subset records, and copied identifiers cannot identify latent causal evidence use. The endpoint was renamed **observable downstream relation-preserving incorporation**. Second, development and evaluation cannot occur on the same sample. The corpus, denominators, primary relation, codebook, exclusions, identity rules, strata, metrics, thresholds, and failure actions must be frozen before a held-out audit is opened.

The Internal Evidence Researcher confirmed that accepted branch concepts are sufficient to draft a prospective apparatus but not to validate it. Existing branch conventions cover claim IDs, epistemic status, provenance, scope, objections, contradictions, and unresolved questions, but no canonical evidence-item, selected-subset, downstream-unit, or lineage schema exists. The role could not calculate pair prevalence, exclusions, identity transitions, downstream incorporation, retained coverage, or reliability from its allowlisted files.

The External Evidence Researcher retained sixteen primary methodological sources, `C0005-SRC-01` through `C0005-SRC-16`. These cover fact-verification rationales, full-document evidence selection, output attribution, summarization content units, overlapping relations, paraphrase stress tests, span agreement, complex-annotation metrics, circular analysis, prevalence effects, and decision-specific pilot sizing.

The Alternative Theorist treated prospective small-core feasibility as the strongest current methodological interpretation while preserving coverage/role mismatch, identity/observability bottlenecks, resource and metric construction, sparse reuse, and hybrid accounts.

The Integrator correctly concluded that C0005 adds apparatus requirements rather than a handoff result. The Archivist accepted that conclusion with narrower provenance and scope corrections.

## New evidence and source IDs

`C0005-SRC-01` and `C0005-SRC-02` show that bounded fact-verification tasks can operationalize atomic claims, relation labels, and evidence rationales with useful but imperfect agreement; FEVER also reports incomplete evidence recall after audit.

`C0005-SRC-03` and `C0005-SRC-04` show that adjudicable evidence often requires full-source access, multiple sentences or paragraphs, tables, figures, or content outside abstracts. These findings weaken a universal one-claim/one-sentence object.

`C0005-SRC-05`, `C0005-SRC-09`, and `C0005-SRC-10` support observable output attribution or content correspondence while not identifying causal reliance. `C0005-SRC-07` and `C0005-SRC-08` support linked, overlapping, multidimensional relation fields. `C0005-SRC-11` rejects lexical overlap as a sufficient identity criterion.

`C0005-SRC-12` directly shows that strict and relaxed span agreement can differ substantially. `C0005-SRC-06`, `C0005-SRC-13`, and `C0005-SRC-15` show that agreement interpretation depends on prevalence, class balance, task, distance function, and annotation structure. `C0005-SRC-14` supports development/audit independence by methodological analogy. `C0005-SRC-16` supports decision-specific pilot sizing and precision by analogy, not agreement metrics or a numeric B001 cutoff.

The source ledger is not sixteen independent validations. It contains overlapping verification, summarization, biomedical, attribution, and annotation-method families.

## Main and alternative interpretations

The accepted main interpretation is that a narrow direct-support apparatus is **prospectively testable**, not established feasible. For an explicitly eligible primary reliability subset, the object may contain one atomic upstream factual claim, one content-bearing evidence unit with source identity and sufficient adjudication context, a direct-support judgment, scope and qualification attributes, and one downstream unit scored on observable incorporation dimensions. The broader schema must permit bounded multi-unit and many-to-many relations and must report what the narrow subset excludes.

The main alternatives remain live. A high-agreement core may be too small or unrepresentative of the Alternative Theorist’s qualification-rich and synthesis-heavy role. Upstream relations may be clear while downstream split, merge, scope, qualification, attribution, contradiction, synthesis, or epistemic retyping defeats stable lineage. Apparent feasibility may depend on expert access, full context, repeated judgments, adjudication, decomposition prompts, filtering, or favorable metrics. The handoff may also contain too few factual incorporation events even when the role performs appropriately.

## Claim-status and reasoning changes

`C0005-INTG-01` through `C0005-INTG-10` are accepted with Archivist qualifications. The major changes are:

- “Actual semantic use,” “reconstruction,” and “post hoc rationalization” are superseded by observable incorporation categories.
- Development/calibration and held-out audit independence are mandatory.
- Direct support remains the first primary relation, while qualification preservation is a required attribute.
- Reliability must be reported with prevalence, class-specific results, strict and relaxed boundaries, uncertainty, clustering, exclusions, retained coverage, role relevance, and metric sensitivity.
- A selected-subset checkpoint records selection only and remains a costed intervention.
- Visible archival packages cannot estimate write loss without a reference inventory.
- Any passing audit has only local measurement validity and no implication for effectiveness or role separation.

The Archivist corrected three provenance issues: PARC does not supply the same strict-versus-relaxed boundary finding as PICO; `C0005-SRC-16` supports sizing and precision rather than agreement metrics; and `C0005-SRC-14` is an indirect anti-circularity precedent rather than target-handoff validation.

## Contradictions and rejected claims

The cycle preserves tensions between narrow reliability and ecological validity, one-to-one tractability and natural many-to-many evidence, strict and relaxed boundary criteria, independent coding and resource neutrality, observable incorporation and causal use, selection observability and behavioral contamination, direct support and qualification, agreement and construct validity, decomposition and target preservation, and full-source access and scalable cost.

Rejected claims include: C0005 established measurability or sufficient pair prevalence; output correspondence or recorded selection proves causal use; the same sample can optimize and validate the apparatus; a failed direct-support audit can switch to qualification on the same held-out set; pair counts are meaningful without fixed denominators; visible packages reveal write loss; high agreement after filtering establishes ecological validity; and separate annotation roles establish fresh-role or multi-agent benefit.

## Unresolved questions and suggested next direction

The branch still needs an authorized development corpus and untouched audit corpus, unit and denominator rules, atomicity and deduplication rules, evidence-context requirements, a direct-support codebook, multidimensional incorporation fields, independent blinded coders, adjudication, a metric family, clustering and uncertainty treatment, retained-coverage and role-relevance gates, decision-specific thresholds, false-passage costs, a full resource ledger, and predeclared failure actions.

The next direction is to specify that prospective pilot completely before inspecting audit data. No relation-availability, representation, role-separation, or fresh-invocation effectiveness test should proceed until the apparatus passes its frozen audit. A failed audit must trigger a predeclared revision, handoff change, new relation-class validation on new data, or termination of the direction rather than post hoc favorable-subset selection.

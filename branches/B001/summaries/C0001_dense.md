# C0001 Dense Summary

## Research objective and frontier

The branch asks which features of a role-separated multi-agent research loop reduce unsupported claims and cumulative reasoning errors relative to repeated single-agent prompting under fixed inference compute. C0001 narrowed that objective to a causal-design problem: isolate fresh role-separated execution from structured external memory, provenance requirements, mandatory functional stages, context partitioning, role permissions, handoff compression, verification, sampling, and compute allocation.

The initial frontier proposed a ladder from repeated general prompting through structured state and staged functions to fresh role-separated execution. The cycle ultimately determined that this ladder is useful descriptively but is not a clean causal design because its decisive contrast changes several factors simultaneously.

## Work performed by each role

The Explorer identified candidate mechanisms and proposed workflow conditions and metrics. It treated role specialization, external memory, staged adversarial passes, fresh-context contamination control, interface loss, failure-mode heterogeneity, and deterministic replacement as distinct hypotheses or possibilities.

The Critic preserved the causal-isolation direction but identified two fatal gaps: the proposed workflows did not isolate role separation, and the outcomes “unsupported claim” and “cumulative reasoning error” lacked operational definitions. It required a factor matrix, one primary estimand, a declared compute rule, claim segmentation, support adjudication, coverage controls, error lineage, evaluator design, and separate budget-removed versus budget-reallocated ablations.

The Internal Evidence Researcher documented the branch’s actual treatment features as protocol facts (`C0001-INT-01` through `C0001-INT-07`): ordered fresh conversations, stage-gated progression, externalized state, epistemic typing, same-model non-independence, role-specific source permissions, and context partitioning. It confirmed that the branch had no accepted empirical record and that the original hypotheses remained unverified.

The External Evidence Researcher assembled sixteen primary sources and found no exact target-factor study. The report separated near-direct, budget-aware adjacent, mechanism, and measurement evidence. It deliberately retained null and negative findings and recorded incomplete compute accounting, bundled architecture differences, task limitations, and evaluator risks.

The Alternative Theorist proposed conditional architecture fit as the strongest interpretation and preserved alternatives centered on functional scaffolding and verifier information, budget allocation and selection, a minimal null, fresh-context contamination control, and hybrid decomposition plus centralized verification.

The Integrator synthesized those records into three principal conclusions: conditional architecture fit (`C0001-INTG-01`), an unresolved compute-isolation gap (`C0001-INTG-02`), and a methodological requirement for claim-level, coverage-sensitive, stage-level evaluation (`C0001-INTG-03`). The Archivist accepted these with scope qualifications and narrowed `C0001-EXP-06` to heterogeneity across represented task, context, and coordination regimes.

## New evidence and source IDs

The new source ledger is `C0001-SRC-01` through `C0001-SRC-16`.

Near-direct fixed-budget evidence came from `C0001-SRC-01` and `C0001-SRC-02`. The first usually found single-agent systems best or tied on clean multi-hop reasoning under requested thinking-token caps, while partitioning helped under some severe masking or substitution. The second found positive coordination effects on decomposable financial analysis and dynamic navigation but substantial degradation on sequential planning under a shared reasoning-token budget.

Budget and debate evidence (`C0001-SRC-03` through `C0001-SRC-05`, plus sampling baseline `C0001-SRC-13`) showed that self-consistency can match or beat debate and Reflexion at comparable resource levels, untuned debate is unreliable and costly, and positive debate studies commonly add agents, rounds, calls, and tokens.

Diversity and failure evidence (`C0001-SRC-06`, `C0001-SRC-07`) showed increasing redundancy among homogeneous same-model outputs and documented context loss, information withholding, repetition, misalignment, and verification or termination failures in multi-agent traces.

Refinement and verification evidence (`C0001-SRC-08` through `C0001-SRC-11`) produced a tension: staged refinement and Chain-of-Verification can improve outcomes, but intrinsic self-correction can fail, and models may correct identified errors more reliably than they locate them. This points toward verifier information and function design rather than generic critic labels.

External-memory evidence (`C0001-SRC-12`) showed navigation gains from reusable workflow records while adding information and memory-processing cost. Measurement evidence (`C0001-SRC-14` through `C0001-SRC-16`) supplied atomic fact support, citation correctness and completeness, and a coverage-sensitive proxy, but no complete stage-by-stage error lineage measure.

## Main and alternative interpretations

The main accepted interpretation is conditional architecture fit. The current evidence does not support a general independent benefit from fresh role separation. Architecture can help when tasks decompose, breadth is valuable, context is degraded, or centralized verification limits propagation. It can harm when sequential dependencies, coordination overhead, incomplete handoffs, or weak verification dominate.

The leading alternative is that functional scaffolding, structured state, external memory, and verifier information account for most useful effects. A second alternative is that sampling, selection, actual consumed compute, prompt tuning, and metric construction explain much of the apparent multi-agent gain. A viable minimal null remains: after full controls, fresh role separation may have no stable average effect, while still interacting strongly with task and context.

## Claim-status changes

`C0001-INTG-01`, `C0001-INTG-02`, and `C0001-INTG-03` entered accepted state. `C0001-EXP-06` was accepted only as task-, context-, and coordination-regime heterogeneity, not as a fully measured failure-mode result. `C0001-EXP-01` remains unresolved and weakened. `C0001-EXP-02`, `C0001-EXP-03`, `C0001-EXP-04`, `C0001-EXP-05`, and `C0001-EXP-07` remain qualified hypotheses or risks within narrow scopes.

## Contradictions and reasoning changes

Positive debate findings conflict with budget-aware neutral or negative comparisons. Self-refinement gains conflict with weak intrinsic self-correction, partly reconciled by verifier-information differences. Context partitioning can protect against degraded context but can also create interface loss. Diversity can improve complementarity but can also be stylistic or wrong. Provenance traceability can improve without support correctness or factual coverage.

The main reasoning change is from asking whether “multi-agent” systems work to identifying manipulable factors and interactions. The cycle also changed the evaluation target from raw unsupported-claim counts to a combination of atomic support, citation correctness and completeness, coverage, task completion, and error lineage.

## Rejected claims

The cycle rejected general superiority of multi-agent or seven-role execution under fixed compute; same-model agreement as independent corroboration; the Explorer’s original ladder as a causal isolation design; added-compute component studies as direct proof of role separation; citation presence or raw claim counts as sufficient evaluation; role-deletion loss as unique value without a freed-compute rule; and broad deterministic replacement of whole roles.

## Unresolved questions

The branch still lacks an accepted definition of role separation, a primary compute-matching rule and full ledger, an informational-equivalence method for persistent and fresh conditions, a coding and adjudication protocol, an evaluator-reliability design, and direct evidence on open-ended multi-source research synthesis.

## Suggested next direction

The next priority is a fresh-boundary isolation design. Run identical stages, messages, artifacts, evidence, tools, verifier inputs, candidate counts, selectors, and integration rules in one persistent context and across fresh invocations. Charge prompt and context processing, completions, state creation, handoffs, tools, sampling, selection, and final-answer generation. Pair this with a coverage-sensitive error-lineage protocol, then test sampling-equated and verifier-information contrasts and later run both budget-removed and budget-reallocated role ablations.

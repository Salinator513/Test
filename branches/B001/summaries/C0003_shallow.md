# C0003 Shallow Summary

## Frontier investigated

C0003 operationalized the stage-package frontier from C0002. It asked how to test one explicit claim–support or claim–qualification relation without conflating added information, representation, geometry, reader behavior, validation privilege, or compute.

## Most important evidence

The fourteen-source external ledger found no direct study of the corrected metadata-availability or equivalent-content representation contrast under full manipulation, coverage, geometry, reader, and end-to-end resource controls in open-ended multi-source synthesis. Nearby evidence shows that metadata and serialization can change model behavior, claim–evidence relations can be annotated, and explicit retrieval can be separated from downstream use. It does not establish a typed-package benefit.

## Main state change

The branch now separates two estimands: relation absent versus correctly supplied measures metadata availability; controlled prose versus a typed field carrying approximately equivalent relation content measures representation. Package experiments must record the reference inventory, written package, explicit selected subset where retrieval is claimed, and final claims with item-level lineage. Exact replay and reference packages remain diagnostic controls, not causal proof.

## Strongest unresolved objection

A positive result could reflect geometry, authority cues, evaluator alignment, privileged validation, prompts, sampling, selection, or added resources rather than relation-specific reasoning. A null could reflect reader non-use or treatment failure.

## Current direction

Validate one minimal pilot apparatus at one stage transition and one relation type before estimating package effects or returning to role-separation or fresh-context comparisons.

# C0004 — Dense Summary

## Research objective and frontier

The branch objective remains to determine which features of a role-separated research loop reduce unsupported claims and cumulative reasoning error under fixed inference compute. C0004 did not test role separation or workflow superiority. It narrowed the frontier to whether the branch can validate a minimal apparatus for studying one class of direct claim–support relation at one pragmatic handoff: External Evidence Researcher → Alternative Theorist. The corrected design separates relation availability from relation representation, records writing, forced selection, and final use as distinct checkpoints, and evaluates factual assertions separately from hypotheses, objections, speculation, and unresolved questions.

## Work performed by each role

The Explorer proposed the handoff and relation type, identified relation-information, representation, geometry, authority, selection, coverage–noise, prompt, and stage-local possibilities, and framed the first work as apparatus validation.

The Critic retained the frontier but substantially narrowed it. It required a direct-support codebook, separate availability and representation estimands, role-aligned outcomes, a common costed selected-subset protocol, and a minimum viable pilot. It deferred broad corrupted-link, cue, prompt, density, and geometry factorials. It also warned that the selected-subset report is itself a behavioral intervention and that the chosen handoff is pragmatic rather than uniquely diagnostic.

The Internal Evidence Researcher showed that prior accepted state already supplies claim IDs, source IDs, epistemic categories, support lists, coverage-sensitive lineage requirements, and write–select–use distinctions. It also found that the branch lacks a canonical evidence-item object, direct-support codebook, selected-subset schema, final-use definition, lineage codebook, evaluator protocol, pilot-specific resource rule, and accepted evidence that the selected handoff contains enough stable direct-support pairs.

The External Evidence Researcher retained fifteen primary sources, `C0004-SRC-01` through `C0004-SRC-15`. No source tested the corrected relation-availability or equivalent-content representation contrast in the target handoff under matched full resources. Evidence ordering and selection studies (`SRC-03`, `SRC-04`, `SRC-06`), evidence-binding and long-form workflow studies (`SRC-07`, `SRC-08`), relational and qualification-sensitive apparatus studies (`SRC-09` through `SRC-15`), and interface or cue studies (`SRC-01`, `SRC-02`, `SRC-12`) supplied indirect apparatus, mechanism, and risk evidence.

The Alternative Theorist retained a conditional relation-information hypothesis but developed four live alternatives: geometry and search burden (`C0004-ALT-01`), authority-cue compliance (`C0004-ALT-02`), apparatus-induced deliberation and selection (`C0004-ALT-03`), and a reader-bottleneck or minimal null (`C0004-ALT-04`). It emphasized hybrid and task-by-model explanations.

The Integrator proposed ten claims, `C0004-INTG-01` through `C0004-INTG-10`, a fifteen-source factual-record addition, revised hypotheses, explicit rejections, and a next frontier centered on measurability and reliability.

## New evidence and source IDs

The most decision-relevant evidence is mixed. `C0004-SRC-01` found that granular claim–evidence provenance reduced reported trust but did not significantly change behavioral reliance or confidence. The provisional preprint `C0004-SRC-02` reported increased hallucination from citation cues, including fabricated citations. `C0004-SRC-03` found better verification efficiency from ranked evidence, while `C0004-SRC-04` found that human-curated selection improved attribution but did not eliminate final attribution errors. `C0004-SRC-05` found that evidence quality was less predictive for complex outputs. `C0004-SRC-06` showed that selected evidence can change model and human decisions, establishing selection as behaviorally consequential. `C0004-SRC-07` and `C0004-SRC-08` reported benefits from bundled evidence-binding or evidence-tracked workflows. `C0004-SRC-09` exposed evaluator susceptibility to superficial form. `C0004-SRC-10`, `SRC-11`, `SRC-13`, and `SRC-15` support codebook, identity, relation, and qualification-sensitive measurement feasibility. `C0004-SRC-14` supplied a partial retained-token comparison for semantic selection. These sources are not independent replications of a claim–support effect.

## Main and alternative interpretations

The main interpretation remains a conditional hypothesis: correct direct-support information may reduce reconstruction burden where evidence is dispersed, ambiguous, or qualification-rich. No benefit magnitude or direction is accepted. Geometry, authority cues, forced deliberation, writer or validator privilege, reader capability, prompt strength, sampling, selector quality, evaluator alignment, coverage changes, and resource reallocation can mimic or reverse an apparent benefit.

## Claim-status changes and reasoning changes

The branch accepted the corrected evidence gap, apparatus-only scope, role-aligned outcome requirement, narrow direct-support codebook requirement, selection–use dissociation, persistent compute non-isolation, and a minimum viable pilot sequence. The Integrator’s wording that forced selection “is an intervention” was qualified: the cited evidence shows that evidence selection and presentation can change behavior, so a required pre-generation selected-subset protocol must be treated as potentially behavior-changing and costed; its specific B001 effect remains unestimated. The wording that apparatus-induced deliberation is a supported explanation was also kept as an evidence-backed alternative, not accepted as the cause of any unrun B001 result.

Authority-cue risk was strengthened from an unresolved current-cycle hypothesis to a qualified evidence-backed risk in adjacent settings, with direct transfer unestablished. Stage-local sensitivity was not accepted; the handoff remains only a pragmatic candidate subject to a measurability screen.

## Contradictions, rejected claims, and unresolved questions

The cycle preserved tensions between structured-evidence benefits and absent behavioral uptake, evidence binding and authority harm, selection value and selection insufficiency, reliable narrow coding and ecological validity, and observability and behavioral contamination. It rejected claims that C0004 establishes a claim–support benefit, typed-field superiority, stage sensitivity, passive retrieval measurement, correct semantic use from citation or selection, full fixed-compute control, source-count independence, or any fresh-role or full-loop advantage.

The central unresolved questions concern whether the handoff contains stable direct-support pairs, how direct support and final semantic use are defined, how identity changes and multi-cause lineage are coded, how the selected-subset protocol is timed and costed, which evaluation and reliability thresholds apply, and whether prose and typed relations can be approximately equivalent.

## Suggested next direction

Run a branch-local measurability and reliability screen before effectiveness testing. Define the minimal schema and direct-support codebook, verify stable identities and role-aligned scoring, implement the same forced-selection protocol across conditions, predeclare coverage and usefulness gates, blind evaluation, and specify a complete resource ledger. Test relation availability first. Compare controlled prose with typed representation only after manipulation, equivalence, and reliability checks pass.

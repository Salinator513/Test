# C0004 — Shallow Summary

## Frontier investigated

C0004 examined whether a minimal apparatus can test explicit direct claim–support relations at the External Evidence Researcher → Alternative Theorist handoff. The cycle separated relation availability from prose-versus-typed representation and treated the selected-subset checkpoint as a forced, costed intervention.

## Most important evidence

The fifteen-source ledger found no direct matched-resource study of the corrected contrast or the target handoff. Adjacent studies show that evidence ordering, curation, selection, relational organization, and qualification preservation can affect verification, attribution, comprehension, coverage, or accuracy. Adverse evidence shows that richer provenance can alter trust without behavioral uptake, citation-like cues can increase belief or hallucination, and correct selection does not guarantee correct final use.

## Main state change

The branch accepted apparatus validation—not an effectiveness result—as the immediate direction. It now requires a narrow direct-support codebook, stable claim and evidence identities, role-aligned epistemic scoring, observable write–select–use lineage, blinded evaluation, coverage and usefulness gates, and full resource accounting.

## Strongest unresolved objection

The apparatus may manufacture the apparent benefit through forced deliberation, geometry, authority cues, curation, validation, evaluator alignment, or added compute. The selected handoff may also lack enough stable direct-support pairs for reliable measurement.

## Current direction

First run a branch-local measurability and reliability screen. Only after successful manipulation checks should the branch test relation availability, then approximately equivalent prose versus typed representation.

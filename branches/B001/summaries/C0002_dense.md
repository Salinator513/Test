# C0002 Dense Summary

## Research objective and frontier

C0002 addressed the unresolved design problem left by C0001: whether persistent staged execution and fresh staged invocations can be compared while holding transferred information and inference resources sufficiently constant. The Explorer initially framed this as a fresh-boundary isolation contrast moderated by “state completeness.” The cycle corrected that framing. The accepted frontier is now the observable decomposition of prior-trace access, state representation, retention, dependency and relation preservation, package transformation, information geometry, retrieval, and compute allocation. No independent fresh-container effect was assumed.

## Work performed by each role

The Explorer proposed hypotheses covering a boundary null, contamination reduction, interface loss, a state-completeness threshold, structured-state dominance, hidden-context privilege, compression, and stage-specific sensitivity. It also proposed a 2 × 2 boundary-by-state comparison, restricted-persistent conditions, dependency probes, and coverage-sensitive evaluation.

The Critic identified two fatal problems. First, a full-trace persistent versus fresh contrast changes information access, context length, salience, retrieval opportunity, and cost, while package-only persistent versus package-only fresh may collapse to functionally identical treatments unless a residual property is defined. Second, scalar “state completeness” is circular unless separated into predeclared, outcome-independent dimensions. The Critic required a three-condition access decomposition and separate measures for schema compliance, retention, dependency coverage, package size, and transformation.

The Internal Evidence Researcher audited the branch architecture. It confirmed that B001 bundles fresh conversations with roles, permissions, external state, and progression rules; separates control state, accepted state, and raw cycle outputs; lacks a canonical standardized stage package; and has no accepted residual boundary treatment, primary compute rule, or complete lineage codebook. It produced a factor matrix and preserved the C0001 measurement and compute gates.

The External Evidence Researcher assembled fourteen primary research sources and separated near-direct access evidence, indirect state evidence, measurement methods, and bundled non-discriminating interventions. No source isolated a noncollapsed package-only persistent-versus-package-only fresh effect under the required controls.

The Alternative Theorist proposed an information-access and relay-fidelity tradeoff as the strongest interpretation, with competing explanations based on context salience, resource allocation, state-write bottlenecks, and a residual-boundary null. It emphasized hybrid interactions rather than a single universal direction.

The Integrator synthesized these findings into five proposed accepted claims, rejected the scalar threshold and original causal 2 × 2 interpretation, and proposed a full state update. The Archivist accepted the main synthesis while narrowing two statements to prevent overclaiming.

## New evidence and source IDs

`C0002-SRC-01` is the closest located study to the corrected frontier. It formalizes full accumulated context versus isolated local contexts connected by relays and reports that relay sufficiency determines whether isolation helps or harms. It remains a preprint, bundles local isolation with compression, and lacks full compute accounting.

`C0002-SRC-02` and `C0002-SRC-03` report multi-turn unreliability and task-switch interference from prior conversational history. `C0002-SRC-08` reports position-sensitive use of the same relevant information in long contexts. These support prior-trace interference and salience mechanisms, not intrinsic freshness.

`C0002-SRC-04`, `C0002-SRC-05`, and `C0002-SRC-14` report benefits from selective condensation, predictive write-time memory, or structured temporal memory, but add prompts, calls, retrieval, tools, or other resources. `C0002-SRC-05` specifically separates write-side loss from retrieval-side loss.

`C0002-SRC-06` and `C0002-SRC-07` provide long-term memory benchmarks and predeclared temporal or causal dependency structures. `C0002-SRC-09` separates coverage and citation using a known insight inventory. `C0002-SRC-10` through `C0002-SRC-13` contribute factual-error categories, content-retention measures, correction scoring, and evaluator-validity cautions. None supplies complete B001-style cross-stage claim lineage.

## Main and alternative interpretations

The accepted main interpretation is a qualified information-access and relay-fidelity tradeoff. Filtering prior trace may reduce interference, premature commitment, redundancy, or poorly positioned information, but may also delete valid qualifications, support links, objections, temporal or causal relations, and unresolved dependencies. The direction is conditional on retention, relation preservation, salience, task structure, model capability, prompts, tools, sampling, selection, and budget use.

Alternative explanations remain active. Context-salience and retrieval geometry can change use even when the same item inventory is retained. Resource allocation, added calls, specialized prompts, tools, candidates, selectors, and saved-budget reallocation can create apparent gains. State-write bottlenecks can discard or retype information before retrieval. A residual-boundary null remains plausible but untestable until a nontrivial residual manipulation is defined.

## Claim-status changes

`C0002-INTG-01` through `C0002-INTG-05` enter accepted state, with qualifications. `C0002-INTG-03` is worded conditionally: package filtering may reduce interference and may create interface loss; no matched B001-style effect is established. `C0002-INTG-05` applies evaluator-reliability requirements specifically to judgment-based unsupported-claim, coverage, mutation, and lineage outcomes.

`C0002-EXP-01` is unresolved and reframed as a residual-boundary null. `C0002-EXP-02` is narrowed to conditional prior-trace interference. `C0002-EXP-03` becomes a qualified supported interface-loss risk. `C0002-EXP-04` is rejected as framed. `C0002-EXP-05` remains a qualified structured-state hypothesis. `C0002-EXP-06` becomes a design factor. `C0002-EXP-07` is retained as an active compression or transformation factor with unresolved direction. `C0002-EXP-08` remains deferred speculation.

## Contradictions

Prior trace can interfere while also preserving valid information. Compression can improve efficiency and salience while causing write loss and interface loss. Structured state can produce strong benchmark results while bundling components and added inference. Fewer unsupported claims can reflect omission rather than better support. Fixed steps or stored-state tokens do not equal fixed end-to-end compute. Package-matched equality can mean a true null, treatment collapse, weak manipulation, task insensitivity, opposing subgroup effects, or unreliable measurement.

## Reasoning changes

The cycle moved from a nominal boundary comparison to observable factors. It replaced scalar completeness with independently measured state dimensions, added manipulation checks to outcome evaluation, separated write loss from retrieval loss, and required exact-package replay to distinguish package-construction effects from downstream reader effects. Same-model agreement and benchmark reuse are explicitly excluded as independent evidential weight.

## Rejected claims

The branch rejects an intrinsic or general fresh-container benefit, the scalar state-completeness threshold, the original 2 × 2 boundary-by-state design as a causal factorial, package-only equality as proof of a boundary null without a residual manipulation, full-trace differences as evidence for freshness, structured-memory gains as direct evidence for role separation or equal-compute superiority, schema completion or semantic similarity as sufficient retention evidence, and unsupported-claim reductions without coverage and task controls.

## Unresolved questions

The branch still lacks a canonical typed stage package, predeclared dependency inventory, transformation log, residual boundary property, primary compute measure, saved-budget rule, complete lineage codebook, evaluator threshold, and minimum coverage requirement. Transfer to open-ended multi-source research synthesis remains unestablished.

## Suggested next direction

Preregister a small branch-local design with full-trace persistent, package-only persistent, and package-only fresh conditions. Define a typed package and dependency inventory before outcomes are scored; archive and replay exact packages; log transformations and trace access; measure item, relation, status, and coverage retention; validate judgment-based lineage scoring; and declare the primary compute and saved-budget rules before causal interpretation.

## Final Accepted Cycle Delta

C0003 is accepted as a methodological narrowing cycle with a negative direct-evidence result.

The branch now distinguishes two package estimands: **metadata availability**, where one correct predeclared claim–support or qualification relation is absent versus supplied; and **metadata representation**, where approximately equivalent relation content is expressed in controlled prose versus a typed field or edge. The first changes substantive information availability; the second remains confounded unless geometry, wording, position, adjacency, density, prompts, reader conditions, and resources are checked.

Future write–retrieve–use claims must use observable objects: a predeclared reference inventory and relations, the written package, an explicitly selected or retrieved subset wherever retrieval is claimed, and final claims with item-level lineage. Presence in context is not retrieval. Exact replay removes package-construction variation only. A reference package is a separately costed diagnostic upper bound.

The C0003 ledger supports that metadata and representation can change behavior, that claim–evidence relations and retrieve–use checkpoints can be measured, and that long-form writing exhibits relevant incorporation and relation errors. It does not establish a typed-package benefit, a write-dominant bottleneck, a full lineage ontology, a universal schema, fixed-compute superiority, role separation, or a fresh-invocation effect.

The immediate accepted direction is apparatus validation around one stage transition and one relation type, with relation-absent, correct, corrupted, and inert-placebo conditions; equivalent-content prose and typed representations; explicit selected-subset logging; item-level lineage; coverage and task-completion gates; blinded evaluation and reliability reporting; equal candidate and selector opportunities; and a declared end-to-end budget and saved-budget rule.

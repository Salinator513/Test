## Relevant Hypotheses and Speculation

- **`C0003-EXP-01` / relation-information hypothesis:** Correct explicit claim–support or qualification relations may reduce reconstruction error when relations are dispersed or dependency-dense. Status: unresolved hypothesis, not an accepted effect.
- **Reader-bottleneck null:** Correct metadata may have little effect if the receiving role does not select or apply it. Status: unresolved hypothesis.
- **Authority-cue hypothesis:** Structured links may change trust or compliance through perceived validation rather than correct evidence reasoning. Status: unresolved hypothesis.
- **Coverage–noise interaction:** Greater correct-link coverage may improve recall while false or irrelevant links increase noise or reverse direction. Status: unresolved hypothesis.
- **Prompt-substitution hypothesis:** Relation-seeking instructions may reconstruct some support relations without explicit typed links. Status: unresolved hypothesis.
- **Writer–validator privilege hypothesis:** Apparent package gains may come from correction, adjudication, validation labor, or privileged information. Status: unresolved hypothesis.
- **State-write bottleneck hypothesis / `C0003-EXP-03`:** Errors may arise during writing, selection, or downstream use, with no accepted evidence that one checkpoint dominates.
- **`C0004-EXP-01` through `C0004-EXP-08`:** These are current-cycle Explorer proposals and remain raw, unaccepted hypotheses or speculation. The Critic preserves the frontier but narrows it to apparatus validation and rejects treating the full mechanism list as one initial pilot.

## Prior Contradictions or Objections

- **Traceability versus support:** Claim IDs and citations can improve traceability without establishing correct or complete support.
- **Relation information versus representation:** Adding a link changes information; typed versus prose presentation changes format and geometry. These cannot be pooled.
- **Package presence versus selection and use:** A relation may be present but not selected, selected but misused, or correctly reconstructed despite being absent.
- **Alternative-role behavior versus unsupported-claim scoring:** The Alternative Theorist is expected to generate hypotheses, objections, and unresolved possibilities. A raw unsupported-claim rate would punish legitimate role behavior unless factual assertions and source-attribution claims are scored separately.
- **Coverage versus apparent factuality:** Fewer unsupported claims can result from fewer claims, fewer alternatives, lower specificity, omission, or task failure.
- **Useful relation versus authority cue:** Correct links may guide valid evidence use or merely induce compliance with a validation-looking marker.
- **Reference quality versus schema effect:** A validated reference package can serve as a separately costed diagnostic upper bound while bundling correction and privilege.
- **Exact replay versus effective access:** Byte-identical packages control writing variance but do not prove equal attention, selection, interpretation, or use.
- **Write loss versus selection and use loss:** One final error can have multiple contributing checkpoints; dominant-cause claims are unsupported.
- **Structured evaluation versus evaluator alignment:** A structured output may be easier for an evaluator to score and can be favored because its format matches the treatment.

## Changes Across Recent Cycles

- **C0001:** The branch moved away from general “multi-agent versus single-agent” superiority claims and required one causal estimand, fixed-resource accounting, atomic support evaluation, coverage controls, and stage-level lineage.
- **C0002:** The branch replaced the fresh-versus-persistent label with observable factors: prior-trace access, representation, retention, transformation, dependency preservation, geometry, write loss, retrieval loss, prompts, tools, selection, and resources. Scalar completeness was rejected.
- **C0003:** The frontier narrowed to package apparatus. Relation availability was separated from representation; exact replay was limited to package-construction control; the selected subset became a required observable checkpoint; and a reference package became a separately costed diagnostic rather than proof of schema value.
- **C0004 Explorer:** The branch selected claim–support at the External Evidence Researcher → Alternative Theorist handoff and proposed availability, representation, geometry, cues, selection, coverage, prompt, lineage, and resource tests.
- **C0004 Critic:** The current direction was narrowed again. The immediate goal is apparatus validation only; direct support must be operationally restricted; availability and representation remain separate estimands; the forced selected-subset object is a costed common intervention; factual support outcomes must be separated from legitimate hypotheses and objections; and broad cue, geometry, prompt, density, and corruption factorials should be deferred.

## Internal Evidence Gaps

1. **No canonical evidence-item object.** The accepted state has claim and source IDs plus support lists, but no required evidence-item ID, exact excerpt, locator, or adjudicated direct-support edge.
2. **No narrow direct-support codebook.** The branch does not define whether support includes partial support, contextualization, example, qualification, contradiction, or motivation. The first pilot cannot silently treat all relevance as support.
3. **No branch-local transition sample in the allowed record.** The authorized accepted state and dense summaries do not expose concrete External Evidence Researcher claims paired with later Alternative Theorist factual reuse. Therefore the allowed record does not establish that this handoff contains enough stable direct-support pairs for reliable measurement.
4. **No concrete identity-edge examples.** The allowed record names paraphrase, split, merge, scope change, qualification weakening, support substitution, contradiction, and synthesis as unresolved cases, but does not provide accepted item-level examples or adjudications.
5. **No selected-subset schema.** Timing, cardinality, identifiers, schema validation, rejection versus omission, capacity limits, and connection to final use are undefined.
6. **No accepted definition of final use.** Citation, support attribution, semantic dependence, paraphrase, and mere selection are not yet distinguished by an accepted codebook.
7. **No accepted multi-cause lineage rule.** The branch requires write–select–use separation but has no accepted rule for assigning one error across multiple contributing checkpoints.
8. **No accepted evaluator protocol.** Blinding, evaluator mix, distance function, adjudication, and reliability thresholds remain unset.
9. **No pilot-specific resource rule.** The branch requires full accounting but has no accepted primary unit, tolerance, saved-budget rule, or reallocation rule for this comparison.
10. **No internal rationale that claim–support is superior to claim–qualification for the first pilot.** Claim–support is a pragmatic fit to the branch objective, not an internally demonstrated best relation type.
11. **No accepted evidence of intervention benefit.** The branch contains no internal causal evidence that explicit claim–support links improve selection, factual support attribution, qualification preservation, alternative quality, or downstream usefulness.

A minimal **candidate**, not validated, pilot record could reuse existing ID and status conventions and add: `claim_id`, atomic `claim_text`, epistemic status, scope or qualification, `evidence_item_id`, `source_id`, exact excerpt or locator, narrowly coded `relation_type`, adjudication record, package condition and representation, selected-subset status, final-use link, lineage event, and per-condition resource ledger. Every added field remains apparatus to validate rather than accepted evidence.

## Questions Requiring External Evidence

1. Is there a direct controlled study presenting the same claims and evidence with versus without explicit claim–evidence relations, with enough reporting to assess content, geometry, prompts, readers, and resources?
2. Is there a study comparing approximately equivalent relation content in controlled prose and typed or structured form, with semantic-equivalence and residual-format checks?
3. What validated methods define a narrow direct-support relation and distinguish it from partial support, qualification, contradiction, contextualization, exemplification, or motivation?
4. What annotation methods achieve acceptable reliability for claim identity across paraphrase, split, merge, scope change, qualification weakening, and derived synthesis?
5. What paradigms record selected evidence before generation, and what evidence shows that the checkpoint itself changes behavior?
6. Are there surface- or plausibility-matched correct-versus-corrupted relation studies that distinguish evidence verification from cue compliance?
7. What methods separate selected evidence from correct final support attribution, unsupported extension, qualification preservation, and final semantic use?
8. Are there fully or substantially resource-accounted designs exposing relation construction, validation, parsing, forced selection, candidate generation, selection, and final-generation cost?
9. Is there evidence supporting transfer from claim verification, structured input, retrieval, or long-context tasks to open-ended multi-source synthesis and specifically to evidence-collection → alternative-explanation handoffs?
10. What adverse findings exist: reliable nulls or harms after successful manipulation, prose matching typed form, reduced coverage or usefulness, correct selection without better final support, or corrupted links followed similarly to correct links?

## Handoff to External Evidence Researcher

Begin from the accepted `C0003-INTG-02` gap rather than conducting another broad search for whether metadata, structured inputs, retrieval, or claim verification matter. The internal record supports apparatus construction and strict interpretation gates, but it contains no accepted effect estimate for explicit claim–support relations and no proof that the selected handoff is a reliable measurement site.

Prioritize narrowly qualifying methodological evidence for: same-content link availability contrasts; equivalent-content prose-versus-typed comparisons; direct-support codebooks and reliability; pre-generation selected-evidence checkpoints and their behavioral effects; final-use and identity lineage; correct-versus-corrupted cue controls; and full resource accounting. For each source, state whether it discriminates relation information, representation, geometry, authority cues, forced selection, and downstream use, or merely supplies adjacent apparatus guidance.

Stop or downgrade a source when underlying evidence content changes with the links, there is no relation-absent or equivalent-content comparison, selection is inferred only from final output, evaluator format is aligned with treatment without counterchecks, added validation or compute is bundled, or the task does not separate factual assertions from hypotheses or opinions.

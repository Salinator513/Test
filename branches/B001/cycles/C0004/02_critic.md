# Critic — C0004

## Fair Reconstruction of the Explorer’s Direction

The Explorer proposes a deliberately local apparatus-validation frontier rather than another broad test of fresh roles or the full seven-role loop. The proposed unit is one relation type, **claim–support**, at one handoff, **External Evidence Researcher → Alternative Theorist**. The intended causal decomposition is:

1. **Relation availability:** claims and evidence are present either without an explicit link or with a correct explicit claim–support link.
2. **Relation representation:** approximately equivalent relation content is expressed in controlled prose or a typed field or edge.
3. **Geometry and cue controls:** adjacency, order, token distance, delimiters, salience, framing, and corrupted or inert markers are considered as competing explanations.
4. **Checkpoint observability:** the reference inventory, written package, receiving-role selection, and final use are recorded separately.
5. **Evaluation and cost controls:** unsupported factual claims are not interpreted without coverage, usefulness, citation, omission, task-completion, and end-to-end resource accounting.

The Explorer’s preferred interpretation is conditional rather than absolute: correct explicit relations may reduce support-reconstruction error, but any observed benefit could instead come from geometry, cue compliance, reader prompts, selection, or added resources. The initial exercise is explicitly framed as manipulation and measurement validation, not proof that typed packages, claim–support metadata, fresh conversations, or role separation improve performance.

That reconstruction is fair and materially consistent with the accepted branch frontier.

## What Is Strong or Worth Preserving

The direction is already adequate in several important respects.

- It respects the accepted split between **availability** and **representation** rather than calling relation-present versus relation-absent a pure formatting test.
- It selects one relation type and one handoff, avoiding immediate full-schema optimization.
- It preserves the accepted write–select–use decomposition and does not equate package presence with retrieval or use.
- It names authority cues, geometry, prompt substitution, coverage, noise, reader capability, and resources as live competing explanations.
- It explicitly treats the first pilot as apparatus validation.
- It includes corrupted-link and inert-control ideas that can provide evidence against a benign “relations help reasoning” interpretation.
- It acknowledges that the selected transition may not generalize and does not claim stage-specific sensitivity from a single transition.
- It recognizes that lower unsupported-claim counts can be misleading without coverage, usefulness, task completion, citation, and omission gates.

These features should be retained. The direction should be narrowed and operationalized, not discarded.

## Fatal Problems

None identified at the frontier level.

The proposed frontier is answerable in principle because it is framed as validation of a manipulation and measurement apparatus. A later effectiveness claim would be blocked, however, unless the material problems below are corrected first.

## Material Problems

### 1. The selected receiving role and the proposed outcome are not yet aligned

The Alternative Theorist is expected to produce competing explanations, hypotheses, objections, and unresolved possibilities. Those outputs can be epistemically appropriate without having direct source support in the same sense as factual claims. A raw unsupported-claim rate could therefore punish correct role behavior or reward an Alternative Theorist that produces fewer alternatives.

The pilot must distinguish at least:

- sourced or source-interpreting factual assertions;
- derived conclusions;
- hypotheses, speculation, objections, and unresolved questions;
- claims about what a source establishes versus claims merely motivated by a source.

Only assertive factual or source-attribution errors should enter the primary unsupported-claim measure. Alternative generation should instead be measured for correct epistemic typing, non-conversion of speculation into fact, preservation of relevant support and qualifications, and useful coverage of competing explanations.

### 2. “Claim–support” is still too ambiguous for a supposedly single-relation pilot

The Explorer treats support as if it were a binary link, but the proposed package may contain evidence that directly supports, partially supports, contextualizes, exemplifies, qualifies, contradicts, or merely motivates a claim. If these are collapsed into one “support” relation, annotation disagreements and apparent downstream errors may reflect an underspecified ontology rather than reader failure.

The first pilot should either:

- restrict eligible pairs to a predeclared narrow class of direct support with clear inclusion and exclusion rules; or
- declare a small relation codebook while keeping only one primary relation estimand.

It should not silently treat all evidential relevance as support.

### 3. The design space is too large for the stated “smallest useful” pilot

The Explorer proposes availability, prose-versus-typed representation, multiple geometry variables, five cue conditions, selected-subset logging, lineage, coverage levels, false-link rates, irrelevant-link rates, prompt interaction, reliability coding, and full resource accounting. Taken together, this is not one narrow pilot. It is a family of experiments with many interactions and a serious risk of sparse, underpowered, or selectively interpreted results.

The first apparatus pilot should validate only the minimum chain:

1. a fixed reference inventory;
2. a fixed package with a relation-absent and correct-relation contrast;
3. one controlled representation comparison, only after semantic equivalence is checked;
4. one selected-subset checkpoint held constant across conditions;
5. final lineage and epistemic-status scoring;
6. manipulation, annotation-reliability, coverage, and cost checks.

Corrupted links, cue framing, prompt substitution, relation density, and broad geometry factorials should be staged as follow-on tests unless needed to diagnose a failed manipulation.

### 4. The selected-subset object is both a measurement device and a behavioral intervention

Requiring the Alternative Theorist to emit a machine-readable selection before generation may improve deliberation, force explicit evidence review, consume resources, or alter which alternatives are produced. It therefore cannot be treated as a neutral observation of natural selection.

For the first pilot, the selected-subset checkpoint should be:

- identical across all compared conditions;
- explicitly classified as part of the apparatus;
- costed;
- interpreted as observed selection under a forced-selection protocol, not as natural latent retrieval;
- excluded from claims that the relation alone caused downstream improvement.

A later no-checkpoint comparison would be required to estimate the checkpoint’s own behavioral effect.

### 5. Availability and representation cannot be estimated cleanly in one undifferentiated comparison

The availability contrast adds relation information. The representation contrast changes surface form, geometry, and possibly token cost even when semantic content is approximately equivalent. These are separate estimands and should not be pooled into a single “explicit relation” effect.

The research direction should predeclare two independent questions:

- Does supplying a correct relation, compared with no supplied relation, change selection or use?
- Conditional on supplying approximately equivalent relation content, does typed versus controlled-prose representation change selection or use?

A positive answer to the first does not imply a typed-field benefit. A positive answer to the second does not establish that relation information is beneficial compared with absence.

### 6. The stage transition is pragmatically selected, not justified as uniquely diagnostic

The Explorer fairly labels stage-local sensitivity as speculation, but the current wording still risks presenting the handoff as especially suitable because the Alternative Theorist “transforms evidence into competing explanations.” That rationale is plausible but does not establish that claim–support links are the limiting information at this handoff.

The pilot may use this transition pragmatically, but it must not infer stage sensitivity or representativeness. Internal research should verify that the handoff actually contains enough stable claim–evidence pairs and downstream factual reuse to make the apparatus measurable. If it does not, the transition is a poor measurement site even if conceptually interesting.

### 7. The proposed primary outcome remains underspecified

“Support reconstruction error,” “relation use,” and “unsupported claims” are not interchangeable. A reader can select the correct evidence yet draw an unsupported extension; ignore a supplied link yet independently reconstruct the correct support; use a correct link but weaken its qualification; or produce fewer unsupported claims by omitting difficult material.

The pilot needs separate outcomes for:

- relation-selection recall and precision;
- correct final support attribution;
- unsupported factual extensions;
- qualification and scope preservation;
- factual and alternative-explanation coverage;
- omission severity;
- task completion and usefulness;
- correct epistemic typing.

No single composite should be treated as the primary outcome unless its weighting is predeclared and justified.

### 8. The research questions risk repeating accepted branch work

Several external questions restate gaps already recorded in C0003: direct relation-link contrasts, prose-versus-typed representation, selected-evidence logging, annotation schemes, and full resource controls. Re-running broad searches on these questions could add volume without adding discrimination.

The External Researcher should use the accepted C0003 gap as the starting point and search only for narrowly qualifying evidence or methodological precedents that materially change the apparatus. The Internal Researcher should map existing conventions and unresolved definitions rather than re-summarize the accepted state.

## Minor Problems

- “As constant as possible” is too weak for a causal contrast. The direction should list which variables are fixed, which are approximately matched, which are measured covariates, and which remain uncontrolled.
- “Validated framing” is ambiguous. It could mean the link was human-checked, model-checked, procedurally certified, or merely labeled as validated. Those are different interventions.
- “Inert placebo” is undefined. A marker is not inert if it changes salience, token distance, reader expectations, or package parsing.
- “False” and “irrelevant” links should not be grouped. A plausible but wrong support link, an unrelated link, and a contradictory link create different failure opportunities.
- “Several evidence items could plausibly support the same claim” introduces adjudication ambiguity. Ambiguous many-to-many cases should be excluded from the first reliability pilot or explicitly coded.
- “Final use” needs an observable definition. Citation, paraphrase, semantic dependence, or merely selecting an item are not equivalent.
- The resource ledger should include annotation and validation effort when those differ by condition, not only generation-time costs.
- A machine-readable selected subset may create formatting failures unrelated to epistemic performance; schema compliance should be scored separately.

## Hidden Assumptions

1. The External Evidence Researcher output contains stable atomic claims and evidence units that can be paired without imposing a preferred reasoning path after the fact.
2. Claim identity can be maintained across paraphrase, synthesis, split, merge, and scope change with sufficient reliability.
3. The Alternative Theorist should use claim–support links rather than mainly using contradictions, qualifications, limitations, or unresolved gaps.
4. The ground-truth support relation is sufficiently determinate for the selected items.
5. Equivalent relation content can be produced in prose and typed form without meaningful residual differences in wording, salience, parsing, or cost.
6. A forced selected-subset report reflects evidence selection rather than post hoc rationalization.
7. The same selected-subset requirement affects all conditions equally.
8. Correct supplied relations are read and understood as descriptive metadata rather than instructions or endorsements.
9. Corrupted relations can be surface-matched to correct relations without becoming obviously implausible.
10. Evaluators can distinguish an unsupported factual assertion from a legitimate hypothesis or objection.
11. Reduced unsupported factual claims will not be achieved by reducing claim count, alternative coverage, specificity, or usefulness.
12. The chosen task set contains enough relation ambiguity or dispersion for the availability manipulation to be nontrivial.
13. The resource budget can accommodate package construction, relation validation, forced selection, and evaluation without silently reducing candidate generation or final reasoning.
14. Apparatus behavior observed at this handoff is informative for later package experiments, despite no current evidence of stage representativeness.

## Scope and Definition Problems

The corrected scope should be stated as follows:

> Validate a measurement apparatus for one predeclared class of direct claim–support relations at the External Evidence Researcher → Alternative Theorist handoff. Estimate manipulation uptake and observable write–select–use lineage under a forced-selection protocol. Do not estimate general role-separation, typed-package, stage-sensitivity, or full-loop effects.

The following terms require operational definitions before effectiveness research:

- **Claim:** atomicity, scope, and treatment of compound or comparative statements.
- **Evidence item:** excerpt, source-level unit, derived summary, or source interpretation.
- **Direct support:** minimum entailment or evidential contribution standard and exclusions.
- **Correct relation:** who adjudicates it, using what information, and with what reliability.
- **Relation absent:** whether claims and evidence remain adjacent, ordered, and inferentially recoverable.
- **Controlled prose:** fixed template, wording, position, and semantic-equivalence check.
- **Typed relation:** exact schema, serialization, identifiers, and parser behavior.
- **Selected subset:** when it is emitted, allowed cardinality, and whether nonselection means rejection, omission, or capacity limit.
- **Final use:** explicit citation, support attribution, semantic reliance, or downstream claim lineage.
- **Unsupported factual claim:** support threshold, source-attribution rule, and treatment of derived conclusions.
- **Alternative quality:** coverage, distinctness, plausibility, epistemic typing, and relation to evidence.
- **Fixed resources:** primary budget unit, tolerance, saved-budget rule, and reallocation rule.

## Risks of Misleading Research

- A lower unsupported-claim count could be caused by fewer factual claims, fewer alternatives, lower specificity, or lower task completion.
- A relation-present condition could appear better because it supplies new information, not because typed structure helps.
- A typed condition could appear better because it is shorter, more adjacent, easier to parse, or closer to evaluator format.
- A selected-subset checkpoint could create the improvement attributed to relation metadata.
- Corrupted links could be rejected because they are conspicuous, not because the reader verifies evidence.
- Correct links could be followed because they appear authoritative, not because the reader understands support.
- Human or model validation used to construct the package could improve content quality and be misattributed to schema.
- Evaluators could favor outputs that reproduce the supplied relation structure, creating format alignment rather than epistemic improvement.
- Many-condition exploratory analysis could produce post hoc stories about geometry, cues, selection, or noise without sufficient discrimination.
- Broad external searching could rediscover adjacent studies already represented in C0003 and incorrectly present source count as new independent evidence.
- A null could be misread as no relation effect when the manipulation was weak, the reader ignored the format, the task lacked ambiguity, or measurement reliability was poor.
- A positive local result could be overgeneralized to other transitions, relation types, package sizes, tasks, models, or the full role-separated loop.

## Questions That Would Not Actually Discriminate

The following questions may support apparatus construction but do not by themselves discriminate among the Explorer’s proposed causal hypotheses:

- Internal Questions 1–7 mainly inventory branch conventions, fields, examples, and accounting rules. They are useful setup questions, not tests of relation benefit, geometry, authority, selection, or use.
- External Question 6 on annotation schemes supports measurement design but does not distinguish a relation effect from cue compliance or geometry.
- External Question 10 asks broadly what would justify transfer. Without predeclared transfer criteria, it invites narrative judgment rather than discrimination.
- Stage-local sensitivity cannot be tested by validating only one transition.
- A correct-versus-corrupted link comparison does not isolate authority cues unless surface plausibility, relation density, wording, geometry, and expected correctness are controlled.
- Neutral versus relation-seeking prompts do not isolate prompt substitution if the stronger prompt adds reasoning steps, output requirements, or effective compute.
- High versus low relation coverage does not isolate a coverage–noise curve if package length, density, selection burden, and ambiguity change simultaneously.
- Package presence, selection, and final citation do not prove causal use. A reader may cite without relying on an item or rely on it without explicit citation.
- Controlled prose versus typed fields does not isolate representation if wording, token count, adjacency, delimiters, or parser affordances remain different.

## Required Corrections to the Research Direction

1. **Keep the frontier, but redefine the immediate goal as apparatus validation only.** No package-effect or stage-effect claim should be authorized from the first pilot.
2. **Define the eligible relation narrowly.** Prefer direct, adjudicable claim–support pairs; exclude ambiguous, partial, qualification-dominant, contradiction-dominant, and many-to-many cases from the first reliability sample unless separately coded.
3. **Separate the two estimands.** Treat relation availability and relation representation as independent comparisons with separate conclusions.
4. **Align outcomes with the Alternative Theorist role.** Score factual assertions separately from hypotheses, speculation, objections, and unresolved questions. Add correct epistemic typing and alternative coverage as required gates.
5. **Make the selected-subset checkpoint an explicit common intervention.** Hold it constant, cost it, and avoid describing it as passive observation of natural retrieval.
6. **Reduce the first pilot to a minimum viable contrast.** Use relation absent versus correct relation, followed by one prose-versus-typed comparison only if semantic-equivalence and reliability checks pass.
7. **Defer broad mechanism factorials.** Corrupted links, authority framing, prompt substitution, relation density, false versus irrelevant links, and multiple geometry manipulations should be follow-on diagnostics.
8. **Predeclare manipulation checks.** Verify that relation content differs where intended, equivalent-content conditions are approximately equivalent, geometry is recorded, readers can parse the schema, and selected-subset records are valid.
9. **Predeclare separate outcome families.** Selection, support attribution, unsupported factual extension, scope and qualification preservation, epistemic typing, coverage, omission, usefulness, task completion, and cost must remain distinguishable.
10. **Specify evidence against the favored direction.** Count as adverse evidence: reliable null or harm after successful manipulation; reduced coverage or usefulness; prose matching typed performance; relation-seeking prompts matching explicit links; correct selection without better final support; or corrupted links being followed at similar rates to correct links.
11. **Treat this handoff as pragmatic.** Do not claim it is especially sensitive or representative unless a later predeclared cross-transition comparison supports that claim.
12. **Prevent search duplication.** Researchers should begin from the accepted C0003 gap and seek only evidence or branch-local definitions that resolve the remaining apparatus questions.

## Internal Research Brief

The Internal Evidence Researcher should not re-summarize the branch’s accepted methodological requirements. The useful internal task is to determine whether this specific pilot can be instantiated from existing branch objects without inventing conclusions.

Priority questions:

1. Identify the exact existing forms of claim IDs, source IDs, evidence excerpts, support statements, epistemic labels, qualifications, objections, and unresolved questions that could populate a minimal claim–support inventory.
2. Determine whether the External Evidence Researcher → Alternative Theorist transition contains enough stable factual claim reuse to measure support attribution separately from legitimate hypothesis generation.
3. Find branch-local examples that expose identity and relation edge cases: paraphrase, split, merge, scope change, qualification weakening, support substitution, contradiction, and derived synthesis.
4. Identify which accepted rules already support a forced selected-subset object and which details remain undefined: timing, cardinality, schema, rejection versus omission, and final-use linkage.
5. Separate fields already required by accepted state from genuinely new fields needed for this pilot.
6. Identify where the Explorer’s proposed internal questions duplicate accepted C0002–C0003 conclusions and replace repetition with unresolved implementation details.
7. Produce a minimal candidate schema and a list of unresolved decisions, not a claim that the schema is valid.
8. State whether the selected handoff is measurable under the available branch conventions. If not, identify the precise missing object or role-output mismatch rather than proposing an unrelated frontier.

The Internal Researcher should explicitly label branch-local examples as examples, not evidence that the intervention works.

## External Research Brief

The External Evidence Researcher should not conduct another broad search for whether metadata, structured inputs, retrieval, or claim verification matter. C0003 already records the broad corrected-contrast gap. Search should be restricted to methodological evidence that could validate or falsify this apparatus.

Priority search targets:

1. Direct controlled studies where the same claims and evidence are presented with versus without explicit claim–evidence relations, with enough detail to assess content, geometry, prompts, and resources.
2. Studies comparing approximately equivalent relation content in prose and typed or structured form, with manipulation checks or reported residual format differences.
3. Paradigms that record selected evidence before generation and distinguish selection from final factual use, including evidence that the checkpoint itself changes behavior.
4. Controlled correct-versus-corrupted relation or cue-compliance paradigms with surface-matched or plausibility-matched controls.
5. Annotation and reliability methods specifically applicable to claim identity, direct support, scope and qualification preservation, and split or merge lineage.
6. Resource-accounted designs that expose the cost of relation construction, validation, parsing, selection, and final generation.

For every located source, record whether it actually discriminates among relation information, representation, geometry, authority cues, forced selection, and downstream use. Adjacent evidence should be labeled as apparatus guidance or transfer-relevant evidence, not as a direct effect estimate.

Stop conditions should include:

- the study changes underlying evidence content along with links;
- the study lacks a relation-absent or equivalent-content comparison;
- selection is inferred only from final output;
- evaluator format is aligned with the treatment without blinding or counterchecks;
- added validation, candidates, tools, or compute are bundled with the relation condition;
- the task does not permit separation of factual assertions from hypotheses or opinions.

## Handoff

Proceed with the frontier after narrowing it. The two researchers should treat C0004 as validation of a minimal claim–support handoff apparatus, not as an effectiveness study. The central correction is to align the outcome with the Alternative Theorist’s epistemic role: factual support errors must be separated from legitimate hypotheses and objections, and the forced selected-subset checkpoint must be treated as a costed common intervention rather than neutral observation. Broad cue, geometry, prompt, coverage, and noise manipulations should be deferred until the minimal availability and representation contrasts pass manipulation and reliability checks.

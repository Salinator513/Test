# Integrator — C0004

## Research Question Addressed

Can the branch validate a minimal apparatus for testing whether explicit claim–support relations change evidence selection and downstream factual use at the External Evidence Researcher → Alternative Theorist handoff, while separating relation availability from representation, treating selection as an observable intervention, preserving role-appropriate epistemic categories, and accounting for full resources?

C0004 does not answer whether claim–support relations improve performance, whether typed fields are superior to prose, whether this handoff is unusually sensitive, or whether fresh role separation improves research quality. It determines what the current evidence permits the branch to accept about the apparatus and what must remain unresolved.

## Evidence Assessment

### Directness

The corrected direct-evidence gap persists. No `C0004-SRC-*` study holds the same atomic claims, evidence excerpts, source identifiers, order, surrounding context, reader prompts, candidate and selector opportunities, and full end-to-end resources constant while adding only correct claim–support relations. No retained study compares approximately equivalent relation content in controlled prose and typed fields while checking semantic equivalence and residual geometry, token, parser, and salience differences. No retained study tests the External Evidence Researcher → Alternative Theorist handoff or an equivalent evidence-collection → alternative-explanation transition. This is a search-bounded conclusion from `C0004-EXT-01` through `C0004-EXT-14`, the C0004 Evidence Gaps, and `C0004-SRC-01` through `C0004-SRC-15`; it reinforces `C0003-INTG-02` rather than replacing it.

### What the evidence does establish

The retained sources provide meaningful apparatus and mechanism evidence:

- Evidence ordering, curation, relational organization, semantic selection, explicit binding, facts-first workflows, and qualification preservation can affect verification efficiency, comprehension, attribution, coverage, or accuracy in their own settings: `C0004-SRC-03`, `C0004-SRC-04`, `C0004-SRC-07`, `C0004-SRC-08`, `C0004-SRC-13`, `C0004-SRC-14`, and `C0004-SRC-15`; interpreted in `C0004-EXT-01` through `C0004-EXT-08`.
- Selection is behaviorally consequential rather than a neutral observation. Selected evidence can change model and human decisions, evidence ordering changes stopping and reading, and curated versus model-selected evidence changes attribution: `C0004-SRC-03`, `C0004-SRC-04`, and `C0004-SRC-06`; `C0004-EXT-01`, `C0004-EXT-03`, and `C0004-EXT-04`.
- Correct selection and visible attribution do not guarantee correct final semantic use. Residual attribution errors remain after evidence selection, and evidence quality is less predictive for complex outputs: `C0004-SRC-04` and `C0004-SRC-05`; `C0004-EXT-12` and `C0004-EXT-13`.
- Citation and provenance cues can change trust, belief, hallucination, or evaluator response without establishing evidence verification: `C0004-SRC-01`, `C0004-SRC-02`, `C0004-SRC-09`, and `C0004-SRC-12`; `C0004-EXT-09` through `C0004-EXT-11` and `C0004-EXT-14`.
- Existing annotation work supports the feasibility of relational schemas, information-change coding, qualification-sensitive evidence sufficiency, and structured support evaluation, but not a validated B001 direct-support or lineage ontology: `C0004-SRC-09` through `C0004-SRC-11`, `C0004-SRC-13`, and `C0004-SRC-15`.

### Independence and reliability

The fifteen retained sources are not fifteen independent replications of a claim–support effect. Several are related fact-verification or attribution families, several are apparatus or evaluation studies rather than interventions, and the long-form sources test bundled systems. `C0004-SRC-02` is a preprint and remains provisional. Human-interface samples are heterogeneous and sometimes small: `C0004-SRC-03` uses five participants, `C0004-SRC-13` uses four experts over ten abstracts, and `C0004-SRC-01` analyzes twenty-six researchers from one organization after exclusions. Same-model agreement among the cycle agents is not independent validation under `AGENT_PROTOCOL.md`, `C0001-INTG-01`, and the accepted source-independence rules.

### Compute and role-separation isolation

The evidence does not isolate role separation from added or differently allocated compute. Positive systems add or change ranking, evidence-set construction, human curation, trained selectors, debate, scrutiny, deterministic validation, graph construction, retrieval, planning, fact extraction, append queries, model specialization, or repeated generation. The closest partial token control, `C0004-SRC-14`, does not include the filtering model’s full construction and inference cost and is not a linked-versus-unlinked equivalent-content test. C0004 therefore strengthens the resource-accounting requirement but supplies no equal-compute claim–support, typed-representation, fresh-role, or full-loop effect estimate. This supports `C0001-INTG-02`, `C0002-INTG-05`, and `C0003-INTG-06`.

## Main Interpretation

The best-supported branch interpretation is methodological and conditional:

A correct explicit claim–support relation may reduce reconstruction burden in some regimes, especially when evidence is dispersed, relations are ambiguous, or qualifications are vulnerable to loss. The current evidence justifies testing that hypothesis, but it does not establish the effect. Any observed advantage can be produced by added relation information, changed geometry and search burden, forced deliberation, authority cues, writer or validator privilege, reader capability, prompt strength, sampling and selection, evaluator alignment, coverage changes, or resource reallocation.

C0004 should therefore authorize a minimal apparatus-validation program, not an effectiveness claim. The first apparatus must define one narrow direct-support relation, preserve claim and evidence identity, separate relation availability from representation, impose the same costed forced-selection protocol across compared conditions, trace write–select–use events, score factual assertions separately from legitimate hypotheses and objections, and apply coverage, usefulness, reliability, and end-to-end resource gates.

## Alternative Interpretations

### Geometry and search-burden account — `C0004-ALT-01`

Retain as an evidence-backed alternative mechanism hypothesis. `C0004-SRC-03`, `C0004-SRC-13`, and `C0004-SRC-14` show effects from ordering, compression, mapping, and semantic filtering. These findings can explain apparent relation benefits without a distinct epistemic interpretation of a support edge. A relation-information interpretation requires survival under recorded or matched position, adjacency, order, token distance, delimiters, package length, visible evidence quantity, and stopping opportunities.

### Authority-cue compliance account — `C0004-ALT-02`

Retain as an evidence-backed adverse-risk hypothesis. `C0004-SRC-02` reports fabricated-citation-induced model hallucination, `C0004-SRC-12` reports a small citation-induced human belief effect, `C0004-SRC-01` reports changed trust without significant behavioral reliance, and `C0004-SRC-09` reports evaluator sensitivity to superficial form. These sources do not directly test claim–support edges, but they show that relation-like markers cannot be assumed to be neutral pointers.

### Apparatus-induced deliberation and selection — `C0004-ALT-03`

Accept as a supported apparatus explanation. The selected-subset checkpoint, stronger prompts, validation, curation, repeated sampling, and selector opportunities can themselves change behavior and consume budget. `C0004-SRC-03`, `C0004-SRC-04`, and `C0004-SRC-06` support this interpretation. A common forced-selection checkpoint can control this factor between relation conditions, but it does not estimate natural latent retrieval and cannot be credited to the relation alone.

### Reader-bottleneck or minimal null — `C0004-ALT-04`

Retain as unresolved. `C0004-SRC-01`, `C0004-SRC-04`, and `C0004-SRC-05` are compatible with a null or weak average effect because richer provenance may not change behavior and correct evidence can still be misused in complex generation. A null is interpretable only after successful manipulation, schema parsing, task sensitivity, adequate power, and annotation reliability; otherwise treatment collapse remains plausible.

### Hybrid account

Retain as the broadest plausible conceptual structure, not an estimated model. Relation information, geometry, authority cues, forced selection, writer quality, reader capability, qualification preservation, coverage, and compute may interact and reverse direction across tasks and readers. The current evidence does not identify a dominant mechanism.

## Accepted New Facts

The Archivist should add the following source-reported facts with their limitations and should not treat the source count as an independence count:

- **`C0004-SRC-01` / `C0004-EXT-09`:** In a preregistered within-subjects study of twenty-six analyzed researchers, a granular claim–evidence interface reduced reported trust relative to a sentence/source-citation baseline but did not significantly change behavioral reliance or confidence. Both conditions contained attribution; interface and preprocessing changes were bundled.
- **`C0004-SRC-02` / `C0004-EXT-10`:** A June 2026 preprint benchmark reports that citation presence, including fabricated citations, increased hallucination across tested models and domains; fabricated citations attached to true claims increased hallucination by reported amounts of 3–22 percentage points. Citations were strings rather than linked evidence passages, and the paper is not peer reviewed.
- **`C0004-SRC-03` / `C0004-EXT-01`:** A five-participant fact-verification user study reported higher decision success, fewer sentences read, and fewer undecided cases for incrementally ranked evidence than fixed selected evidence. Ordering, stopping, visible quantity, and interaction differed.
- **`C0004-SRC-04` / `C0004-EXT-03`, `C0004-EXT-13`:** In one hundred PolitiHop claims, human-curated evidence improved attribution relative to model-selected evidence, while tested LLMs still produced inaccurate final attributions. Evidence-set size and content differed, and human curation added privilege.
- **`C0004-SRC-05` / `C0004-EXT-12`:** Across six long-document tasks and five LLMs, attribution procedure effectiveness varied by model scale and tuning; evidence quality predicted response quality for simpler outputs but not reliably for complex outputs. Procedures used different process structures and compute.
- **`C0004-SRC-06` / `C0004-EXT-04`:** Trained evidence agents selected passage sentences that changed model and human answers and generalized across readers, demonstrating that selected evidence is an active intervention. The task was passage QA and used additional training and inference.
- **`C0004-SRC-07` / `C0004-EXT-05`:** A fact-checking system combining atomic subclaim–evidence contracts, mechanized citation scrutiny, and judge selection improved joint label-and-evidence metrics, with component ablations indicating contributions from binding and validation. The components and added calls were bundled.
- **`C0004-SRC-08` / `C0004-EXT-06`:** An eight-topic long-form reporting system combining evidence units, a knowledge graph, retrieval, outlining, fact extraction, append queries, and multimodal tools reported gains in factual coverage, accuracy, and visual-evidence integration; ablations reduced coverage or accuracy. No isolated claim–support or equal-compute effect was shown.
- **`C0004-SRC-09` / `C0004-EXT-14`:** A long-form report evaluation study operationalized claims, evidence, warrants, and qualifiers and found that off-the-shelf LLM judges could be influenced by superficial form or miss broken support relations. It validates an evaluation risk, not the C0004 intervention.
- **`C0004-SRC-10`:** The SciClaim corpus demonstrates a fine-grained scientific relation schema with 12,738 labels, supporting codebook feasibility but not a direct-support handoff effect.
- **`C0004-SRC-11`:** The SPICED dataset contains 6,000 semantically matched scientific-finding pairs and supports annotation of information change across paraphrase. It does not provide complete split/merge lineage or a relation-metadata effect.
- **`C0004-SRC-12` / `C0004-EXT-11`:** Six human experiments report a small citation-induced increase in belief in trivia claims, summarized as `d=0.13` with a reported 95% confidence interval of `[0.06, 0.20]`. The citations were not linked evidence passages.
- **`C0004-SRC-13` / `C0004-EXT-02`:** EvidenceMap reports a 51.9% reduction in comprehension time relative to raw abstracts in a small four-expert, ten-abstract evaluation, with dependency-annotation agreement reported as 0.691. Compression, layout, organization, and expert construction were bundled.
- **`C0004-SRC-14` / `C0004-EXT-07`:** In a FACTKG setting, semantic filtering reported 78.85% accuracy versus 77.54% for a lexical heuristic under a retained-token comparison, while unfiltered truncation-dominated input reported 52.70%. Filter-construction cost was not fully accounted, and the comparison was not linked versus unlinked equivalent content.
- **`C0004-SRC-15` / `C0004-EXT-08`:** Fluency-preserving evidence omissions produced uneven model detection, including reported 21% accuracy for omitted adverbial modifiers and 63% for omitted dates; trained methods improved evidence-sufficiency and fact-checking metrics. This supports qualification-sensitive measurement, not a relation-metadata effect.

## Qualified or Narrowed Claims

- The relation-information hypothesis remains experimentally justified but unsupported as an effect. Its plausible scope is limited to predeclared tasks with nontrivial support reconstruction, sufficient ambiguity or dispersion, validated readers, and controlled resources.
- “Claim–support” is narrowed to a predeclared class of direct, adjudicable support. Partial support, contextualization, exemplification, qualification, contradiction, motivation, and ambiguous many-to-many relations require separate coding or exclusion from the first reliability sample.
- The selected-subset checkpoint is retained as a common costed apparatus intervention, not natural retrieval and not evidence of relation causality.
- The Alternative Theorist outcome must distinguish factual assertions and source attributions from hypotheses, speculation, objections, and unresolved questions.
- The External Evidence Researcher → Alternative Theorist handoff is retained only as a pragmatic candidate. A preliminary measurability screen must establish enough stable direct-support pairs and downstream factual reuse.
- Relation availability and representation remain separate estimands. A positive availability result does not establish typed-field superiority, and a representation result does not establish benefit relative to relation absence.
- Geometry, authority cues, reader capability, writer or validator privilege, evaluator alignment, prompt strength, selection, and compute remain active explanations rather than nuisance details that can be ignored after the fact.

## Contradictions and Tensions

- **Structured evidence benefit versus no behavioral uptake:** `C0004-SRC-03` and `C0004-SRC-13` report efficiency benefits, while `C0004-SRC-01` reports changed trust without significant reliance change.
- **Evidence binding versus authority harm:** `C0004-SRC-07` reports gains in a mechanized evidence-binding system, while `C0004-SRC-02` and `C0004-SRC-12` show that citation cues can increase hallucination or belief.
- **Selection value versus selection insufficiency:** `C0004-SRC-04` shows better attribution from curated selection, yet also shows residual attribution errors; `C0004-SRC-05` finds weak evidence-quality prediction for complex outputs.
- **Long-form gains versus component attribution:** `C0004-SRC-08` reports large bundled improvements, but graph construction, retrieval, planning, extraction, repeated queries, tools, and compute remain inseparable.
- **Traceability versus semantic support:** IDs, links, and citations improve observability but do not prove correct support, qualification, or use.
- **Forced observability versus behavioral contamination:** The selected-subset checkpoint makes selection inspectable while also changing deliberation and resource use.
- **Narrow direct-support reliability versus ecological validity:** Excluding ambiguous and qualification-rich pairs may improve annotation reliability but reduce transfer to real research synthesis.
- **Geometry control versus feasible equivalence:** Strong matching is necessary for interpretation, but prose and typed forms cannot be perfectly identical in wording, tokens, parsing, and salience.
- **Null versus treatment collapse:** Equality may represent a real bounded null, reader non-use, redundant relation content, insensitive tasks, weak reliability, or offsetting subgroup effects.
- **Apparatus cost versus fixed budget:** Validation, adjudication, schema repair, and forced selection improve control while consuming the same resources whose allocation is under study.

## Remaining Hypotheses

- **`C0004-EXP-01` / relation-information hypothesis:** Correct direct-support relations may reduce reconstruction error under nontrivial ambiguity or dispersion.
- **`C0004-ALT-01` / geometry and search-burden hypothesis:** Benefits may be caused mainly by position, adjacency, compression, ordering, delimiters, and reduced search.
- **`C0004-ALT-02` / authority-cue hypothesis:** Relation markers may induce trust or compliance with correct and corrupted links.
- **`C0004-ALT-03` / apparatus-induced deliberation hypothesis:** Forced selection, prompts, validation, curation, sampling, or selector opportunities may produce the observed advantage.
- **`C0004-ALT-04` / reader-bottleneck null:** Relations may have no stable average downstream benefit after successful manipulation and full controls.
- **Representation-equivalence hypothesis:** Controlled prose may reproduce typed-field performance when content and geometry are approximately matched.
- **Selection-to-use mediation hypothesis:** Relation information may change selection without improving final support attribution, or may improve attribution without changing selection.
- **Qualification-preservation hypothesis:** A benefit may appear primarily through preserved scope and modifiers rather than fewer unsupported claims.
- **Task × model × ambiguity interaction:** Effects may vary by reader model, relation ambiguity, evidence dispersion, qualification density, package length, and task complexity.
- **Coverage–noise interaction:** More correct links may improve recall while clutter, false links, and irrelevant relations create sign reversals.
- **Prompt-substitution hypothesis:** Relation-seeking instructions may reconstruct some links but may add deliberation and compute.
- **Writer–validator privilege hypothesis:** Gains may come from curation, correction, adjudication, or validation labor rather than schema.
- **Evaluator-alignment hypothesis:** Structured outputs may score better because treatment and evaluator formats align.
- **Residual role-boundary hypothesis:** Deferred. No residual boundary property or effect is established.

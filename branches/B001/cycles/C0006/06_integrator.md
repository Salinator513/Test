# Integrator — C0006

## Research Question Addressed

How should the External Evidence Researcher → Alternative Theorist handoff be represented so that a later direct-support measurability audit cannot hide pair inflation, non-independent observations, qualification loss, low retained coverage, or role mismatch; and under what conditions could a retained-coverage or role-relevance gate later be specified?

C0006 does not answer whether the handoff contains a nontrivial measurable direct-support core. It addresses the prior methodological dependency: what objects, mappings, candidate universes, coverage ratios, exclusions, cluster structures, and observational boundaries must be fixed before such an audit can be interpreted.

## Evidence Assessment

The current-cycle evidence supports a denominator-map deliverable, not a numerical gate or a branch-local prevalence conclusion.

The Internal Evidence Researcher found that accepted branch state already requires claim-level lineage, role-aligned epistemic scoring, multidimensional transfer measurement, observable write–select–incorporate checkpoints, a narrow direct-support primary object embedded in a broader many-to-many schema, development/audit separation, coverage-sensitive reliability, and local failure policies. However, the branch has no authorized development corpus, untouched audit corpus, target population, canonical object system, role-function codebook, candidate-universe procedure, cluster model, role-relevance rubric, or threshold. The internal contribution is therefore a prospective denominator-and-observability map and allowed-ratio constraints, not empirical counts or reliability.

The twenty-source C0006 external ledger supplies component methods rather than a direct validation of one B001 apparatus. Crossed and multiple-membership models support non-nested object mappings (`C0006-EXT-01`; `C0006-SRC-01`). Structured annotation and evaluation methods show that discontinuous spans, relations, sets, groups, alignments, and boundary-sensitive agreement can be represented (`C0006-EXT-02`; `C0006-SRC-02` through `C0006-SRC-06`). TREC pooling supplies a strong precedent for procedure-bounded candidate universes and challenge testing, while also showing that shallow pools can be biased and deep pools can be robust only conditionally and at substantial cost (`C0006-EXT-03`, `C0006-EXT-14`; `C0006-SRC-07`, `C0006-SRC-08`). Selective prediction establishes that quality can improve as retained coverage falls, but does not define role relevance or a valid B001 operating point (`C0006-EXT-04`, `C0006-EXT-13`; `C0006-SRC-09` through `C0006-SRC-11`). Pseudoreplication and mixed-effects sources establish that the replication and cluster unit must follow the intended inference, while conflicting maximal and parsimonious guidance defeats a universal cluster model (`C0006-EXT-05`, `C0006-EXT-15`; `C0006-SRC-12` through `C0006-SRC-14`). Sampling and decision sources support prospective strata, known inclusion probabilities, decision-linked reliability planning, and false-passage analysis only after a sampling frame and downstream decision are fixed (`C0006-EXT-06`, `C0006-EXT-07`, `C0006-EXT-16`; `C0006-SRC-15` through `C0006-SRC-18`). CONSORT supplies an exclusion-flow reporting analogy, not validation of exclusions (`C0006-EXT-08`, `C0006-EXT-17`; `C0006-SRC-19`). Turpin et al. supplies direct evidence that generated explanations can omit causal influences, reinforcing the accepted boundary that reports and checkpoints are observable records rather than causal-use evidence (`C0006-EXT-09`; `C0006-SRC-20`).

The strongest negative evidence is decisive. No source studies the target handoff, validates a mixed factual/nonfactual Alternative Theorist workload denominator, establishes a representative retained direct-support core, supplies a complete latent candidate-relation universe, selects the correct grouping or metric, defines a B001 cluster model, provides a B001 numerical gate, or isolates role separation from added information, labor, selection, annotation, and compute (`C0006-EXT-10` through `C0006-EXT-17`; C0006 Evidence Gaps and Fixed-Compute Versus Added-Compute Evidence).

The twenty sources are not twenty independent validations. They form partially dependent method families in structured annotation, TREC pooling, selective prediction, dependence modeling, sampling, decision analysis, and reporting. Same-model or same-agent agreement is not used as validation, and agreement among the current agents is not treated as evidence.

## Main Interpretation

The strongest supported interpretation is a **linked-denominator and conditional-gate account**.

The handoff cannot be represented by one clean denominator hierarchy. Task instances, role outputs, sections, role-function units, upstream factual units, evidence units, procedure-defined candidate sets, relation edges, bounded relation groups, eligible primary units, downstream units, observable lineage links, exclusions, and source/claim/output/task clusters are distinct and frequently non-nested. The apparatus should therefore use a linked lattice or multi-table object system with explicit mappings and an allowed-ratio table.

Every coverage or prevalence result must name its numerator, denominator, candidate-generation procedure, identity rules, mapping direction, and scope. Edge counts and bounded-group counts should remain visible in parallel because pairwise expansion can inflate counts while grouping can erase legitimate complementary or jointly necessary support. Candidate universes must be named by how they were produced—writer-supplied, coder-proposed under fixed access, bounded-source enumeration, or checkpoint-reported—and none may be called the complete universe of true relations.

A numerical retained-coverage or role-relevance gate remains premature. Before a gate can be specified, the branch must freeze a one-sentence target decision: what later experiment a pass would authorize, what claim that experiment could support, what task population or role stratum it would cover, and what primary unit of inference applies. The corpus, sampling frame, role-function taxonomy, codebook, candidate universe, role-relevance rubric, cluster model, resource rule, and false-passage and false-failure consequences must then be fixed. Until those decisions exist, a denominator map can prevent invalid ratios but cannot determine an operating threshold.

## Alternative Interpretations

### Apparatus-created measurability

`C0006-ALT-01` remains a live evidence-backed mechanism. Atomic splitting, candidate generation, eligibility filtering, source access, repeated coding, adjudication, challenge testing, and checkpoints may create or materially reshape the measurable core. The relevant distinction is not “real versus artificial,” but ordinary-artifact objects versus offline-annotation objects versus intervention-created records. A successful instrumented audit could validate a curated local measurement object without showing that the ordinary uninstrumented handoff naturally exposes the same object.

### Construct and metric underdetermination

`C0006-ALT-02` is strongly supported as a methodological alternative. MASI, gamma, CEAF, LEA, strict and relaxed boundaries, edge and group scoring, weighting, and cluster models encode different theories of identity and error. A linked lattice makes those choices explicit but does not produce a uniquely correct ontology. Pass/fail may reverse across plausible prospectively frozen definitions. This does not make local validation impossible; it requires a sharply scoped decision target and predeclared robustness panel.

### Factual-stratum sparse-reuse null

`C0006-ALT-03` remains unresolved. Direct-support incorporation may be measurable within a factual/assertive stratum but too sparse or peripheral to represent the Alternative Theorist’s full workload, which legitimately includes hypotheses, speculation, objections, hybrids, and unresolved questions. A factual-stratum pilot can still be useful if the later claim is explicitly limited to that stratum. It cannot be generalized to the whole role without separate role-relevance evidence.

### Deep-curation bounded-universe account

TREC evidence supports a hybrid possibility: a procedure-bounded candidate universe may become adequate for one stated decision after deep, diverse generation and challenge testing. Such adequacy would be local and resource-intensive. It would not establish completeness, low-cost ordinary-handoff measurability, role separation, or fresh-invocation benefit.

### Minimal descriptive null

C0006 contains no branch-local prevalence, retained-coverage, role-relevance, reliability, exclusion-composition, cluster-diversity, or cost result. The alternatives therefore cannot yet be ranked by magnitude. The immediate result is improved methodological specification only.

## Claim Decisions

### Claim ID: C0006-INTG-01 — Linked denominator-and-observability lattice

- **Proposed status:** Accepted methodological requirement.
- **Evidence and source IDs:** C0006 Critic Required Corrections 3–5; C0006 Internal Evidence denominator map and allowed-ratio constraints; `C0006-EXT-01`, `C0006-EXT-02`, `C0006-EXT-11`; `C0006-SRC-01` through `C0006-SRC-06`.

### Claim ID: C0006-INTG-02 — Named ratios and procedure-bounded candidate universes

## Accepted New Facts

- **Source-reported non-nesting fact:** Crossed and multiple-membership models permit observations to belong to non-nested and multiple higher-level classifications. This supports explicit mappings but does not define B001 semantics or ratios. Provenance: `C0006-EXT-01`; `C0006-SRC-01`.
- **Source-reported structured-object fact:** brat, MASI, gamma, CEAF, and LEA provide representations or metrics for discontinuous spans, sets, relations, groups, alignments, entities, and links. Their differences show that grouping and distance encode substantive assumptions. Provenance: `C0006-EXT-02`, `C0006-EXT-12`; `C0006-SRC-02` through `C0006-SRC-06`.
- **Source-reported procedural-universe fact:** TREC pooling defines a candidate/judgment universe by contributing retrieval procedures rather than by the latent universe of all relevant documents. Shallow pooling can be biased; a deep diverse pool can remain robust under later challenge testing. Provenance: `C0006-EXT-03`, `C0006-EXT-14`; `C0006-SRC-07`, `C0006-SRC-08`.
- **Source-reported coverage–quality fact:** Reject-option and selective-classification methods improve retained-case error by rejecting items and explicitly expose a quality–coverage tradeoff. They do not define B001 role relevance or annotation reliability. Provenance: `C0006-EXT-04`, `C0006-EXT-13`; `C0006-SRC-09` through `C0006-SRC-11`.
- **Source-reported dependence fact:** Pseudoreplication and mixed-effects methods require replication and error structures to match the design and intended generalization. Maximal and parsimonious guidance conflict when data cannot support complex models. Provenance: `C0006-EXT-05`, `C0006-EXT-15`; `C0006-SRC-12` through `C0006-SRC-14`.
- **Source-reported sampling fact:** Stratified and unequal-probability estimators require a defined finite frame, prospective strata or inclusion probabilities, and nonzero inclusion probability. They cannot repair a convenience sample or outcome-selected stratum. Provenance: `C0006-EXT-06`, `C0006-EXT-16`; `C0006-SRC-15`, `C0006-SRC-16`.
- **Source-reported decision-linked design fact:** Reliability-study sizing and decision-curve methods tie operating decisions to target reliability, uncertainty, power, and false-positive/false-negative consequences rather than universal cutoffs. Transfer to B001 is methodological. Provenance: `C0006-EXT-07`; `C0006-SRC-17`, `C0006-SRC-18`.
- **Source-reported reporting fact:** CONSORT requires transparent flow, losses, exclusions, and reasons. This supports an exclusion ledger by analogy but does not validate exclusions. Provenance: `C0006-EXT-08`, `C0006-EXT-17`; `C0006-SRC-19`.
- **Source-reported explanation-unfaithfulness fact:** Model explanations can omit experimentally induced influences on answers. Reports and rationales are not reliable causal-process records. Provenance: `C0006-EXT-09`; `C0006-SRC-20`.
- **Search-bounded external gap:** No C0006 source validates the target handoff’s prevalence, representative factual core, role-relevance rubric, candidate completeness, preferred grouping, cluster model, numerical gate, or role-separation effect. Provenance: `C0006-EXT-10` through `C0006-EXT-17`; C0006 Evidence Gaps.
- **Source-independence fact:** The twenty-source ledger contains related method families and component evidence; it is not twenty independent confirmations of one denominator system.

## Qualified or Narrowed Claims

- “Denominator hierarchy” is narrowed to a linked denominator-and-observability lattice with explicit mappings.
- “Coverage” is not one quantity. It must be named as factual-stratum share, eligible factual coverage, edge eligibility, group eligibility, downstream factual-unit coverage, qualification coverage, role-function coverage, source/cluster diversity, or prospectively weighted substantive coverage.
- “Full handoff workload” is separated into complete sampled outputs, multilabel role-function units, factual/assertive units, eligible direct-support units, and observable-incorporation units.
- “Candidate relations” is replaced by one explicitly procedure-defined universe at a time.
- “Pair inflation” survives only as a risk; grouping can also erase legitimate multiplicity.
- “Role relevance” is limited to prospectively defined observable role functions or blinded importance ratings, with unweighted results retained.
- “Effective count” is limited to a declared inferential and sampling model; cluster sensitivities cannot be mined for a favorable answer.
- “Substantive factual content depends on eligible units” is replaced by observable relation-preserving incorporation or prospectively blinded role-function importance.
- A factual-stratum success can authorize only a factual-stratum claim unless separate whole-role relevance is established.
- A procedure-bounded candidate universe may be adequate for a local decision after challenge testing, but is not complete.

## Contradictions and Tensions

- **Lattice clarity versus construct validity:** Explicit object classes prevent invalid ratios but do not identify the correct semantic ontology.
- **Edge preservation versus group preservation:** Edge counts can inflate nominal information; group counts can erase complementary or jointly necessary support.
- **Procedure-bounded adequacy versus completeness:** Deep pools can be robust for a stated decision while remaining incomplete and expensive.
- **Coverage–quality improvement versus ecological loss:** Higher quality on retained items can be achieved by rejecting central workload.
- **Strict versus relaxed identity:** Strict matching exposes instability; relaxed matching can preserve substantive overlap or conceal scope changes.
- **Maximal versus parsimonious clustering:** Design-justified dependence structures can be necessary and simultaneously unsupported by available data.
- **Probability weighting versus substantive importance:** Sampling weights recover a frame-defined estimand under known inclusion probabilities; importance weights encode a separate evaluative rubric.
- **Ordinary observability versus checkpoint observability:** Instrumentation improves records while altering behavior and resources.
- **Factual-stratum feasibility versus whole-role mismatch:** A local factual audit may succeed while the handoff remains unsuitable as a whole-role apparatus.
- **Transparent exclusions versus valid retained object:** Complete bookkeeping can coexist with severe role and qualification loss.
- **Added apparatus quality versus fixed-resource interpretation:** Better candidate pools, coding, verification, and challenge tests consume the resources under study.
- **Method volume versus independence:** Multiple methods address different components and include family overlap; agreement does not multiply evidential independence.

## Remaining Hypotheses

- **C0006-ALT-01 — Apparatus-created measurability:** Candidate generation, source access, atomicity, filtering, repeated coding, adjudication, challenge testing, and checkpoints may materially create the measurable core.
- **C0006-ALT-02 — Construct and metric underdetermination:** Plausible atomicity, grouping, boundary, weighting, metric, and cluster choices may reverse a feasibility conclusion.
- **C0006-ALT-03 — Factual-stratum sparse-reuse null:** Direct support may be locally measurable but too peripheral for whole-role relevance.
- **Nontrivial eligible-core hypothesis (`C0006-EXP-01`):** A sufficiently large, qualified, diverse factual core may exist; no evidence yet.
- **Reliable-but-trivial residue (`C0006-EXP-02`):** High reliability may require exclusions that destroy coverage or role relevance.
- **Qualification-loss hypothesis (`C0006-EXP-05`):** Narrow eligibility may selectively remove scope- and limitation-bearing material.
- **Clustered-sparsity hypothesis (`C0006-EXP-06`):** Nominal pairs may collapse under target-relevant source, claim, output, or task dependence.
- **Deep-curation bounded-universe hypothesis:** Diverse candidate generation and challenge testing may produce a locally adequate but expensive universe.
- **Checkpoint-contamination hypothesis:** New candidate, selection, or rationale records may improve observability while changing the handoff.
- **Robust-local-construct hypothesis:** A sharply scoped estimand may remain stable across predeclared plausible mappings, metrics, and cluster sensitivities despite global underdetermination.

# Alternative Theorist — C0006

## Strongest Current Interpretation

The strongest current interpretation is a **linked-denominator and conditional-gate account**.

Under this interpretation, the External Evidence Researcher → Alternative Theorist handoff cannot be represented by one natural denominator hierarchy. Task instances, role outputs, sections, role-function units, upstream factual units, evidence units, candidate-relation sets, relation edges, bounded relation groups, eligible primary units, downstream units, lineage links, and clusters are distinct and often non-nested objects. A defensible apparatus should therefore preserve these objects in a linked lattice or multi-table schema, define every candidate universe by its generation procedure, state every coverage quantity as a named numerator over a named denominator, preserve both edge-level and grouped-relation views, and distinguish ordinary-artifact observability from checkpoint-created observability.

This interpretation is supported internally by the C0006 Critic’s corrections and the denominator/observability map in `03_internal_evidence.md`. It is supported externally by `C0006-EXT-01` through `C0006-EXT-09`: crossed and multiple-membership methods support non-nested mappings; structured-annotation methods support edges, sets, groups, and boundary-sensitive alignment; TREC pooling supports procedure-bounded candidate universes; selective prediction supports joint quality–coverage reporting; pseudo-replication and mixed-model sources support dependence-sensitive inference; sampling methods support prospective strata only when a frame exists; decision-linked methods support thresholds tied to a later action; exclusion reporting keeps losses visible; and rationale evidence weakens causal interpretations of checkpoint reports.

The interpretation remains strictly methodological. It does not establish that the target handoff contains a nontrivial direct-support core, that any retained core represents the Alternative Theorist’s workload, that a candidate universe is adequate, or that any numeric gate is justified. The gate remains conditional on a one-sentence target decision specifying the later experiment, the claim it could support, the population or stratum covered, the primary inferential unit, the sampling frame, the role-relevance rubric, the resource rule, and the cost of false passage and false failure.

The strongest version of this interpretation therefore says: **build the denominator map now; specify a numerical gate only after the downstream decision and construct are frozen.** It does not say that the linked lattice will itself resolve representativeness, identity, grouping, role relevance, or feasibility.

## Alternative Theory 1

### C0006-ALT-01 — Apparatus-Created Measurability Theory

- **Core claim:** The measurable direct-support core may be produced substantially by the apparatus—candidate generation, atomic splitting, eligibility filtering, source access, checkpoint reporting, verification, adjudication, challenge testing, and repeated coding—rather than discovered as a stable property of the ordinary handoff.
- **Evidence status:** Evidence-backed mechanism alternative; its magnitude in B001 is untested.

### Mechanism

The ordinary handoff does not expose a complete candidate-relation universe, stable atomic units, adjudicable evidence boundaries, bounded relation groups, or recorded selection. The proposed apparatus creates these objects through offline annotation or new reporting procedures. Writer-supplied links, coder-proposed links, links within a bounded evidence set, and checkpoint-reported candidates are different generated universes. Atomicity rules change claim counts; grouping rules change relation counts; exclusion rules change the retained distribution; full-source access changes adjudicability; repeated judgments and adjudication change the apparent reference standard; and selected-subset or rationale checkpoints change the records available while potentially changing agent behavior.

A later high-reliability, nontrivial-looking core could therefore be real relative to the constructed apparatus but not naturally present in the ordinary External Evidence Researcher → Alternative Theorist transition. The apparatus would be an active measurement-and-selection pipeline. Its success would show that a curated relation object can be created and audited, not that the uninstrumented handoff already contains the same measurable object.

This theory also explains why expensive procedural universes can become adequate. `C0006-EXT-14` shows that bounded pools may be robust after deep, diverse construction and later challenge testing, while shallow pools may be biased. Transferred cautiously, B001 could obtain a useful candidate universe through costly curation, source review, multiple generators, and challenge sets. The adequacy would be attributable partly to those resources and procedures, not to the denominator representation alone.

### Evidence it explains

- `C0006-EXT-03` shows that practical candidate universes are procedure-bounded rather than latent exhaustive sets.
- `C0006-EXT-04` and `C0006-EXT-13` show that quality can improve as items are rejected, without establishing that the retained subset represents the discarded workload.
- `C0006-EXT-14` shows that procedural incompleteness can be tolerable only under depth, diversity, new judgments, and challenge testing.
- `C0006-EXT-17` shows that transparent exclusions and rich serialization do not validate the retained residue.
- `C0006-EXT-09` and `C0006-EXT-16` show that checkpoints and reports are observable records, not faithful evidence of latent causal dependence.
- The Internal Evidence Researcher’s observability map explicitly classifies eligible units, coder-proposed candidates, relation groups, lineage links, clusters, and exclusions as annotation-created, and checkpoint-reported candidates as intervention-created.
- The fixed-resource section of `04_external_evidence.md` records added schema design, retrieval, candidate generation, grouping, calibration, repeated judgment, adjudication, challenge testing, statistical modeling, and checkpoint cost.

### Evidence it struggles with

- Ordinary artifacts may already contain enough explicit claims, evidence locators, source links, and observable downstream correspondence to support a useful offline audit without new agent behavior. That would weaken a strong claim that measurability is mostly checkpoint-created.
- `C0006-EXT-01` through `C0006-EXT-03` support representational methods that can preserve pre-existing complex structure rather than manufacture it. A linked schema may reveal structure already present but previously dispersed.
- Deep procedural pools can be empirically robust. If several independently generated candidate universes converge on the same retained relations and later challenge procedures add few new central relations, the constructed universe may be a credible approximation for a stated local decision.
- This theory does not imply that apparatus-created objects are invalid. Measurement routinely requires operational construction. The issue is interpretation and resource attribution, not automatic rejection.

### Predictions

1. The retained core will vary materially across writer-supplied, coder-proposed, bounded-source, and checkpoint-reported candidate universes even when applied to the same tasks.
2. More source access, more candidate generators, repeated sampling, adjudication, and challenge testing will increase measured coverage or stability, but also increase resource use.
3. Atomicity, eligibility, and grouping restrictions will produce a visible coverage–quality frontier: stricter rules will raise agreement while reducing qualification, many-to-many, or role-function coverage.
4. A checkpoint-enabled audit will show more explicit lineage records than an ordinary-artifact audit, but the checkpoint condition may also change selection, deliberation, output composition, or cost.
5. Apparent apparatus benefit will shrink when full construction, retrieval, candidate generation, annotation, verification, adjudication, challenge, and evaluation resources are charged.
6. Repeated sampling or multiple candidate generators will improve candidate-universe robustness more than merely splitting the same material into more nominal pairs.

## Alternative Theory 2

### C0006-ALT-02 — Construct and Metric Underdetermination Theory

- **Core claim:** Pass or failure may be driven less by an underlying amount of measurable direct-support work than by unresolved choices about object identity, atomicity, grouping, alignment, distance, weighting, clustering, and the target population. The denominator lattice can prevent category errors but cannot identify a uniquely correct construct.
- **Evidence status:** Strongly evidence-backed methodological alternative; B001-specific effect size is untested.

### Mechanism

The proposed objects do not arrive with a unique ontology. One factual statement can be split into several claims or retained as a qualified compound. Several passages can be duplicate, substitutable, complementary, or jointly necessary. A downstream unit can paraphrase, merge, narrow, generalize, contradict, or retype upstream material. Relation groups can be represented as sets, aligned entities, link structures, or boundary-sensitive units. Each representation changes what counts as an item, match, error, exclusion, cluster, and covered unit.

The external methods do not converge on one neutral solution. MASI grades set overlap; gamma aligns variable units under a chosen dissimilarity; CEAF aligns entity clusters; LEA scores links with entity weighting. Exact and relaxed boundaries can produce different reliability profiles. Crossed or mixed models require a declared generalization target, while maximal and parsimonious specifications can conflict. Weighted coverage requires a sampling frame or a separate substantive rubric. A linked lattice makes these choices explicit, but the feasibility conclusion can still reverse under plausible, prospectively defensible definitions.

Under this account, the central threat is not merely researcher misconduct or post hoc selection. Even honestly frozen alternatives can encode different legitimate constructs. A pass under one construct may coexist with failure under another because the constructs answer different questions. There may be no single scalar “true coverage” of the handoff.

### Evidence it explains

- `C0006-EXT-02` and `C0006-EXT-12` show that set, boundary, entity, and link metrics encode different theories of structure and error.
- `C0006-EXT-11` states that a linked lattice remains descriptive until the sampling and inferential model is fixed.
- `C0006-EXT-15` shows that cluster modeling has no universal specification and can fail through either overcomplexity or under-modeling.
- `C0006-EXT-16` shows that weighting and stratification cannot recover a population without a frame and known inclusion design.
- The C0006 Critic establishes that the proposed hierarchy contains non-nested objects, pair reduction can erase legitimate multiplicity, role importance is evaluative, denominator and codebook cannot be frozen independently, and an effective count requires an inferential target.
- The Internal Evidence Researcher’s allowed-ratio table shows that validity depends on shared atomicity rules, named generation universes, mapping direction, and matching numerator/denominator objects.
- The current accepted state already rejects one pooled agreement statistic, scalar completeness, lexical similarity as identity, and one-to-one units as generally representative.

### Evidence it struggles with

- The existence of multiple defensible constructs does not imply that no decision can be made. A one-sentence target decision can select the construct that is relevant to a particular later experiment.
- Some choices may prove empirically robust. If feasibility conclusions remain stable across predeclared strict/relaxed boundaries, edge/group views, reasonable role rubrics, and cluster sensitivities, metric underdetermination would be reduced.
- Certain distinctions are not merely conventional. For example, causal dependence is outside the observable endpoint, and candidate universes generated by different procedures are factually different objects. The theory should not blur hard observational boundaries into arbitrary metric choice.
- A local construct can be valid even if it is not unique or globally representative. The theory weakens broad claims more than a narrowly scoped audit claim.

### Predictions

1. Feasibility conclusions will change under plausible predeclared alternatives for atomicity, duplicate/complementary support, edge-versus-group scoring, strict-versus-relaxed boundaries, and role-relevance weighting.
2. Different structured metrics will rank the same annotation sets differently, especially for split/merge, partial overlap, and many-to-many cases.
3. A high pair-level reliability result may coexist with lower group-level or output-level coverage, without either result being mathematically erroneous.
4. Cluster adjustment will vary by the intended population: source-level, claim-family-level, output-level, and task-level analyses will support different uncertainty statements.
5. Agreement on the denominator schema between two designers will not guarantee construct validity; disagreement will identify underdefined rules, while convergence will remain provisional.
6. Once the later decision target is sharply narrowed, metric sensitivity should decrease for the specific estimand even if broader workload representativeness remains unresolved.

## Additional Alternative or Null Explanation

### C0006-ALT-03 — Factual-Stratum Sparse-Reuse Null

- **Core claim:** The Alternative Theorist handoff may contain too little role-relevant, directly supportable factual incorporation to justify the selected direct-support experiment, even after a correct denominator system is built. This can be a legitimate property of the role rather than an apparatus failure.
- **Evidence status:** Evidence-consistent null hypothesis; no B001 prevalence data establish it.

The Alternative Theorist is authorized to generate hypotheses, speculation, objections, hybrids, and unresolved questions. Much of its useful work may be recombination, contrast construction, mechanism proposal, or qualification rather than direct reuse of upstream factual claim–evidence pairs. A clean factual stratum could exist but remain peripheral to the role’s central function. Conversely, direct support could be common in factual assertions but sparse across the complete workload.

This null explains why dividing eligible direct-support units by all role units would mechanically produce low coverage, while excluding nonfactual work would conceal role mismatch. It supports the Critic’s two-part reporting rule: direct-support coverage should use an eligible factual-content denominator, while role relevance should separately report the size and importance of that factual stratum within the whole workload.

It can explain a high-reliability/low-role-coverage result without invoking defective coding. It can also explain why a factual-stratum pilot might still be locally useful if the later claim is explicitly limited to factual incorporation. It cannot explain a result in which eligible factual units are both common and central across prospectively defined role functions, qualifications, tasks, and source structures.

**Predictions:**

1. The factual/assertive stratum will be a minority of role-function units or will receive low blinded role-importance ratings relative to mechanism, objection, hybrid, and unresolved-question work.
2. Eligible direct-support coverage may be respectable within the factual stratum but low at the output, role-function, or qualification-rich workload level.
3. Increasing annotation resources or changing metrics will not substantially increase the fraction of the role that is properly within the direct-support estimand.
4. A pilot explicitly limited to factual assertions may pass, while any claim about the whole Alternative Theorist role will fail the predeclared role-relevance condition.
5. Low factual reuse will not correlate necessarily with low alternative coverage, usefulness, epistemic typing, or task completion.

### C0006-ALT-04 — Minimal Descriptive Null

A still more minimal explanation is that C0006 has not yet produced evidence about handoff prevalence, coverage, role relevance, or feasibility at all. The available evidence supports only a better bookkeeping and design language. Under this null, apparently competing stories about a nontrivial core, trivial residue, cluster collapse, qualification loss, or role mismatch remain unconstrained because no authorized development corpus, audit corpus, counts, coder data, or decision target exists.

This minimal null is strongly supported by the Internal Evidence Gaps and `C0006-EXT-10` through `C0006-EXT-17`. It predicts that any confident ranking of mechanisms before an executed prospective audit will be driven by assumptions rather than branch-local observations.

## Hybrid Explanations

The alternatives are not mutually exclusive. Several hybrids are especially plausible.

### Small core + apparatus creation + metric sensitivity

A genuine factual direct-support core may exist, but its measured size may depend on candidate-generation depth, source access, atomicity, filtering, and grouping. It may pass under a local construct while remaining sensitive to relaxed boundaries or cluster definitions. This hybrid predicts stable central relations alongside a large ambiguous fringe.

### Deep curation + bounded-universe adequacy + high resource cost

A procedure-bounded universe may become adequate for one stated audit after multiple candidate generators, full-source review, adjudication, and challenge testing. The result could be methodologically useful but expensive. It would not show that the ordinary handoff is naturally measurable or that role separation supplies the benefit.

### Factual-stratum feasibility + whole-role mismatch

The direct-support apparatus may be reliable and useful for factual assertions while failing to represent hypotheses, objections, hybrids, and unresolved questions. This is not a contradiction if the later decision and claim are explicitly restricted to the factual stratum. It becomes misleading only if generalized to the full role.

### Qualification loss + pair/group instability

Qualification-rich claims may be split into atomic units that appear reliable at the edge level while losing the scope relations that make them epistemically safe. Group-level or qualification-preservation measures may then show poorer performance. Both edge accuracy and ecological loss could be real.

### Checkpoint observability + behavior change

A candidate or selected-subset report may improve lineage observability and coder agreement while changing attention, deliberation, stopping, or output form. The checkpoint may be useful as a common intervention in a later experiment, but its results cannot be generalized to the ordinary uninstrumented handoff without a separate comparison.

## False Dichotomies or Framing Problems

1. **Hierarchy versus no structure:** The choice is not between one clean hierarchy and disorder. A linked lattice can preserve structure without pretending that all objects are nested.

2. **Pair inflation versus legitimate multiplicity:** Multiple edges can be artificial duplication, repeated citation, substitutable evidence, complementary evidence, or genuinely distinct dependencies. Edge and group counts answer different questions.

3. **Reliable core versus useless residue:** A small reliable core can be useful for a narrowly scoped factual experiment and still be unrepresentative of the full role.

4. **Natural discovery versus total manufacture:** Apparatus construction can reveal real regularities while also changing the measurable object. The relevant question is which objects existed in ordinary artifacts, which were produced by offline annotation, and which required new agent behavior.

5. **Complete candidate universe versus invalid evaluation:** No practical universe may be complete. A procedure-bounded universe can be adequate for a stated decision if its generation process, depth, diversity, omissions, and challenge tests are explicit.

6. **Strict versus relaxed matching:** Strict boundaries expose instability; relaxed matching can preserve substantive overlap or conceal scope changes. Both should be reported with a predeclared interpretation.

7. **Unweighted versus weighted coverage:** Unweighted counts show item prevalence; sampling weights recover a frame-defined population under known inclusion probabilities; substantive weights encode a role-value rubric. These are distinct quantities, not interchangeable corrections.

8. **More data versus more independent evidence:** More nominal pairs, repeated citations, or multiple edges from one passage do not necessarily add independent information. Conversely, grouping all related edges may erase real diversity.

9. **More compute versus role separation:** Apparent success may result from more source access, longer context, more candidate generators, repeated sampling, adjudication, challenge testing, stronger prompts, or selection opportunities. None of the C0006 sources isolates role separation or fresh invocation.

10. **Better prompts versus better denominator ontology:** A stronger candidate-reporting or relation-seeking prompt can create more explicit links and cleaner artifacts. That can improve observability without proving that the ontology or role architecture improves epistemic performance.

11. **Pass/fail versus scientific adequacy:** A numerical gate encodes a downstream decision and error costs. Passing one gate does not establish general representativeness, and failing one gate does not prove that no other prospectively defined relation or handoff is measurable.

12. **Transparent reporting versus valid construct:** An exhaustive exclusion ledger and complete schema are necessary safeguards, but they do not establish that exclusions are acceptable or that the retained object is central.

13. **Null versus treatment failure:** A low measured core can reflect genuine sparse factual work, weak candidate generation, restrictive eligibility, identity failure, inadequate source access, cluster concentration, metric choice, or checkpoint mismatch. These require checkpoint-specific observations.

14. **Evidence quantity versus independence:** Twenty sources provide component-method evidence, not twenty independent confirmations of one denominator system. Method-family overlap and transfer by analogy limit cumulative evidential weight.

## Discriminating Tests

### 1. Ordinary-artifact versus apparatus-created object inventory

For one development corpus, mark every denominator object and field as directly present in ordinary artifacts, produced by offline annotation, or created by new agent reporting/retrieval/checkpoints. Compare coverage, identity stability, resource use, and output composition across the ordinary-artifact and instrumented versions.

- **Supports apparatus-created measurability** if most eligible units, candidate links, or lineage records exist only after intervention-created reporting and the intervention changes output behavior or cost.
- **Weakens it** if ordinary artifacts support nearly the same central relation set and instrumented procedures mainly improve formatting.

### 2. Candidate-universe challenge test

Freeze at least two independently generated candidate universes on development data, such as writer-supplied links and coder-proposed links under fixed source access. After freezing the primary universe, challenge it with a new generator or expanded source review.

- **Supports bounded-universe adequacy** if the challenge adds few central eligible relations and does not materially change coverage or decisions.
- **Supports candidate-generation dependence** if new procedures add many central relations or reverse feasibility conclusions.

### 3. Atomicity and grouping factorial

Apply a small predeclared set of plausible atomicity and grouping rules: coarse versus fine factual units; edge-level versus bounded-group-level scoring; duplicate, substitutable, and complementary support coded separately.

- **Supports construct underdetermination** if pass/fail reverses across plausible rules.
- **Weakens it** if the same local conclusion holds across rules and the remaining differences are well bounded.

### 4. Metric and boundary robustness panel

Report strict and relaxed span matching, set overlap, link-aware, and group-alignment metrics where applicable. Predeclare which metric is primary for the target decision and which are sensitivity analyses.

- **Supports metric-induced feasibility** if the apparatus passes only under permissive overlap, one grouping alignment, or one role weighting.
- **Supports a stable local construct** if conclusions remain consistent across the predeclared panel.

### 5. Factual-stratum versus whole-role decomposition

Freeze a multilabel role-function taxonomy blind to eligibility and audit outcomes. Report: factual-stratum share; eligible factual coverage; downstream factual-unit incorporation coverage; qualification coverage; and role-function coverage for hypotheses, objections, hybrids, and unresolved questions.

- **Supports the sparse-reuse null** if factual work is small or peripheral even when internally measurable.
- **Supports factual-stratum concentration** if a stable, substantively important factual stratum exists but other role functions remain outside scope.
- **Weakens role-mismatch claims** if eligible units are central across several prospectively defined role functions.

### 6. Qualification-loss audit

Before eligibility filtering, identify qualification-bearing factual units. Track whether they are retained, excluded, split, or observably weakened downstream. Compare retained and excluded units by qualification burden and blinded importance.

- **Supports coverage-failure theory** if high reliability is achieved by disproportionately excluding or simplifying qualification-bearing central units.
- **Weakens it** if qualifications are retained and reliably preserved at similar rates to unqualified claims.

### 7. Cluster-generalization test

Freeze the later generalization target, then declare the primary cluster unit accordingly. Report sensitivity to source, passage, claim-family, output, task, and annotator clustering without selecting among them after audit.

- **Supports clustered-sparsity** if nominal prevalence or precision collapses at the target-relevant cluster level.
- **Supports a diverse core** if adequate counts and uncertainty remain under the relevant cluster structure.

### 8. Resource-ablation and reallocation comparison

On development data, compare candidate generation and adjudication under predeclared resource levels: one generator versus repeated sampling; locator-only versus full-source access; single coding versus independent duplicate coding; no challenge set versus challenge testing. Record all model, human, retrieval, and evaluation costs.

- **Supports resource-privilege theory** if apparent coverage or reliability improves mainly with added resources.
- **Weakens it** if the same local result appears under a minimal resource configuration.
- This test must not be interpreted as role-separation evidence unless role boundaries are isolated from all added opportunities.

### 9. Prompt-substitution comparison

Hold the denominator and candidate universe fixed while comparing ordinary instructions with a stronger relation-reporting prompt, without adding typed fields in one condition.

- **Supports prompt-driven observability** if stronger instructions recover most explicit links or lineage records.
- **Supports representation-specific effects only weakly** if typed structure adds stable value after approximately equivalent prompt content and geometry controls.

### 10. Decision-rule rehearsal with fixed utilities

Before the held-out audit, write a one-sentence target decision and map high-reliability/low-coverage, moderate-reliability/high-coverage, cluster-specific passage, qualification failure, checkpoint dependence, and excessive-cost outcomes to predetermined actions.

- This does not test the apparatus itself.
- It discriminates a genuine decision-linked gate from a reporting threshold chosen to rescue a preferred direction.

### 11. Reporting-bias audit

Require every sensitivity result, excluded stratum, failed metric, unsuccessful candidate generator, and nonconvergent cluster model to be reported. Freeze which results are primary before audit.

- **Supports reporting-bias concerns** if the favorable conclusion depends on omitting failed definitions, low-coverage strata, or expensive procedures.
- **Weakens them** if the conclusion survives complete reporting and predeclared priorities.

## Remaining Uncertainty

- The later decision target is not frozen. It remains unclear whether a pass would authorize an experiment about the full Alternative Theorist role, a factual stratum, or a narrower direct-support incorporation contrast.
- No authorized development corpus, untouched audit corpus, target population, sampling frame, or inclusion-probability design exists.
- No branch-local prevalence, retained-coverage, role-relevance, qualification-loss, exclusion-composition, reliability, cluster-diversity, or resource result exists.
- The multilabel role-function taxonomy and mixed-unit splitting rules are unvalidated.
- Candidate-relation universes remain procedure-dependent and no challenge protocol is fixed.
- Duplicate, repeated, substitutable, complementary, jointly necessary, split, merge, overlapping, and many-to-many support semantics are unresolved.
- No role-relevance or substantive-importance rubric is validated or protected fully from evaluator preference.
- The primary inferential and cluster unit cannot be selected until the population and claim scope are fixed.
- No external method validates a representative factual residue for a mixed factual/nonfactual Alternative Theorist workload.
- No numeric reliability, coverage, role-relevance, diversity, precision, or cost threshold is justified.
- Checkpoint effects on behavior and resources remain unestimated.
- Apparatus construction, longer source context, repeated sampling, stronger prompts, selection, verification, adjudication, and reporting choices remain live explanations for any future apparent benefit.
- Nothing in C0006 isolates role separation, fresh invocation, or the seven-agent architecture.

## Handoff to Integrator

Preserve the main methodological conclusion narrowly: C0006 supports a linked denominator-and-observability system with explicit object mappings, procedure-bounded candidate universes, allowed numerator/denominator ratios, parallel edge and group views, quality reported with retained coverage and exclusions, and dependence-sensitive uncertainty. It supports a denominator-map deliverable now and only a conditional gate later.

Do not accept a numerical coverage or role-relevance gate, a representative factual core, a complete candidate universe, a preferred grouping or metric, a target-handoff prevalence estimate, or a role-separation effect. The one-sentence target decision, population, sampling frame, primary inferential unit, codebook, role-relevance rubric, resource rule, and false-passage consequences remain prerequisites.

Carry forward at least three live alternatives: `C0006-ALT-01` apparatus-created measurability, `C0006-ALT-02` construct and metric underdetermination, and `C0006-ALT-03` the factual-stratum sparse-reuse null. Treat deep curation, bounded-universe adequacy, factual-stratum feasibility, qualification loss, checkpoint contamination, and resource privilege as potentially interacting mechanisms rather than exclusive theories.

The most informative next branch step is not another broad literature search. It is to freeze the target decision and prospective object system, authorize a development corpus and untouched audit corpus, and execute discriminating analyses that expose whether the measurable core is ordinary or apparatus-created, robust or metric-dependent, role-central or role-peripheral, diverse or cluster-concentrated, and affordable or resource-enabled. Any later positive result must remain local to the tested task frame, role stratum, candidate procedure, codebook, metric panel, cluster model, checkpoint, and resource rule.

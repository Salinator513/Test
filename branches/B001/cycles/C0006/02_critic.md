# Critic — C0006

## Fair Reconstruction of the Explorer’s Direction

The Explorer proposes a narrow apparatus-design frontier rather than an effectiveness study. The intended task is to define how the full External Evidence Researcher → Alternative Theorist handoff workload should be counted, how clean one-claim/one-evidence direct-support units should be related to that larger workload, and what retained-coverage and role-relevance conditions would prevent a reliable but trivial subset from being declared feasible.

The proposal is trying to preserve several distinctions already accepted by the branch: raw pairs are not independent units; many-to-many relations should not be silently expanded into inflated pair counts; reliability must be interpreted jointly with retained coverage and exclusions; the Alternative Theorist performs factual and nonfactual functions; qualification-rich material can be lost by narrow direct-support eligibility; and some denominators may be visible only after a behavior-changing checkpoint.

The intended frontier is therefore not “find a denominator that produces a favorable prevalence.” It is “construct an auditable denominator system that shows what is retained, excluded, duplicated, clustered, and role relevant before any held-out feasibility result is interpreted.” That is a legitimate and timely direction.

## What Is Strong or Worth Preserving

The frontier directly follows the accepted C0005 requirement that a reliable one-claim/one-evidence residue must not conceal low retained coverage, low role relevance, unstable identity, or high resource dependence. It does not claim that the selected handoff is measurable, that direct-support relations improve performance, or that role separation is beneficial.

The Explorer correctly treats pair count, relation-group count, source clustering, claim clustering, downstream-output coverage, qualification coverage, and role-function coverage as potentially different quantities. The warnings about pair multiplication, pseudo-replication, post hoc favorable strata, checkpoint contamination, and a reliability–coverage tradeoff are already adequate and should be retained.

The proposed development-only sensitivity analyses are useful provided they are used to expose instability and freeze rules, not to select whichever definition yields the strongest feasibility result. The requirement for an exclusion ledger is especially valuable because it makes ecological loss visible rather than treating excluded material as if it never belonged to the workload.

## Fatal Problems

### 1. The gate has no fixed decision target

A pass/fail retained-coverage or role-relevance gate cannot be specified until the branch states what later decision the gate is meant to authorize. The Explorer alternates among at least three possible targets: feasibility for the entire Alternative Theorist role, feasibility for its factual stratum, and feasibility for a narrower direct-support incorporation experiment. Those targets require different denominators, weights, sampling frames, and acceptable exclusion losses.

Without a fixed target claim and unit of generalization, “nontrivial,” “central,” “role relevant,” and “enough to justify a later experiment” are not falsifiable. Numeric thresholds chosen before that decision would be arbitrary; thresholds chosen after observing development results would be circular. The researchers may investigate denominator methods now, but they must not present a numerical gate as resolved until the intended later experiment and claim scope are frozen.

### 2. One proposed role-relevance measure violates the accepted observational boundary

C0006-EXP-01 proposes measuring “the share of final outputs whose substantive factual content depends on eligible units.” Dependence is a causal or counterfactual claim that ordinary artifacts, citations, lineage, and recorded selection cannot establish. The accepted branch state permits observable downstream relation-preserving incorporation, not latent dependence or causal use.

This measure must be replaced with observable quantities such as the share of output units containing eligible factual assertions, the share observably linked to eligible upstream support, qualification-preservation coverage, or blinded ratings of the substantive function of those output units. No denominator or gate may rely on inferred causal dependence.

## Material Problems

### 1. The proposed “hierarchy” contains non-nested objects

Corpus items, role outputs, output sections, upstream claim clusters, evidence clusters, bounded relation groups, eligible primary units, and downstream incorporation units do not form one clean hierarchy. A downstream unit may merge several upstream claims; one claim may connect to several passages; one passage may support multiple claims; and a relation group can cross output sections or sources. Treating these as nested levels risks invalid ratios and false reconciliation.

The apparatus should use a denominator lattice or linked multi-table schema, with explicit mappings among object types and a declared rule for which numerator may be divided by which denominator. Edge counts, grouped-relation counts, unique-claim counts, source-cluster counts, and downstream-output coverage should be reported in parallel rather than collapsed into one ladder.

### 2. “Full handoff workload” is ambiguous

The Alternative Theorist is authorized to produce factual assertions, source interpretations, derived conclusions, hypotheses, speculation, objections, hybrids, and unresolved questions. Some units may contain more than one epistemic function. The full workload is therefore not equivalent to all sentences, all claims, all candidate evidence relations, or all output sections.

The branch must freeze a workload taxonomy and sampling frame before coverage is computed. Direct-support coverage should use an eligible factual-content denominator, while role relevance should separately report how large and important that factual-content stratum is within the full role workload. Dividing eligible relations by all nonfactual role units would mechanically create low coverage; excluding nonfactual work entirely would conceal role mismatch.

### 3. The candidate-relation denominator is not naturally exhaustive

A denominator of “candidate relations” assumes a recoverable universe of possible claim–evidence links. Ordinary artifacts expose only written claims, cited or supplied evidence, and possibly recorded selections. Additional plausible links may require full-source review, coder generation, retrieval, or a new checkpoint. There is no naturally observable complete set of all true or possible relations.

The protocol must name each candidate universe precisely, such as writer-supplied links, coder-proposed links under fixed source access, all links within a bounded evidence set, or checkpoint-reported candidates. These universes cannot be treated as interchangeable, and none should be labeled the full latent relation denominator.

### 4. Pair reduction can hide real multiplicity as well as remove inflation

Multiple edges are not automatically duplication. Several passages may provide distinct support components, one passage may independently support several factual claims, and split claims may represent real qualification or scope differences. Grouping everything into one bounded relation object could undercount substantive dependencies just as pairwise expansion can overcount them.

The researchers need operational criteria for duplicate edges, complementary support, substitutable support, repeated citation, shared passage, split/merge identity, and genuinely distinct relation groups. Both edge-level and group-level results should remain visible. The direction should not presume that lower grouped counts are more truthful.

### 5. Role relevance and importance weighting remain underdefined

“Central rather than incidental,” “substantive output importance,” and “role relevance” are evaluative judgments, not self-evident properties. A rubric can become circular if importance is assigned after eligibility, reliability, or treatment outcomes are known, or if factual units are rated more important merely because the proposed apparatus can measure them.

The role-relevance rubric must be frozen on development data, blind to eligibility and audit outcomes where practicable, and based on observable role functions or task obligations. Inter-rater uncertainty and disagreement must be reported. Weighted and unweighted coverage should both remain visible; weighting must not replace item counts.

### 6. Several candidate hypotheses overlap rather than compete

The nontrivial-core, factual-stratum concentration, qualification-loss, clustered-sparsity, and reliable-but-trivial hypotheses can all be true at once. They are useful diagnostic possibilities, but they are not mutually exclusive hypotheses that one research question will discriminate among.

The researchers should treat them as dimensions of a result profile: eligible prevalence, retained workload coverage, role-function coverage, qualification coverage, cluster diversity, and cost. The handoff should not imply that evidence for one rejects the others.

### 7. The order “denominator first, codebook later” is only partly possible

Conceptual denominator objects can be mapped before full codebook validation, but operational counts depend on atomicity, identity, direct-support eligibility, evidence boundaries, split/merge rules, and many-to-many coding. Those are codebook choices. A denominator cannot be frozen independently of the rules that create its units.

The correction is to co-develop a minimal unit-and-relation codebook with the denominator schema on development material, then freeze both before held-out audit. The branch should not claim that denominator validation has priority over or independence from codebook validation.

### 8. Cluster-adjusted “effective counts” require a declared inferential target

Source, passage, claim-family, output-section, and task clustering are different dependence structures. Reporting all of them as sensitivity analyses is useful, but an “effective sample size” is not meaningful without a sampling model and a stated population or generalization target. Arbitrarily choosing the smallest count would be conservative but not necessarily valid; choosing the largest would be misleading.

The primary cluster unit must follow the intended later inference. Other clusterings should be reported as sensitivity analyses, not alternate answers from which a favorable result can be selected.

### 9. External research could repeat C0005 rather than advance the frontier

The branch already has substantial adjacent evidence on direct-support annotation, span boundaries, reliability limits, qualification, filtering, and development/audit separation. A broad search for more annotation or fact-verification methods would duplicate prior work.

External research should be restricted to methods that directly address non-nested denominator systems, grouped or many-to-many relation units, selective coverage–quality reporting, clustered dependence, rare-stratum sampling, exclusion accounting, weighted content coverage, and decision-linked feasibility thresholds. General evidence-verification sources should be included only if they add a missing denominator method.

## Minor Problems

“Qualification density,” “source diversity,” “genuinely distinct relation groups,” “central content,” and “substantive importance” need explicit operational definitions.

The proposed top-level sequence omits a separately identified task or prompt instance between corpus item and role output. One task can generate multiple outputs or variants, and one output can contain several sections and role functions.

The false-passage simulation is a useful policy consistency check, but it does not test the substantive hypotheses. It should be labeled a decision-rule rehearsal.

Two independently drafted denominator systems can reveal ambiguity, but agreement between two drafts does not validate the construct. Divergence is diagnostic; convergence is only provisional.

## Hidden Assumptions

- The selected corpus can represent the intended future population of Alternative Theorist work.
- Ordinary outputs contain stable enough boundaries to support output-unit and role-function coding.
- Factual assertions can be separated reliably from source interpretation, derived conclusion, hypothesis, speculation, objection, and unresolved question.
- Role-function categories are sufficiently exclusive or can support multilabel coding.
- Importance can be judged prospectively and independently of apparatus success.
- A bounded many-to-many relation group can be defined reproducibly without erasing distinct dependencies.
- Exclusion reasons can be assigned exhaustively and reliably.
- Source, passage, claim, and output clustering correspond to meaningful dependence rather than merely shared labels.
- Direct-support factual work is important enough within the Alternative Theorist role to justify the selected handoff.
- A coverage gate can be portable across task strata, models, source types, and output lengths.
- Added annotation or checkpoint procedures can be separately costed and do not silently become part of the treatment.

## Scope and Definition Problems

The frontier must distinguish at least four scopes: the sampled task corpus, the complete Alternative Theorist outputs for those tasks, the factual or assertive subset of those outputs, and the eligible direct-support relation subset. “Coverage” without naming one of these denominators is uninterpretable.

The apparatus must also distinguish an upstream claim unit, an evidence unit, a relation edge, a bounded relation group, a downstream output unit, and an observable lineage link. These are related but not interchangeable units.

“Role relevance” should mean relevance to a predeclared role function or task obligation, not expected treatment benefit. “Representativeness” should name the dimensions represented: role function, task stratum, qualification burden, source count, synthesis complexity, output position, or other frozen dimensions. No single scalar should be treated as complete representativeness.

“Natural observability” should mean recoverable from ordinary authorized artifacts plus annotation that does not request new agent behavior. Anything requiring candidate reports, selection declarations, rationales, or new retrieval should be labeled checkpoint-created observability.

## Risks of Misleading Research

A high reliability result could be paired with a denominator chosen after exclusions, producing an apparently strong but trivial residue.

A high item-count coverage result could conceal low downstream-output coverage, low qualification coverage, low source diversity, or concentration in incidental factual remarks.

A low grouped-relation count could be presented as proof of pair inflation even when multiple edges encode distinct support components.

Importance weighting could be used to rescue a sparse subset if weights are assigned after eligibility or with knowledge of apparatus performance.

A factual-stratum success could be generalized to the entire Alternative Theorist role despite sparse mechanism, objection, hybrid, and unresolved-question coverage.

Checkpoint-enabled denominators could be described as properties of the ordinary handoff even though the checkpoint changes attention, deliberation, cost, and available records.

External methods from selective prediction, annotation, or clustered sampling could be transferred as if they validated B001 thresholds, despite different tasks, objects, coders, costs, and decision consequences.

Multiple sensitivity analyses could become unacknowledged researcher degrees of freedom if the primary denominator, cluster unit, weighting rule, and pass criterion are not frozen before audit.

## Questions That Would Not Actually Discriminate

Asking which denominator objects are representable does not identify which denominator is appropriate for the intended later claim. It only establishes observability and cost.

Asking for a hierarchy that “avoids double counting” cannot yield a unique answer before duplicate, complementary, substitutable, split, merge, and many-to-many relation semantics are fixed.

Comparing pair counts with grouped counts does not by itself distinguish artificial inflation from legitimate multiplicity.

A coverage–reliability frontier shows a tradeoff but does not establish role relevance, future experimental value, or the correct operating point.

An importance-weighting robustness comparison can show sensitivity to a rubric but cannot establish that the rubric measures true importance.

A direct external precedent for representative role workload would not validate B001 representativeness unless the role functions, corpus, outputs, and decision target match.

The false-passage simulation tests whether decisions were predeclared; it does not provide evidence for or against the eligible-core, trivial-residue, qualification-loss, or clustered-sparsity possibilities.

## Required Corrections to the Research Direction

1. Freeze a one-sentence target decision before specifying a numeric gate: what later experiment would a pass authorize, what claim could it support, and what population or stratum would that claim cover.
2. Separate the current deliverable into a denominator-map deliverable and a gate-specification deliverable. The first can proceed now; the second must remain conditional until the target decision, corpus, and unit of inference are fixed.
3. Replace the single hierarchy with a linked denominator lattice. Keep task instances, outputs, sections, epistemic or role-function units, upstream claim units, evidence units, relation edges, relation groups, clusters, downstream units, and lineage links as distinct tables or object classes.
4. State every coverage measure as a named numerator over a named denominator. Do not divide across non-nested object types without a defined mapping rule.
5. Define the sampled corpus and task-instance denominator before estimating workload or prevalence. Report what population the sample is intended to represent and what it cannot represent.
6. Freeze a multilabel role-function taxonomy grounded in `AGENT_PROTOCOL.md`, separating assertive factual content and improper promotions from legitimate hypotheses, speculation, objections, hybrids, and unresolved questions.
7. Replace “substantive factual content depends on eligible units” with observable relation-preserving incorporation, output-unit coverage, qualification-preservation coverage, or blinded role-function importance ratings.
8. Define each candidate-relation universe by its generation procedure and source access. Do not claim access to a complete latent universe of possible or true relations.
9. Co-develop the minimal atomicity, identity, evidence-boundary, direct-support, split/merge, duplicate, complementary-support, substitutable-support, and many-to-many rules with the denominator schema. Freeze them together before audit.
10. Preserve both edge-level and bounded-group-level counts. Use grouping to diagnose sensitivity, not to declare all multiplicity inflation.
11. Freeze role-relevance and importance rubrics independently of eligibility, reliability, treatment outcome, and audit results. Report weighted and unweighted coverage with rater uncertainty.
12. Declare the primary cluster unit from the intended inferential target. Treat other clustering schemes as predeclared sensitivity analyses.
13. Maintain an exhaustive exclusion ledger with multilabel reasons, affected role functions, qualification burden, source structure, and whether exclusion arose from the primary narrow unit rather than from absence of relevant material.
14. Keep development and held-out audit separate. Development may expose instability and select one frozen rule; audit may only test the frozen rule. No favorable audit stratum, metric, cluster level, or threshold may be promoted post hoc.
15. Do not import universal reliability, prevalence, coverage, diversity, or cost thresholds from external literature. Thresholds must be tied to the intended downstream decision and false-passage cost.
16. Predeclare local failure actions for low factual-stratum prevalence, low eligible coverage, low role relevance, high qualification loss, unstable identity, cluster collapse, checkpoint dependence, excessive resource cost, and metric sensitivity.

## Internal Research Brief

Produce a branch-grounded denominator and observability map, not a prevalence estimate.

Identify which object types are directly present in ordinary authorized artifacts, which can be created by offline annotation without changing agent behavior, and which require new agent reporting, retrieval, selection, or rationale checkpoints. For every object, state its fields, identity rule, parent or mapping relations, and whether it is non-nested with other objects.

Derive a minimal multilabel role-function taxonomy from `AGENT_PROTOCOL.md` and accepted state. Separate assertive factual claims, source interpretations, derived conclusions, hypotheses, speculation, objections, hybrids, and unresolved questions. Identify where mixed units require splitting or multilabel coding.

Draft a denominator lattice and an allowed-ratio table. At minimum distinguish task instances, role outputs, output sections, role-function units, upstream factual units, evidence units, candidate-relation sets by generation procedure, eligible primary units, relation edges, bounded relation groups, downstream units, observable lineage links, and source/claim/output clusters.

Specify which definitions are already constrained by accepted branch state and which are new design choices. Do not invent numeric thresholds, claim a representative corpus, estimate prevalence, or infer latent causal use.

Propose an exclusion-ledger schema and a role-relevance rubric that are blind to eligibility, reliability, and later treatment outcomes. State where prospective blinding is impossible and what bias remains.

Flag duplication explicitly. Do not re-establish accepted C0005 conclusions about development/audit separation, observable incorporation, qualification, reliability limits, or selected-subset contamination except where needed to constrain the new schema.

## External Research Brief

Search only for primary methodological sources that can inform the missing denominator design. Prioritize:

- multilevel or non-nested annotation and evaluation units;
- grouped, set-valued, hyperedge, or bounded many-to-many relation annotation;
- reliability reported jointly with retained coverage or abstention;
- clustered dependence, pseudo-replication, and sampling units in annotation audits;
- rare-stratum or stratified prospective sampling without post hoc favorable subgroup selection;
- exclusion accounting and selective coverage–quality curves;
- weighted content or task coverage with safeguards against outcome-based weighting;
- split/merge and overlapping identity reliability;
- decision-linked feasibility thresholds, precision requirements, and false-passage costs.

For every source, report the exact object, denominator, sampling unit, cluster unit, exclusion rule, metric, decision target, and transfer limitation. Distinguish a directly reusable method from a loose analogy.

Do not repeat the general C0005 search for fact verification, attribution, span agreement, or qualification methods unless a source supplies a denominator or coverage method absent from the existing branch record. Do not import universal cutoffs or claim that an external method validates B001 role relevance.

Specifically assess whether the method supports non-nested objects, preserves edge and group counts simultaneously, handles unknown candidate-relation universes, reports coverage after filtering, and separates ordinary-artifact observability from intervention-created records.

## Handoff

The Explorer’s core direction is adequate and should be preserved, but it must be narrowed from “define a denominator hierarchy and gate” to “define a linked denominator-and-observability system and the conditions under which a later gate could be specified.”

The most important correction is that no numerical retained-coverage or role-relevance gate is valid until the later decision target, claim scope, corpus, unit of inference, and observable role-relevance rubric are frozen. The researchers should proceed on methods and branch-local schema constraints, while treating B001 prevalence, representativeness, causal dependence, and pass thresholds as unresolved.

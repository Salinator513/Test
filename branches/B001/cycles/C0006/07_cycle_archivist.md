## Integrator Statements Qualified or Corrected

1. **Single-hierarchy wording:** “The handoff cannot be represented by one clean denominator hierarchy” was narrowed. The accepted conclusion is that the currently proposed cross-cutting object set must not be forced into a single nesting hierarchy; a linked lattice or multi-table system is required unless a narrower, valid nesting is explicitly demonstrated for a particular estimand.
2. **Challenge testing:** `C0006-INTG-02` implied that adequacy requires prospective challenge testing. TREC supplies a strong local precedent, but not a universal requirement for every candidate universe. The accepted state treats an independent challenge procedure as a recommended prospective adequacy check whose necessity and design depend on the stated decision.
3. **Role relevance and importance:** `C0006-INTG-05` is accepted as workload decomposition, but no substantive-importance weighting method is validated. The accepted requirement is to define a prospective, outcome-blind role-function or importance rubric, report rater uncertainty, and retain unweighted results; no weighted gate is accepted.
4. **Selective-prediction transfer:** The quality–coverage sources directly establish a retained-coverage tradeoff for prediction, not annotation reliability or Alternative Theorist role relevance. Their use in `C0006-INTG-06` is accepted only as a methodological analogy supporting joint reporting, not as validation of a B001 operating curve.
5. **CONSORT transfer:** `C0006-SRC-19` supports transparent flow and exclusion reporting by analogy. It does not establish that B001 exclusions are valid, exhaustive, or ecologically acceptable.
6. **Explanation-unfaithfulness provenance:** `C0006-SRC-20` supports the claim that generated explanations can omit experimentally induced influences. It does not by itself establish that citations or selected-subset reports are unfaithful, or that checkpoints change behavior and cost. Those broader precautions are retained from prior accepted state and the C0006 internal observational map.
7. **Decision-linked design fact:** `C0006-SRC-17` is recorded specifically as a reliability-study sizing precedent tied to null and target reliability, rater design, significance, and power. It is not a universal B001 reliability model. `C0006-SRC-18` supplies a separate decision-consequence analogy.
8. **Broad bundled citations:** Support for `C0006-INTG-06`, `C0006-INTG-07`, and `C0006-INTG-10` was narrowed in the accepted state to the specific source families that establish each component, with the External Evidence fixed-resource synthesis used for the overall resource conclusion.

# Critic — C0002

## Fair Reconstruction of the Explorer’s Direction

The Explorer proposes a narrow methodological frontier rather than another broad test of multi-agent superiority. The intended target is the independent effect, if any, of replacing a persistent staged workflow with fresh staged invocations while holding stage sequence, instructions, visible evidence, intermediate artifacts, verifier inputs, candidate opportunities, selector rules, integration access, and charged compute as constant as possible.

The proposal also treats the transferred state as a possible moderator. Its central possibilities are: no stable boundary effect after matching; reduced contamination when fresh stages do not inherit unstructured earlier reasoning; interface loss when handoffs omit dependencies; a boundary-by-state interaction; dominance of structured state over freshness; an apparent persistent advantage caused by privileged access to prior trace; effects of handoff compression; and stage-specific sensitivity to resets.

The proposed program contains three conceptually different lines of inquiry:

1. a boundary/access contrast between persistent and fresh execution;
2. a state-representation and retention contrast across weak, structured, and higher-retention packages; and
3. a measurement program for coverage-sensitive error lineage and full compute accounting.

That reconstruction is coherent and faithful to the accepted C0001 state. The problem is not the overall motivation. The problem is that the primary treatment and the state-completeness factor are not yet operationally defined well enough to support the causal interpretations assigned to them.

## What Is Strong or Worth Preserving

The frontier is appropriately narrower than C0001 and explicitly avoids claiming that the seven-role loop is generally superior. It correctly treats informational access, handoff transformation, verifier information, sampling opportunities, coverage, and compute as possible confounders rather than as incidental implementation details.

The following parts are already adequate and should be preserved:

- The distinction between unrestricted persistent access and access limited to a standardized stage package is essential.
- The warning that byte-identical inputs do not guarantee identical use is appropriately cautious.
- The phrase “complete-as-specified” is better than claiming literal completeness, although it still needs a separate semantic-retention definition.
- The coverage-error tradeoff is correctly recognized. Unsupported-claim counts cannot be interpreted without factual coverage, task completion, citation completeness, and usefulness.
- Handoff generation and context consumption are correctly included in compute accounting.
- Dependency-injection probes are a promising way to create a known reference set for preservation, omission, contradiction, mutation, correction, and amplification.
- C0002-EXP-08 is correctly labeled speculative.
- The Explorer explicitly states that the primary compute rule and evaluator protocol remain unresolved rather than silently assuming them.

The state-format and handoff-retention work can produce useful results even if a pure invocation-boundary effect cannot be isolated. Those useful components should not be discarded.

## Fatal Problems

### 1. The proposed boundary-only estimand has no defined nonconfounded treatment

The proposal says that the only intended manipulation is persistent versus fresh invocation, but it does not identify what observable difference remains after visible stage inputs and prior-context access are equalized.

If the persistent condition retains earlier unstructured trace, it has more information, more retrieval opportunities, and different context length than the fresh condition. Any difference then estimates prior-trace access plus its costs and salience effects, not an invocation boundary alone.

If the persistent condition is restricted to exactly the same serialized package and cannot use earlier trace, the proposal has not stated what functionally relevant treatment still differs. Calling one execution “persistent” and the other “fresh” does not itself define a measurable causal factor. The Explorer already notices that restriction may create a functional reset, but the proposed primary contrast still treats it as a boundary test.

This is fatal to C0002-EXP-01 through C0002-EXP-03 as currently framed. Before research is spent on a “boundary-only” effect, the branch must define the treatment in observable terms. A valid design may estimate the effect of authorized prior-trace access, context reset, or state serialization. It must not claim an independent invocation-boundary effect unless a distinct manipulation remains after all inputs are matched.

A three-condition decomposition should replace the current ambiguity:

- persistent execution with full prior trace;
- persistent execution with standardized-package-only access;
- fresh execution with the same standardized-package-only access.

The first contrast can estimate prior-trace privilege. The second contrast is informative about a boundary only if the protocol identifies and verifies a remaining difference other than labels, message history, role prompt, permissions, tools, or visible information. Otherwise it should be treated as an equivalence or implementation-control comparison, not as a causal test of freshness.

### 2. “State completeness” is circular unless assessed against a predeclared task-relative reference

The proposal alternates among schema completeness, handoff retention, preservation of essential dependencies, and outcome adequacy. These are not the same factor.

A package can fill every declared field and still omit a task-critical relation. Conversely, a concise package can omit many details while retaining every dependency needed for the final answer. If a state level is called “complete” because later stages perform well, the hypothesized state-completeness threshold becomes circular. If “essential dependencies” are identified after observing failures, the threshold can be manufactured post hoc.

This is fatal to the proposed 2 × 2 boundary-by-state interpretation and C0002-EXP-04 unless completeness is defined independently of outcomes. The correction is to separate:

- structural completeness: compliance with a declared schema;
- retention: proportion of a predeclared information inventory preserved;
- task-relative dependency coverage: preservation of dependencies designated before the system output is scored; and
- package size or compression: amount and form of transferred content.

Dependency-injection probes can supply a known reference set. For open-ended tasks, independent annotation must identify required claims, qualifications, relations, objections, and unresolved items before condition outcomes are compared. The branch should avoid a single scalar “completeness” label until these dimensions are operationalized.

## Material Problems

### 1. The primary 2 × 2 comparison mixes state quality with asymmetric trace access

In “persistent + weak package,” the persistent workflow may still recover information from the earlier trace, while “fresh + weak package” cannot. In “persistent + complete-as-specified package,” the persistent condition may receive both the package and redundant prior trace. The cells therefore do not differ only on boundary and state. The proposed factorial is interpretable only if prior-trace access is an explicit third factor or is identically restricted.

### 2. The proposal contains too many frontiers for one cycle

The cycle includes boundary isolation, state completeness, structured-state dominance, context privilege, compression, stage placement, dependency probes, measurement design, and compute accounting. Several are prerequisite methods rather than competing hypotheses. Attempting all of them will produce a broad literature inventory instead of a decisive correction to the primary contrast.

C0002 should prioritize, in order:

1. defining the observable treatment and estimand;
2. defining independent state-retention measures;
3. specifying the minimum measurement and compute controls required for an interpretable contrast.

Stage-placement effects and broad comparisons of handoff formats should be deferred unless directly needed to resolve those three items.

### 3. Several hypotheses are not mutually discriminating

The boundary-only null, structured-state dominance, and successful restriction of persistent access can all predict similar outcomes between package-matched fresh and persistent conditions. A null difference would not distinguish “freshness has no effect,” “state dominates,” “the manipulation collapsed,” or “the task is insensitive.”

Likewise, better fresh-condition outcomes could support contamination reduction, shorter or less complete outputs, lower exposure to valid dependencies, different context-processing cost, or a package-promotion rule that selectively excludes earlier claims. The design needs manipulation checks and outcome controls before those hypotheses can be separated.

### 4. “Hidden-context privilege” is partly a design diagnosis, not a substantive hypothesis

C0002-EXP-06 states that persistent access is unequal when later stages can inspect material unavailable to fresh stages. That is logically correct but largely definitional. Research should determine the effect of that access and how to control it, not spend resources trying to establish that unequal inputs are unequal.

### 5. The compute rule remains a gating requirement

A secondary ledger is useful, but recording multiple costs does not itself create fixed-compute equivalence. The primary matching rule must specify what is held fixed and how budget released or consumed by shorter contexts, package generation, or compression is handled. Otherwise state-format effects can be budget-allocation effects.

### 6. The literature questions do not yet contain strict inclusion criteria

A search for “persistent versus reset,” “memory fidelity,” or “structured summaries” can retrieve studies that change prompts, model calls, agent counts, role labels, context quantity, tools, retrieval, or total compute. Near-match studies may be methodologically useful without discriminating among C0002 hypotheses. The external brief must require an explicit intervention matrix and label indirect evidence as such.

### 7. The measurement plan needs manipulation checks

Outcome scoring alone cannot show that the intended state or access manipulation occurred. Each condition should verify at least:

- which exact inputs were authorized and supplied;
- whether prior trace was accessible;
- whether injected dependencies appeared in the package;
- package length and field compliance;
- actual compute consumed by each stage; and
- whether output coverage and claim count differed materially.

Without these checks, a null or positive effect cannot be interpreted.

## Minor Problems

- “Weak stage package” is evaluative and underspecified. It should name the omitted fields or retention rule.
- “High-retention” can be confused with semantic completeness. It should be defined by a measurable retention policy or size rule.
- “Potentially beneficial when sufficiently complete” in C0002-EXP-04 combines a directional prediction with an undefined threshold. It should be split into separate preregistered contrasts.
- “Full charged compute” is used before a primary accounting rule exists. The document should say “recorded cost categories” until the matching rule is selected.
- “Unstructured prior reasoning” should be limited to observable prior conversational trace. The branch should not make claims about inaccessible internal reasoning.
- “Matched-output” analysis can create its own selection problem if outputs are truncated or filtered after generation. Minimum-coverage constraints are safer as a prespecified design feature than post hoc matching.
- Boundary-placement comparisons are unlikely to be interpretable until the main access and package definitions are settled.

## Hidden Assumptions

1. Invocation continuity has a causal meaning independent of visible message history, instructions, tools, permissions, and state access.
2. A persistent workflow can be prevented from using earlier trace without thereby eliminating the treatment of interest.
3. The same serialized package can be supplied to both conditions without differences in role prompts, system instructions, permissions, or integration rules.
4. State completeness is scalar, ordered, and comparable across tasks.
5. The branch can identify “essential dependencies” independently of observed performance.
6. More retained information is monotonically closer to completeness rather than a mixture of useful dependencies, irrelevant details, and unsupported claims.
7. Equal authorized input produces sufficiently comparable retrieval and salience conditions.
8. Package construction does not selectively favor one hypothesis by deciding which claims are promoted, omitted, or retyped.
9. Compute consumed by package construction and longer context can be matched without changing the reasoning resources available to later stages.
10. Dependency-injection tasks represent the difficulties of open-ended research synthesis rather than only synthetic preservation.
11. Error-lineage categories can be applied reliably enough to distinguish preservation, mutation, correction, and amplification.
12. Null average effects are informative despite possible task, stage, and verifier heterogeneity.

## Scope and Definition Problems

The following terms require operational definitions before the two researchers search broadly:

- **Invocation boundary:** Is this a new conversation container, absence of prior messages, a new role prompt, a new model call, changed permissions, or a declared combination?
- **Persistent workflow:** Does every stage see the full prior transcript, only selected messages, or a standardized package appended within one conversation?
- **Fresh workflow:** Which instructions, artifacts, role permissions, tools, and model settings are reintroduced, and which are absent?
- **Informational equivalence:** Equality of authorized visible inputs, equality of bytes, equality of fields, equality of task-relevant dependency coverage, or equality of retrievable information?
- **State:** Raw stage output, accepted state, a claim ledger, a summary, or all transferred artifacts?
- **State completeness:** Schema compliance, semantic retention, dependency coverage, or observed sufficiency?
- **Weak package:** Which information is deliberately missing, and by what rule?
- **Compression:** Token count, field deletion, summarization, lossy transformation, or prioritization?
- **Boundary effect:** Effect of missing prior trace, effect of context reset, or an asserted effect of a fresh invocation after trace access is equalized?
- **Cumulative reasoning error:** Which lineage events are counted, how are omissions weighted, and what is the unit of analysis?
- **Fixed compute:** What primary resource is matched, what tolerance is allowed, and what happens to saved budget?

The cleanest correction is to stop using “invocation boundary” as the primary operational label until the residual treatment is specified. Use observable factors such as prior-trace access, package representation, package retention, stage instruction, and context budget.

## Risks of Misleading Research

- A fresh advantage may be reported when the fresh condition simply produces fewer claims or lower coverage.
- A persistent advantage may be reported when it receives extra valid information, more retrieval opportunities, or more context-processing compute.
- A null may be reported as evidence against freshness when the restricted persistent and fresh conditions are functionally the same treatment.
- A state threshold may be inferred after the fact by calling successful packages complete and unsuccessful packages incomplete.
- Longer packages may appear better because they contain more task information, or worse because they consume more budget; either could be mislabeled a completeness effect.
- Literature on summaries, memory, context windows, or agent handoffs may be treated as direct evidence for fresh-role separation even when it only informs package design.
- Generic multi-agent or self-correction studies may duplicate C0001 without reducing the central identification problem.
- Average effects may conceal opposite results across sequential and decomposable tasks, stage locations, verifier conditions, or evidence-access regimes.
- Semantic-similarity measures may count a claim as preserved while its epistemic status, qualification, support, or contradiction relation has changed.
- Synthetic dependency probes may overstate transfer to open-ended research if the dependencies are unusually explicit.

## Questions That Would Not Actually Discriminate

The following questions may be useful background, but they do not by themselves discriminate among the proposed boundary hypotheses:

- Internal question 6, insofar as it asks about “unrecorded reasoning.” Repository inspection can identify observable trace, instructions, and artifacts, but not inaccessible internal cognition.
- Internal question 7, unless the branch first defines what remains different between package-only persistent and fresh execution.
- Internal questions 9 and 10. They establish measurement and accounting requirements but do not distinguish contamination reduction, interface loss, or a boundary null.
- External question 6. Evidence that structured claim fields improve verification supports a state-representation mechanism, not an invocation-boundary effect.
- External question 8. Cost-accounting practice is necessary for design quality but does not distinguish the substantive hypotheses.
- External questions 9 and 10. Coverage tradeoffs and evaluator designs are measurement safeguards, not direct tests of the boundary mechanism.
- The restricted-persistent versus unrestricted-persistent comparison. It estimates prior-trace access, not fresh invocation.
- The handoff-retention comparison with boundary held constant. It estimates package representation or retention effects, not boundary effects.
- A generic fresh-versus-persistent study that does not match visible inputs and compute. Its result cannot distinguish freshness from information and budget differences.
- A null result in package-matched fresh versus persistent conditions without a verified residual manipulation. It cannot distinguish a true null from treatment collapse.

These questions should not be dropped entirely. They should be labeled as design, measurement, or state-mechanism questions rather than evidence for or against a boundary-only effect.

## Required Corrections to the Research Direction

1. **Replace the metaphysical boundary question with an observable estimand.** Define whether C0002 studies prior-trace access, context reset, package serialization, or a verified residual property of persistent versus fresh execution.
2. **Use a three-condition access decomposition.** Compare full-trace persistent, package-only persistent, and package-only fresh conditions. Interpret full-trace versus package-only persistent as an access effect. Treat package-only persistent versus package-only fresh as a boundary contrast only if a distinct residual manipulation is defined and verified.
3. **Separate state dimensions.** Measure schema compliance, predeclared information retention, task-relative dependency coverage, package size, and transformation type separately. Do not collapse them into one “completeness” axis.
4. **Predeclare the dependency reference.** Use injected dependencies or independent task annotation created before condition outcomes are scored. Do not infer essential dependencies from which outputs succeeded.
5. **Add manipulation checks.** Verify supplied inputs, accessible trace, package contents, field compliance, dependency retention, context length, stage costs, claim count, and coverage.
6. **Choose the primary compute rule before causal interpretation.** Record the secondary ledger, but also state what resource is matched and how saved or excess budget is treated.
7. **Narrow C0002 research.** Prioritize treatment definition, state-retention operationalization, and minimum measurement controls. Defer stage-placement and broad handoff-format optimization unless a source directly resolves the primary identification problem.
8. **Set strict external inclusion criteria.** Extract model, prompts, stage instructions, visible inputs, prior-trace access, state artifact, tools, samples, selector, evaluator, task, and full cost for each study. Mark any mismatch as a confound.
9. **Separate direct from methodological evidence.** Studies of serialization, summaries, memory, lineage, or evaluator reliability may inform implementation without supporting a fresh-boundary effect.
10. **State disconfirming evidence in advance.** Evidence against the favored contamination-reduction direction includes: fresh conditions preserving fewer valid dependencies; fresh gains disappearing after coverage controls; full-trace effects fully explaining apparent freshness effects; no difference after a verified noncollapsed boundary manipulation; or state-format effects dominating all boundary contrasts.

## Internal Research Brief

The Internal Evidence Researcher should produce a branch-local design audit, not attempt to prove which architecture is better.

Required outputs:

1. Map every stage-defined information object in the current protocol and identify which later roles are authorized to receive it. Distinguish raw role output, control state, accepted state, instructions, evidence references, and handoff text.
2. Build a treatment-factor table with separate columns for conversation continuity, prior-trace access, standardized package, package format, package retention rule, stage instruction, role label, permissions, tools, evidence access, verifier input, candidate count, selector access, model settings, and compute allocation.
3. Determine whether a package-only persistent condition is implementable under the branch rules and identify exactly what observable property would remain different from a fresh package-only condition. If no such property is defined, state that the branch cannot presently identify a boundary-only effect.
4. Inventory which proposed state fields already exist in the protocol or accepted state. Do not infer that existing fields are sufficient or complete.
5. Separate structural schema compliance from task-relative semantic retention. Propose branch-local names and measurable fields for both without claiming external validation.
6. Identify every point where package construction can omit, summarize, normalize, reclassify, or mutate prior content.
7. Identify which C0002 questions duplicate accepted C0001 conclusions or unresolved questions. Preserve only the new operational work.
8. Specify the minimum manipulation checks and cost records the repository can support.
9. Flag any requested quantity that cannot be observed from authorized repository artifacts, especially inaccessible internal reasoning.
10. End with a corrected experimental contrast table and an explicit statement of which contrasts estimate trace access, state representation, retention, compression, or any genuinely residual boundary factor.

The Internal Researcher should not call a package complete merely because every protocol section is present. It should report only schema compliance and observable retention.

## External Research Brief

The External Evidence Researcher should search for primary studies and methods under strict separation of direct and indirect relevance.

Priority A — direct identification:

- Controlled comparisons of prior-trace access versus standardized-package-only access.
- Controlled reset or fresh-context comparisons where model, stage instructions, visible inputs, evidence, tools, candidate opportunities, selector rules, and compute are held approximately constant.
- Studies that explicitly reveal what remains different after message history is equalized.

Priority B — state and handoff methods:

- Controlled manipulation of state serialization, summary retention, memory fidelity, claim-ledger structure, or handoff compression.
- Methods that define a predeclared information or dependency reference and score preservation, omission, contradiction, mutation, correction, and amplification.
- Evaluator designs with explicit reliability checks for those lineage categories.

For every located study, extract an intervention matrix covering model, task, prompts, role labels, prior-trace access, visible inputs, state artifact, evidence, tools, sampling, selector, evaluator, context length, generation cost, and final-answer cost. Classify the source as:

- direct evidence on the corrected boundary/access estimand;
- indirect evidence on state or handoff design;
- measurement method only; or
- non-discriminating due to bundled changes.

Do not spend the cycle repeating generic multi-agent debate, self-refinement, added-sampling, or broad fixed-compute comparisons already represented in C0001 unless a source supplies a materially cleaner reset/access isolation. Do not treat improved structured memory or provenance as evidence for fresh invocation. Do not treat lower error counts as improvement unless coverage and task completion are available.

The search should actively seek evidence against contamination reduction, including cases where reset loses valid qualifications, structured summaries distort status or relations, package-only access harms sequential tasks, or apparent gains vanish after equalizing output coverage and cost.

## Handoff

The Explorer’s direction is worth continuing only after reframing. The immediate research target should be an observable decomposition of prior-trace access and state transfer, not an undefined “invocation boundary” effect.

The Internal Evidence Researcher should determine whether the branch can define a noncollapsed package-only persistent versus package-only fresh manipulation and should build the factor and access matrix. The External Evidence Researcher should search only for studies that map cleanly onto that matrix, while treating state serialization and error-lineage work as methodological support rather than direct evidence for freshness.

Do not advance C0002 on the assumption that the proposed 2 × 2 design is already causal. The principal correction is to split trace access, state representation, semantic retention, compression, and any residual invocation property into separate factors before interpreting outcomes.

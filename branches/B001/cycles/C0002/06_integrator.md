# Integrator — C0002

## Research Question Addressed

What can the current evidence establish about persistent versus fresh staged execution when the branch attempts to control transferred information, and how should the branch operationalize state transfer without confusing invocation labels with prior-trace access, compression, retention, salience, or compute allocation?

The answer is narrower than the Explorer’s initial “boundary-only” framing. C0002 can support conclusions about observable information access and state-transfer factors. It cannot yet identify an independent causal effect of a fresh conversation or invocation container after all visible inputs and prior-trace access are matched.

## Evidence Assessment

### Overall evidential weight

The strongest evidence supports an **information-access and relay-fidelity framing**. `C0002-SRC-01`, interpreted in `C0002-EXT-01`, is the closest located study to the corrected question. It treats full accumulated context and isolated local contexts connected by relays as different information-flow systems. Its theoretical and empirical results assign the operative difference to relay sufficiency, compression, and loss: sufficiently rich relays can reproduce upstream context, while bounded relays can help or harm depending on what information survives. This is near-direct evidence for the Critic’s decomposition, but it is not evidence for an intrinsic fresh-container effect because local isolation and relay compression remain bundled.

The record also contains credible indirect evidence that prior trace can be harmful. `C0002-SRC-02`, `C0002-SRC-03`, and `C0002-SRC-08`, synthesized in `C0002-EXT-02`, report multi-turn unreliability, task-switch interference, and position-sensitive use of long context. These findings support treating authorized prior-trace access, context length, and information position as real factors. They do not isolate context reset or freshness with identical visible inputs.

The strongest evidence against a general contamination-reduction account comes from the same information-flow record. `C0002-SRC-01` reports reversals when relays omit task-relevant information; `C0002-SRC-05`, `C0002-SRC-06`, and `C0002-SRC-07` report or diagnose write-time evidence loss, sustained-interaction degradation, and difficulty preserving temporal or causal dependencies. Therefore contamination reduction and interface loss are not independent mutually exclusive theories. They are competing consequences of filtering and serialization.

The structured-state evidence is positive but indirect and bundled. `C0002-SRC-04`, `C0002-SRC-05`, and `C0002-SRC-14` support selective condensation, predictive memory writing, or structured temporal memory in their tested settings. However, they change state construction, prompts, calls, retrieval, tools, or other resources. They support state representation and retention as active interventions, not fresh invocation and not equal-compute superiority.

The measurement sources provide useful components rather than a complete branch solution. `C0002-SRC-07`, `C0002-SRC-09`, and `C0002-SRC-11` support predeclared information inventories or reference-relative content coverage. `C0002-SRC-10`, `C0002-SRC-12`, and `C0002-SRC-13` support fine-grained factual-error categories, correction scoring, and evaluator caution. None follows stable claim identities and epistemic statuses through generation, criticism, evidence review, and integration using the complete introduced/preserved/corrected/mutated/amplified lineage required by `C0001-INTG-03`.

### Directness and isolation

No source in the fourteen-source C0002 ledger compares package-only persistent and package-only fresh execution while holding the serialized package, prior-trace inaccessibility, model, prompts, stage instructions, permissions, tools, evidence, candidate opportunities, selector rules, and full inference compute approximately constant and while identifying a nontrivial residual manipulation. This is a search-bounded evidence-gap finding from `04_external_evidence.md`, not a universal literature claim.

No source satisfies the branch’s full compute-isolation requirement. `C0002-EXT-07` finds that even the closest studies match only partial resources such as maximum steps, stored-memory tokens, or readers, while leaving prompt processing, accumulated-context processing, package construction, tool calls, retrieval, candidates, selection, orchestration, or final-answer generation unequal. The current evidence therefore does **not** isolate role separation from added or differently allocated compute.

### Source reliability and independence

The external ledger contains fourteen original research sources: eleven peer-reviewed or accepted benchmark papers and three original-author preprints. The most directly relevant source, `C0002-SRC-01`, is a recent preprint and should receive less reliability weight than peer-reviewed evidence until independently tested.

Several positive findings are not independent replications. `C0002-SRC-05` evaluates LongMemEval from `C0002-SRC-06`; `C0002-SRC-14` evaluates LongMemEval and LoCoMo from `C0002-SRC-06` and `C0002-SRC-07`; `C0002-SRC-13` aggregates existing factuality datasets and may overlap with individual measurement sources. These overlaps limit any claim based on counting studies.

Agreement among the C0002 Critic, Internal Evidence Researcher, External Evidence Researcher, and Alternative Theorist is also not independent validation. Their agreement is useful only where it correctly traces to branch facts or the cited source ledger. Repetition of the same interpretation by same-model roles adds no evidential weight under `AGENT_PROTOCOL.md` and `C0002-INT-02`.

### Internal branch evidence

`C0002-INT-01` through `C0002-INT-04` establish that B001 currently bundles fresh conversations with ordered functions, permissions, external state, exact-reference rules, and gated progression; distinguishes control state, accepted state, and raw cycle outputs; and lacks a canonical standardized stage package. These are branch-architecture facts and evidence gaps, not outcome evidence.

`C0002-INT-05` correctly identifies that the branch lacks an accepted operational definition of a residual invocation-boundary treatment. `C0002-INT-07` and `C0002-INT-08` correctly carry forward the accepted compute and measurement gates from `C0001-INTG-02` and `C0001-INTG-03`. `C0002-INT-09` through `C0002-INT-13` preserve relevant C0001 risks and metric components, but they do not constitute new independent empirical evidence.

## Main Interpretation

The best-supported current interpretation is a **qualified information-access and relay-fidelity tradeoff**.

Persistent full-trace execution and package-mediated execution differ because package construction filters, selects, compresses, reorders, types, and sometimes retrieves prior information. Filtering can improve later work when it removes premature commitments, mismatched-task material, unsupported claims, redundancy, or distracting context. The same filtering can harm later work when it omits qualifications, temporal or causal relations, support links, objections, contradictions, or unresolved dependencies.

The sign and magnitude of the effect are therefore expected to depend on at least:

- authorized prior-trace access;
- the predeclared information inventory retained;
- task-relative dependency coverage;
- relation and epistemic-status preservation;
- write-time selection and retrieval-time access;
- information order, adjacency, position, and surrounding context volume;
- task structure and model capability;
- stage prompts, permissions, tools, candidate and selector opportunities; and
- the budget consumed by constructing, validating, reading, and using the package.

This interpretation is a derived conclusion supported by `C0002-EXT-01` through `C0002-EXT-07` and `C0002-SRC-01` through `C0002-SRC-14`, with the greatest mechanism weight on `C0002-SRC-01`, `C0002-SRC-02`, `C0002-SRC-03`, `C0002-SRC-05`, `C0002-SRC-08`, and `C0002-SRC-09`. It is not a branch-local causal estimate and does not establish which mechanism dominates in open-ended multi-source research synthesis.

The branch should consequently stop treating “fresh versus persistent” as one operational factor. Until a residual property is named and verified, the primary observable factors are prior-trace access, package representation, retention, dependency coverage, transformation, salience, retrieval, prompt and permission structure, sampling and selection, and compute allocation.

## Alternative Interpretations

### Context-salience and retrieval geometry

`C0002-SRC-08`, with supporting relevance from `C0002-SRC-02`, `C0002-SRC-03`, and `C0002-SRC-04`, supports the possibility that apparent reset or package effects arise from information position, adjacency, redundancy, and retrieval competition even when the same reference items are present. This alternative is compatible with the main interpretation but identifies usability of retained information, rather than only retention, as a separate mechanism. It remains untested in B001-style synthesis.

### Resource allocation, sampling, and orchestration

`C0002-EXT-07` and the bundled interventions in `C0002-SRC-04`, `C0002-SRC-05`, and `C0002-SRC-14` strongly support unequal resource allocation as a confounding explanation. Shorter contexts, extra write or condenser calls, specialized prompts, added tools, repeated samples, selectors, and saved-budget reallocation can create apparent gains. This theory does not explain every position or interference effect, but no present source excludes it.

### State-write bottleneck

`C0002-SRC-05`, supported methodologically by `C0002-SRC-06`, `C0002-SRC-07`, and `C0002-SRC-09` through `C0002-SRC-12`, supports the view that decisive errors can occur when prior material is promoted, omitted, retyped, or relationally flattened in the package. This mechanism predicts that exact-package replay with fixed downstream readers will reproduce package-construction differences. It does not explain all effects of reader capability, salience, or continuing trace access.

### Residual-boundary null

The hypothesis that no stable effect remains after every observable factor is matched is plausible but unproven. The present evidence mainly shows non-identification and possible treatment collapse. If package-only persistent and package-only fresh conditions have identical authorized inputs and no accessible prior trace, the branch must first identify what nontrivial property still differs. Without such a property, equality is an implementation-equivalence result, not evidence that a real boundary treatment has zero effect.

### Hybrid interaction account

The evidence is most compatible with interactions among harmful-trace exclusion, valid-dependency loss, write quality, salience, reader capability, task structure, prompts, and resource allocation. A pooled average could conceal positive effects in interference-heavy or separable tasks and negative effects in sequential, temporal, causal, or dependency-dense tasks. This is a useful organizing framework, not yet a quantified empirical claim.

## Claim Decisions

### Claim ID: C0002-INTG-01 — Observable-factor reframing

- **Proposed status:** Accepted derived conclusion.
- **Evidence and source IDs:** Critic Fatal Problem 1 and Required Corrections; `C0002-INT-01`, `C0002-INT-04`, `C0002-INT-05`; `C0002-EXT-01`; `C0002-SRC-01`.
- **Reasoning:** The current branch implementation bundles freshness with other features, and the closest external evidence assigns outcomes to information access and relay sufficiency. A nominal conversation-container label is not a sufficient causal treatment.
- **Scope:** Interpretation and design of B001 contrasts involving persistent and fresh staged execution.
- **Limitations:** This does not prove that no residual invocation property can exist. It states that none is currently operationally defined or externally isolated.

### Claim ID: C0002-INTG-02 — Residual fresh-container evidence gap

- **Proposed status:** Accepted search-bounded evidence-gap conclusion.
- **Evidence and source IDs:** `04_external_evidence.md`, Research Scope, Evidence Weakening the Main Direction, and Evidence Gaps; `C0002-EXT-01`; `C0002-EXT-07`; all `C0002-SRC-01` through `C0002-SRC-14` as classified in the intervention matrix.
- **Reasoning:** No located source identifies a noncollapsed package-only persistent-versus-package-only fresh manipulation with matched visible inputs, prior-trace inaccessibility, tools, sampling, selection, and full compute.
- **Scope:** The fourteen-source C0002 ledger.
- **Limitations:** Absence from this search is not proof that no qualifying study exists elsewhere.

### Claim ID: C0002-INTG-03 — Filtering tradeoff

- **Proposed status:** Accepted qualified derived conclusion.
- **Evidence and source IDs:** `C0002-EXT-01` through `C0002-EXT-04`; `C0002-SRC-01` through `C0002-SRC-08`; prior risk `C0001-EXP-05`; current proposals `C0002-EXP-02`, `C0002-EXP-03`, and `C0002-EXP-07`.
- **Reasoning:** Prior trace can cause interference, but state construction and bounded relays can discard valid dependencies. Contamination reduction and interface loss are simultaneous possible consequences of the same selection and compression operation.
- **Scope:** Agentic benchmarks, multi-turn generation, task switching, conversational memory, long-context QA, and related state-transfer settings represented in the ledger.
- **Limitations:** Direction and magnitude for open-ended research synthesis are unknown. The underlying studies do not fully match compute or isolate all mechanisms.

### Claim ID: C0002-INTG-04 — Multidimensional state-transfer requirement

- **Proposed status:** Accepted methodological requirement; not an empirical performance claim.
- **Evidence and source IDs:** Critic Fatal Problem 2 and Required Corrections; `C0002-INT-04`; `C0002-INT-13`; `C0002-EXT-03`, `C0002-EXT-05`, `C0002-EXT-06`; `C0002-SRC-05` through `C0002-SRC-13`.
- **Reasoning:** Schema compliance, reference-item retention, task-relative dependency coverage, package size, transformation type, relation and status preservation, write loss, and retrieval loss can vary independently. A scalar “state completeness” label would be circular or misleading unless these components are separately predefined and measured.
- **Scope:** Future B001 package and state experiments.
- **Limitations:** The exact inventory, dependency annotation process, transformation taxonomy, weights, and reliability thresholds remain unresolved.

### Claim ID: C0002-INTG-05 — Causal-interpretation gate

- **Proposed status:** Accepted methodological requirement extending `C0001-INTG-02` and `C0001-INTG-03`.
- **Evidence and source IDs:** Critic Material Problems 5 and 7 and Required Corrections 5 and 6; `C0002-INT-07`, `C0002-INT-08`; `C0002-EXT-05` through `C0002-EXT-07`; `C0002-SRC-09` through `C0002-SRC-13`.
- **Reasoning:** Any access, package, or boundary contrast requires manipulation checks, coverage-sensitive outcomes, evaluator reliability, a primary compute-matching rule, and a saved-budget rule before it can receive a causal interpretation.
- **Scope:** Future branch-local experiments and any external result proposed as direct evidence.
- **Limitations:** The branch has not selected the primary budget, tolerance, reallocation policy, minimum coverage threshold, or final lineage codebook.

### Claim ID: C0002-EXP-01 — Boundary-only null

- **Proposed status:** Unresolved and reframed as the residual-boundary null.
- **Evidence and source IDs:** Critic Fatal Problem 1; `C0002-INT-05`; `C0002-EXT-01`, `C0002-EXT-07`; search-bounded gap in `04_external_evidence.md`.
- **Reasoning:** No evidence establishes a stable zero effect. The treatment may collapse once prior trace and visible inputs are equalized. A null result would be interpretable only after a nontrivial residual property is named and verified.
- **Scope:** Package-only persistent versus package-only fresh execution under future full controls.
- **Limitations:** Present support is logical and absence-of-evidence based, not an empirical estimate.

### Claim ID: C0002-EXP-02 — Contamination-reduction benefit

- **Proposed status:** Qualified and narrowed.
- **Evidence and source IDs:** `C0002-EXT-02`; `C0002-SRC-02`, `C0002-SRC-03`, `C0002-SRC-08`; weakening evidence `C0002-SRC-01`, `C0002-SRC-05` through `C0002-SRC-07`; prior narrow context-partition hypothesis `C0001-EXP-04`.
- **Reasoning:** Prior trace can create interference, anchoring, premature commitment, and salience problems. This supports a conditional prior-trace-exclusion mechanism, not an intrinsic fresh-invocation benefit. Gains must survive coverage and dependency-retention controls.
- **Scope:** Conditions containing mismatched, misleading, redundant, or poorly positioned prior trace.
- **Limitations:** Evidence comes mainly from multi-turn generation, task switching, and long-context use rather than staged research synthesis; compute and visible inputs differ.

### Claim ID: C0002-EXP-03 — Interface-loss harm

- **Proposed status:** Qualified supported risk, strengthening but not isolating `C0001-EXP-05`.
- **Evidence and source IDs:** `C0002-EXT-01`, `C0002-EXT-03`; `C0002-SRC-01`, `C0002-SRC-05`, `C0002-SRC-06`, `C0002-SRC-07`; prior claim `C0001-EXP-05`.
- **Reasoning:** Relay and state-writing systems can omit required information before downstream stages can retrieve or use it. The risk is strongest where tasks depend on temporal, causal, sequential, qualification, or support relations.
- **Scope:** Package-mediated workflows and dependency-dense tasks represented in the ledger.
- **Limitations:** The independent contribution of fresh context remains unmeasured; package representation, retrieval, task, and compute are bundled.

### Claim ID: C0002-EXP-04 — State-completeness threshold

- **Proposed status:** Rejected as framed; superseded by multidimensional state-transfer hypotheses under `C0002-INTG-04`.
- **Evidence and source IDs:** Critic Fatal Problem 2; `C0002-INT-04`; `C0002-EXT-03`, `C0002-EXT-05`; `C0002-SRC-05` through `C0002-SRC-11`.
- **Reasoning:** “Completeness” conflates schema compliance, retention, dependency coverage, size, transformation, write quality, and observed success. A threshold inferred from outcomes would be circular. Separate preregistered dimensions and interactions are permissible; the scalar claim is not.
- **Scope:** The exact Explorer wording and proposed 2 × 2 interpretation.
- **Limitations:** Nonlinear effects across separately measured retention and dependency variables remain possible.

### Claim ID: C0002-EXP-05 — Structured-state dominance

- **Proposed status:** Qualified hypothesis with indirect support.
- **Evidence and source IDs:** `C0002-EXT-03`, `C0002-EXT-04`; `C0002-SRC-04`, `C0002-SRC-05`, `C0002-SRC-14`; related prior hypothesis `C0001-EXP-02`.
- **Reasoning:** Structured and selective state can materially affect outcomes, and write quality can dominate retrieval. However, no source directly compares state structure with freshness at matched information and full compute, so “dominance” is not established.
- **Scope:** Long-term memory, multi-turn condensation, and conversational QA systems represented in the ledger.
- **Limitations:** Added prompts, calls, retrieval, graph construction, tools, and benchmark reuse prevent component attribution and independent replication counts.

### Claim ID: C0002-EXP-06 — Hidden-context privilege

- **Proposed status:** Accepted only as a design diagnosis; renamed **prior-trace access asymmetry**.
- **Evidence and source IDs:** Critic Fatal Problem 1 and Material Problem 4; `C0002-INT-03`, `C0002-INT-05`; `C0002-EXT-02`; `C0002-SRC-02`, `C0002-SRC-03`, `C0002-SRC-08`.

That is an observable treatment difference and must be labeled as such. The proposition that unequal access is unequal is not itself a substantive empirical hypothesis.
- **Scope:** Design and interpretation of persistent full-trace versus package-only conditions.
- **Limitations:** The direction of the access effect remains task- and trace-dependent.

### Claim ID: C0002-EXP-07 — Compression as treatment

- **Proposed status:** Accepted in narrowed form as an active factor with unresolved direction.

Benefits and harms depend on retention, relation preservation, salience, model capability, and budget handling.

### Claim ID: C0002-EXP-08 — Stage-specific sensitivity

- **Proposed status:** Unresolved speculation; defer.
- **Evidence and source IDs:** `C0002-INT-05` through `C0002-INT-13`; External Evidence Gaps; Alternative Theorist Remaining Uncertainty.

- **Scope:** Future boundary-placement experiments.
- **Limitations:** Stage-specific effects remain plausible and could be revisited after the core design is coherent.

## Accepted New Facts

The following should enter the branch as source-reported facts, with their stated limitations preserved:

1. **Search-bounded direct-evidence gap:** No source in the fourteen-source C0002 ledger isolates a noncollapsed package-only persistent-versus-package-only fresh effect with matched visible inputs, no prior-trace access, matched tools and sampling, and full compute. Provenance: `04_external_evidence.md`; `C0002-EXT-01`, `C0002-EXT-07`.

2. **Near-direct relay result, preprint:** `C0002-SRC-01` formalizes full accumulated context versus isolated local contexts connected by relays. Its reported equivalence with unlimited relay capacity and gains or reversals under bounded relays support information access and relay sufficiency as operative variables. Local isolation and compression remain bundled, maximum steps are not a full compute ledger, and the tasks are agentic benchmarks. Provenance: `C0002-SRC-01`; `C0002-EXT-01`.

3. **Prior-history interference facts:** `C0002-SRC-02` reports an average 39% drop across six generation tasks when information is distributed over simulated multi-turn conversations rather than given as a fully specified single turn, with unreliability associated with early assumptions and wrong turns. `C0002-SRC-03` reports degradation on many tested task switches when mismatched conversational history precedes the target task. Both change input timing, content, length, and cost and are not fresh-boundary tests. Provenance: `C0002-SRC-02`, `C0002-SRC-03`; `C0002-EXT-02`.

4. **Context-position fact:** `C0002-SRC-08` reports that models often use the same relevant information less effectively when it appears in the middle of a long context than near the beginning or end. This supports salience and retrieval geometry, not reset or freshness. Provenance: `C0002-SRC-08`; `C0002-EXT-02`.

5. **Selective-condensation fact:** `C0002-SRC-04` reports up to 72% history-token reduction in ten-turn dialogues with improved or preserved accuracy across thirteen models and multiple benchmarks. The method adds a condenser, prompts, and a decider and does not match total inference compute. Provenance: `C0002-SRC-04`; `C0002-EXT-04`.

6. **Write-versus-retrieval fact, preprint:** `C0002-SRC-05` reports that four of six tested memory baselines are write-dominant under its diagnostic and that Expected Predictive Compression raises complete-stored-memory score from 0.44 to 0.49 under the reported fixed stored-state budget and readers. It adds a write-time LLM call and does not equalize total end-to-end compute. Provenance: `C0002-SRC-05`; `C0002-EXT-03`.

7. **Long-term dependency benchmark facts:** `C0002-SRC-06` supplies 500 evidence-grounded questions and separates indexing, retrieval, and reading, reporting an approximately 30% sustained-interaction accuracy drop for existing systems. `C0002-SRC-07` supplies human-verified persona and temporal-event graphs over long conversations and reports persistent difficulty with temporal and causal dependencies. These are benchmark and method facts, not boundary effects. Provenance: `C0002-SRC-06`, `C0002-SRC-07`; `C0002-EXT-03`, `C0002-EXT-05`.

8. **Coverage and citation measurement fact:** `C0002-SRC-09` uses a known relevant-insight inventory and source map to score Coverage and Citation separately and reports that even oracle-relevance systems remain more than ten points below the estimated human Joint Score in its setup. This supports coverage controls, not invocation effects. Provenance: `C0002-SRC-09`; `C0002-EXT-05`.

9. **Measurement-method facts:** `C0002-SRC-10` provides grounded factual-error categories and reported annotation-reliability evidence; `C0002-SRC-11` uses reference-derived questions to estimate content retention; `C0002-SRC-12` provides category-sensitive scoring for factual correction; `C0002-SRC-13` reports that factuality metrics vary by generator and error type and that no single metric is best in every setting. None supplies complete multi-stage claim lineage. Provenance: `C0002-SRC-10` through `C0002-SRC-13`; `C0002-EXT-05`, `C0002-EXT-06`.

10. **Structured-memory fact:** `C0002-SRC-14` reports 88.88% on LoCoMo and 86.2% on LongMemEval using an entity-centric temporal property graph, append-only history, conflict-aware multi-tool retrieval, and compact query-time summaries. Components and added inference are bundled, and the benchmarks overlap with `C0002-SRC-06` and `C0002-SRC-07`. Provenance: `C0002-SRC-14`; `C0002-EXT-04`.

11. **Compute-accounting fact:** No located C0002 source matches system and role-prompt processing, context processing, package generation and validation, completions, tools, retrieval, candidates, selection, orchestration, and final-answer generation while declaring a saved-budget rule. Provenance: `C0002-EXT-07`; External Evidence, Fixed-Compute Versus Added-Compute Evidence.

12. **Transfer gap:** No located source directly tests open-ended multi-source research synthesis using B001-style epistemic typing, staged permissions, provenance, and cross-stage claim lineage. Provenance: External Evidence Gaps; Alternative Theorist Remaining Uncertainty.

## Qualified or Narrowed Claims

- `C0002-EXP-02` should be retained only as a conditional **prior-trace interference** hypothesis. It must not be called a fresh-invocation benefit.
- `C0002-EXP-03` should strengthen the existing `C0001-EXP-05` interface-loss risk, while explicitly attributing the risk to package selection, relation loss, retrieval, and dependency structure rather than freshness alone.
- `C0002-EXP-05` should remain a structured-state hypothesis with indirect support, not an accepted dominance claim.
- `C0002-EXP-06` should be recast as the design fact that prior-trace access is an explicit treatment and confound.
- `C0002-EXP-07` should be retained as a compression/transformation factor with potentially positive or negative effects and charged compute.
- `C0002-EXP-01` should be retained only as a residual-boundary null hypothesis whose test is invalid until a noncollapsed residual property exists.
- The C0001 fresh-context-plus-state interaction should be narrowed: any interaction should be expressed across measured retention, dependency coverage, salience, and trace access rather than an undefined scalar completeness level.

## Rejected or Unsupported Claims

- Reject an intrinsic or general fresh-container benefit from the current evidence.
- Reject the original `C0002-EXP-04` scalar state-completeness threshold as circular and non-operational.
- Reject the Explorer’s original 2 × 2 boundary-by-state design as a causal factorial unless prior-trace access is identically controlled or added as a separate factor and state dimensions are independently predefined.
- Reject package-only persistent versus package-only fresh equality as proof of a boundary null when no residual manipulation is defined.
- Reject full-trace persistent versus package-only or fresh differences as evidence for freshness; they estimate access, position, retention, and associated cost.
- Reject structured-memory, condenser, predictive-writing, or graph-retrieval gains as direct evidence for role separation or equal-compute superiority.
- Reject field completion, Markdown structure, or semantic similarity as sufficient evidence of task-relative state retention.
- Reject lower unsupported-claim counts as improvement when claim count, factual coverage, citation completeness, task completion, usefulness, and omission severity are not controlled.
- Reject same-model role agreement as independent validation of any claim or package.
- Reject counting LongMemEval- or LoCoMo-reusing studies as independent benchmark replications.

## Contradictions and Tensions

1. **Trace interference versus valid-context preservation:** `C0002-SRC-02`, `C0002-SRC-03`, and `C0002-SRC-08` show that earlier history can interfere or become hard to use, while `C0002-SRC-01`, `C0002-SRC-05`, `C0002-SRC-06`, and `C0002-SRC-07` show that compression and memory construction can remove information later stages need. The appropriate synthesis is a tradeoff, not a universal direction.

2. **Compression efficiency versus interface loss:** `C0002-SRC-04` reports broad efficiency and outcome benefits from condensation, while `C0002-SRC-01` reports reversals under insufficient relays and `C0002-SRC-05` reports write-side loss. Compression quality, retention policy, and compute are moderators.

3. **Structured-state gains versus component attribution:** `C0002-SRC-14` reports strong benchmark results, but graph construction, append-only state, retrieval, tools, conflict handling, and summaries are bundled. Positive performance does not identify which component caused it.

4. **More context versus usable context:** Full trace can preserve evidence but bury it; concise packages can foreground evidence but delete dependencies. Inventory retention and retrieval geometry must be measured separately.

5. **Null result versus treatment collapse:** Package-matched equality could mean a true zero effect, no distinct residual treatment, weak manipulation, task insensitivity, opposing subgroup effects, or unreliable measurement.

6. **Factual precision versus coverage:** Fewer unsupported claims can result from omission. `C0002-SRC-09`, `C0002-SRC-11`, and `C0002-SRC-13` require joint interpretation of support, coverage, citation, and evaluator behavior.

7. **State budget versus full compute:** Fixed stored-memory tokens or maximum steps do not equal fixed end-to-end inference compute. Package construction can consume budget while shorter context can release it.

8. **Evidence volume versus evidence independence:** The ledger contains benchmark reuse and measurement-source overlap. The number of aligned studies must not be treated as the number of independent mechanisms or datasets.

## Remaining Hypotheses

- **Residual-boundary null:** No stable effect remains after a verified noncollapsed residual manipulation and full controls. Plausible, unproven.
- **Conditional prior-trace interference:** Excluding mismatched, misleading, or poorly positioned trace can improve later stages if valid dependencies remain available.
- **Interface-loss risk:** Package construction can omit, mutate, retype, or separate valid claims, qualifications, support, and relations.
- **State-write bottleneck:** Write-time promotion and classification may explain more downstream failure than retrieval in some regimes.
- **Context-salience and retrieval geometry:** Order, position, adjacency, redundancy, and context volume may change use even at constant inventory retention.
- **Structured-state advantage:** Typed, relational, or selectively retrieved state may outperform raw trace in some regimes, though component and compute attribution remain unresolved.
- **Resource-allocation explanation:** Prompt specialization, added calls, tools, samples, selectors, and saved-budget handling may explain much apparent architecture gain.
- **Task × model × retention interaction:** Stronger models and dependency-dense tasks may benefit more from full context and suffer more from lossy packages; separable or interference-heavy tasks may benefit from filtering.
- **Relation-preservation hypothesis:** Packages preserving claim-support, qualification, contradiction, temporal, and causal relations may outperform flat packages with identical atomic-item coverage.
- **Stage-specific sensitivity:** Some transitions may benefit more from reset or trace restriction, but this remains speculative and should be deferred.

## Unresolved Questions

- Should the branch treat a new conversation container as operationally irrelevant unless it changes observable context, instructions, state, tools, or sampling?
- What canonical stage-package schema should B001 use, and which fields are mandatory for claim identity, epistemic status, support, scope, uncertainty, objections, contradictions, unresolved questions, and lineage?
- How will the branch predeclare a task-relative information inventory and required dependency relations without deriving them from observed condition success?
- How should schema compliance, item retention, dependency coverage, relation preservation, package size, transformation type, write loss, retrieval loss, position, and adjacency be measured separately?
- What package transformations are permitted, and how will deletion, summarization, normalization, reordering, retyping, and support-link mutation be logged?
- What is the primary fixed-compute rule, acceptable tolerance, and saved-budget policy? Which secondary resource measures are mandatory by stage?

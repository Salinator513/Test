## Prior Contradictions or Objections

- **Observability versus causality:** Accepted write–select–use language supports checkpoints, but the current Critic correctly objects that output correspondence and selection logs cannot identify latent causal reliance. The operational endpoint must be observable relation-preserving incorporation.
- **Reliability versus ecological validity:** A narrow codebook may increase agreement by excluding qualification-rich, synthesis-heavy, or ambiguous cases that dominate real research work. Retained coverage and exclusion composition must accompany any agreement result.
- **Selection observability versus behavioral contamination:** A selected-subset report improves observability but may itself change deliberation, exposure, stopping, and resource use.
- **Direct support versus qualification:** Direct support is the accepted primary candidate, while qualification preservation is an accepted attribute and possible alternative focus. Switching the primary relation after seeing the same data would be outcome-driven substitution.
- **Traceability versus support:** IDs, links, citations, and structured fields improve auditability but do not establish support correctness, completeness, or downstream incorporation.
- **Stable identity versus legitimate transformation:** Paraphrase, split, merge, scope change, qualification change, contradiction, synthesis, and epistemic retyping can be legitimate. A single flat identity label would erase these distinctions.
- **Agreement versus construct validity:** Same-model agreement is explicitly non-independent, and annotation agreement alone cannot show that the retained object is relevant or valid.
- **Archival visibility versus write loss:** Existing outputs can show only package-visible relations. Without a reference inventory they cannot show valid relations that were never written.

## Internal Evidence Gaps

1. **No authorized empirical corpus for the proposed archival screen.** Prior raw External Evidence Researcher and Alternative Theorist outputs are outside the allowlist. No candidate pair count, exclusion rate, factual-unit proportion, identity-transition rate, downstream-incorporation rate, or task-stratum density can be computed.
2. **No reference inventory.** Write loss and omitted valid relations cannot be estimated from visible outputs.
3. **No canonical minimal object.** Existing conventions can seed claim ID, source/provenance, status, scope, support, objections, and limitations, but evidence-item boundaries, direct-support relation fields, selected-subset fields, downstream-unit fields, and lineage dimensions require new specification or annotation.
4. **No validated direct-support codebook.** Inclusion, exclusion, evidence-context, partial-support, qualification-only, many-to-many, and ambiguity rules are unresolved.
5. **No independent reliability evidence.** No authorized blinded duplicate coders, held-out audit set, adjudication, prevalence-sensitive statistic, uncertainty estimate, or threshold exists.
6. **No operational output-incorporation definition.** The accepted state still lists final semantic use as unresolved; the current Critic supplies a correction but not a validated codebook.
7. **No fixed sampling frame or denominators.** Outputs, upstream factual units, evidence units, candidate relations, retained relations, and downstream units are not prospectively defined.
8. **No prospective development/audit split.** Existing branch summaries cannot substitute for a frozen held-out validation design.
9. **No accepted gate thresholds or failure policy.** Pair count, retained coverage, reliability, identity stability, role relevance, uncertainty, and intended-experiment requirements remain unset.
10. **No accepted complete resource rule.** Selection checkpoints, validation, adjudication, schema repair, candidates, selectors, context processing, and evaluation costs remain unallocated.

## Questions Requiring External Evidence

1. What primary methods define narrow direct-support relations with explicit inclusion, exclusion, evidence-boundary, and source-context rules?
2. What validated methods separate content correspondence, split/merge structure, scope or qualification change, relation preservation, attribution, contradiction, synthesis, and epistemic-status transition?
3. How do primary annotation studies separate codebook development/calibration from held-out audit, and what failures arise when relation classes or strata are selected post hoc?
4. Which agreement statistics and uncertainty procedures are appropriate for clustered, prevalence-sensitive, many-to-many relation data, and what do they not establish about validity?
5. What adjudication, blinding, annotator-expertise, source-access, and independence procedures are used for complex claim–evidence and lineage annotation?
6. How is output-level relation-preserving incorporation operationalized without equating citation presence, rationale generation, selected evidence, identifiers, or textual correspondence with causal reliance?
7. How do primary studies report retained coverage and exclusion composition alongside reliability, especially in long-form or open-ended synthesis?
8. What methods set decision-specific feasibility thresholds based on the intended next experiment, uncertainty, evaluator burden, and false-passage cost rather than conventional cutoff frequency?
9. What methods handle bounded many-to-many relations while keeping a narrower primary reliability unit?
10. What evidence-unit designs distinguish content-bearing excerpts from locators or source-level references, and when is source access required for support adjudication?

The External Evidence Researcher should not spend the cycle merely re-establishing that no exact matched-resource target-handoff study exists, that selection and incorporation can differ, or that claim–evidence schemas exist. Those points are already stored. The external task is to recover operational procedures, validity evidence, failure modes, and transfer limits that can make the future apparatus prospectively testable.

## Handoff to External Evidence Researcher

Treat the current frontier as a method-design search, not an effectiveness search. Prioritize primary sources with extractable codebooks, sampling frames, evidence-unit rules, development/held-out validation procedures, identity and qualification dimensions, output-incorporation definitions, independent annotation and adjudication, retained-coverage reporting, clustered/prevalence-sensitive reliability, and decision-specific feasibility gates. For every retained source, record what was actually validated, what remained unobservable, and what assumptions would fail when transferred to the B001 External Evidence Researcher → Alternative Theorist handoff.

The branch-local conclusion is limited: the accepted state contains enough concepts to draft a prospective apparatus, but not enough authorized empirical material to measure pair prevalence, identity stability, downstream incorporation, or reliability in C0005. A later pilot must use an explicitly authorized corpus, a development/audit split, genuinely independent coding, and a predeclared failure policy.

# Alternative Theorist — C0005

## Strongest Current Interpretation

The strongest current interpretation is a **prospective small-core feasibility account**.

A narrowly bounded apparatus may be possible if it begins with one atomic upstream factual claim, one content-bearing evidence unit with source identity, an adjudicated direct-support relation, explicit scope or qualification fields, and one downstream unit scored for observable relation-preserving incorporation. Bounded precedents such as `C0005-EXT-01` through `C0005-EXT-06` show that atomic claims, minimal evidence spans, separate relation judgments, multidimensional fields, and output-content correspondence can be operationalized and can sometimes achieve useful agreement.

This interpretation is methodological rather than empirical about B001. The authorized branch corpus contains no pair-prevalence estimate, no independent duplicate coding, no held-out audit, no accepted threshold, and no direct measurement of the target handoff. The strongest defensible claim is therefore that a prospectively frozen apparatus is plausible enough to test, not that it is feasible, representative, beneficial, or causally connected to role separation.

The interpretation also requires the C0005 Critic’s corrections. Development and audit samples must be separate. The corpus, denominators, primary direct-support object, exclusions, identity dimensions, strata, thresholds, and failure actions must be frozen before audit inspection. Qualification preservation must be a required attribute rather than pooled with other relation classes. Recorded selection and final-text correspondence must not be called latent use, reconstruction, or causal reliance.

## Alternative Theory 1

### C0005-ALT-01 — Coverage-Failure and Role-Mismatch Theory

- **Core claim:** A direct-support apparatus may appear reliable only because it filters out the qualification-rich, multi-source, synthesis-heavy, and nonfactual work that is central to the Alternative Theorist role. The apparatus could pass technically while failing to represent the handoff.
- **Evidence status:** Evidence-backed alternative mechanism; untested in B001.

### Mechanism

The apparatus increases apparent measurability by restricting the object to atomic claims, bounded evidence units, simple relation polarity, stable scope, and downstream units with recognizable correspondence. Difficult cases are excluded as partial support, multi-span dependence, many-to-many structure, qualification-only relevance, synthesis, split or merge transformation, or ambiguous identity.

This filtering can raise agreement and simplify adjudication, but it also changes the target. The retained core may measure a small factual-transfer residue rather than the Alternative Theorist’s main function, which includes hypotheses, objections, speculative mechanisms, unresolved questions, and recombination of evidence into competing explanations.

### Evidence it explains

- `C0005-EXT-08` shows that one-claim/one-evidence structure is often not the natural unit: evidence can require multiple sentences, paragraphs, pages, or answers.
- `C0005-EXT-12` shows that useful agreement can be produced by atomicity restrictions, verifier filtering, and exclusion of adjacent semantic domains.
- `C0005-EXT-10` shows that agreement statistics can remain moderate or weak for fine-grained long-form judgments even when majority agreement looks high.
- `C0005-EXP-02` and `C0005-EXP-05` identify qualification dominance and sparse factual reuse as live handoff possibilities.
- The accepted role-aligned requirement `C0004-INTG-04` directly predicts that much legitimate Alternative Theorist output should not be scored as unsupported factual reuse.
- The internal evidence gap in `03_internal_evidence.md` is consistent with this theory because no authorized corpus currently establishes that the retained direct-support object covers a meaningful fraction of the role’s work.

### Evidence it struggles with

- `C0005-EXT-01` through `C0005-EXT-06` show that small, systematic units can be operationally useful in bounded tasks. This theory does not imply that every narrow core is trivial.
- A prospectively frozen audit could show both high reliability and substantial retained coverage within a clearly relevant factual stratum. That result would weaken a broad role-mismatch claim.
- The theory does not by itself explain why a narrow factual-transfer measure would be useless as one secondary diagnostic, even if it cannot represent the entire handoff.

### Predictions

1. Agreement will rise as exclusions increase, while retained coverage and role relevance fall.
2. The highest-agreement subset will contain disproportionately atomic, single-source, low-qualification claims.
3. Qualification-rich, multi-source, and synthesis-heavy units will account for a large share of exclusions or disagreements.
4. Factual relation-preserving incorporation will be sparse even when independent evaluators judge the Alternative Theorist output useful and role-appropriate.
5. A codebook optimized on development data will appear feasible, but a frozen held-out audit with the original denominator will show lower coverage or reliability.
6. Switching to qualification or another relation class may improve role fit, but only in a new prospective validation rather than on the failed audit set.

## Alternative Theory 2

### C0005-ALT-02 — Identity-and-Observability Bottleneck Theory

- **Core claim:** The upstream direct-support relation may be clear and sufficiently frequent, but the apparatus fails because downstream alternative generation legitimately transforms content and because final text cannot reveal causal evidence use.
- **Evidence status:** Evidence-backed alternative mechanism; untested in the target handoff.

### Mechanism

The External Evidence Researcher can provide a valid atomic claim and supporting evidence, yet the Alternative Theorist may split the claim, merge it with another, narrow or generalize it, preserve the proposition while changing qualification, convert a fact into a premise for a hypothesis, reverse it into an objection, or construct a derived synthesis.

A flat identity label treats these distinct transformations as either preservation or loss. A selected-subset checkpoint adds an observable record of selection but still does not reveal whether the evidence causally influenced the final reasoning. Final-text correspondence can also occur without recorded selection or causal dependence. The bottleneck is therefore not necessarily relation prevalence but multidimensional lineage and the limits of observable endpoints.

### Evidence it explains

- `C0005-EXT-04` supports observable source-supported output coding while explicitly not identifying causal reliance.
- `C0005-EXT-05` supports separate linked dimensions rather than one flat identity label.
- `C0005-EXT-06` shows that decomposition can make correspondence more reproducible and that partial presence is common.
- `C0005-EXT-09` shows that exact evidence boundaries can be unstable even when annotators agree on a broader region.
- `C0005-EXT-11` defeats simple lexical or semantic-similarity identity rules.
- `C0005-EXT-13` states that none of the retained observable endpoints identifies causal evidence use.
- `C0005-EXP-03` and the C0005 Critic’s Fatal Problem 1 directly anticipate this bottleneck.

### Evidence it struggles with

- In a deliberately restricted exact-preservation stratum, content, attribution, scope, and epistemic status may remain stable enough for reliable coding. The theory predicts difficulty, not universal impossibility.
- It does not explain low upstream pair prevalence. If few direct-support pairs exist before downstream transformation, coverage failure or a sparse-reuse null is more direct.
- It does not establish that multidimensional human coding will be sufficiently reliable or affordable; it relocates rather than resolves the apparatus problem.

### Predictions

1. Upstream direct-support agreement will be materially higher than downstream lineage agreement.
2. Most downstream disagreements will concentrate in split or merge structure, qualification change, epistemic retyping, and derived synthesis rather than basic source identity.
3. Exact-match measures will report substantial failure while relaxed or multidimensional measures report partial preservation.
4. Recorded selection and observable incorporation will show all four combinations: selected/incorporated, selected/not incorporated, not selected/incorporated, and neither.
5. Adding explicit lineage markers will improve observability but may also alter behavior and resource use.
6. Even a successful output-incorporation codebook will not justify claims about latent use without an intervention that changes evidence availability while holding the remaining apparatus constant.

## Additional Alternative or Null Explanation

### C0005-ALT-03 — Resource, Prompt, and Metric Construction Alternative

- **Core claim:** Apparent apparatus feasibility may be driven primarily by privileged source access, expert annotators, repeated verification, decomposition prompts, favorable filtering, and metric design rather than by the intrinsic measurability of the relation schema.
- **Evidence status:** Strongly evidence-backed as a confound family; relative contributions are unestimated.

The retained precedents add full-document access, domain experts, separate writers and verifiers, multiple judgments, qualification tests, majority voting, reannotation, adjudication, or super-annotator audits. `C0005-EXT-03`, `C0005-EXT-10`, the alternative-explanation section of `04_external_evidence.md`, and the Fixed-Compute section all support this account.

Longer or fuller context can improve adjudication because relevant evidence often lies outside abstracts or spans multiple sentences, but it also increases reading cost and may introduce additional ambiguity. Repeated judgments and adjudication can improve the final reference object through sampling and selection rather than through role separation. Better decomposition and qualification prompts can make units easier to judge. Metric choices can make the same annotation set look stable or unstable through exact versus relaxed boundaries, prevalence effects, clustering rules, distance functions, or filtering.

Selection effects are especially important. A system can appear reliable because disputed units are removed, failed QA units are regenerated, favorable task strata are retained, or easy relations are selected during development. Reporting bias is a further speculative possibility: finalized datasets and successful codebooks are visible in the retained literature, while abandoned schemas, failed strata, and unsuccessful pilot iterations may be less visible. The C0005 evidence does not quantify that bias, so it should remain labeled speculation rather than fact.

This alternative predicts that feasibility estimates will deteriorate when expert access, repeated verification, flexible exclusion, and favorable metric choices are restricted or fully costed. It also predicts substantial sensitivity to the exact boundary tolerance, prevalence, coder expertise, source context, and adjudication protocol.

### C0005-ALT-04 — Sparse-Reuse Minimal Null

- **Core claim:** The selected handoff may simply contain too few role-relevant direct-support incorporation events for this relation to support a useful primary apparatus.
- **Evidence status:** Unresolved null; neither supported nor rejected by the authorized corpus.

This null does not imply that the Alternative Theorist role is ineffective, that upstream evidence is poor, or that relation metadata has no value elsewhere. It says only that direct factual claim–support transfer may be a low-density object at this specific transition because the role is designed to construct alternatives, objections, hybrids, and unresolved questions.

The null predicts low pair prevalence under a fixed denominator, low downstream factual incorporation despite acceptable role performance, and failure of the audit gate without any need to invoke identity instability or bad coding. It struggles if a prospective audit finds a substantial, representative, reliably coded factual-transfer core.

## Hybrid Explanations

Several mechanisms can operate simultaneously.

1. **Small core plus coverage failure:** A reliable atomic core may exist while representing only a minor fraction of qualification-rich and synthesis-heavy work. The correct conclusion would be “useful secondary diagnostic,” not “handoff validated.”
2. **Clear upstream relations plus unstable downstream lineage:** Direct support may be measurable at package construction while split, merge, scope, and epistemic transformations make downstream correspondence difficult.
3. **Expert-enabled feasibility:** The apparatus may work only with full-source access, expert coders, repeated verification, and adjudication. This would establish a resource-intensive upper bound, not a low-cost general procedure.
4. **Metric-dependent pass:** The same audit could pass under relaxed overlap and fail under exact boundaries, or pass on pooled agreement while failing on the sparse positive class. Both results could be technically correct under different estimands.
5. **Role-fit interaction:** Factual-transfer density may vary by task stratum. A predeclared factual-comparison stratum may support a narrow pilot while open-ended alternative generation remains dominated by qualifications and derived synthesis.
6. **Selection-checkpoint interaction:** A recorded-selection checkpoint may improve lineage observability and simultaneously alter deliberation, exposure, and compute. The apparatus can become more measurable because it changed the process being measured.
7. **Multiple resource mechanisms:** More context, better prompts, repeated sampling, expert review, and selection can jointly produce apparent gains. None of these would establish a fresh-role or role-separation effect.

## False Dichotomies or Framing Problems

1. **Feasible versus infeasible:** Apparatus feasibility is multidimensional. A system can be reliable but unrepresentative, representative but unreliable, or useful only under expensive expert conditions.
2. **Direct support versus qualification:** Qualification can be an attribute of a directly supported claim rather than a mutually exclusive relation. Qualification-only relevance should be reported separately without forcing a winner on the current audit.
3. **Stable identity versus identity failure:** Content can be preserved while scope, attribution, relation polarity, or epistemic status changes. Identity must be decomposed.
4. **Selected versus used:** Recorded selection and observable incorporation are different observations; neither establishes causal reliance.
5. **One-to-one versus many-to-many:** A narrow one-to-one reliability unit can coexist with bounded many-to-many structure in the broader schema.
6. **Schema value versus role separation:** Even a successful claim–support apparatus would validate an information and measurement object, not a fresh conversation boundary or seven-role architecture.
7. **High agreement versus valid construct:** Agreement can rise because hard cases were removed, prevalence is imbalanced, or the distance function is permissive.
8. **More context versus better evidence:** Full-source access may reveal necessary support and also add cost, ambiguity, and selection burden. Context length is not a free or monotonic benefit.
9. **More independent roles versus better reasoning:** Separate human roles in dataset construction can reduce direct self-confirmation, but gains can be caused by expertise, repeated sampling, adjudication, and privileged information rather than role separation itself.
10. **Audit failure versus relation uselessness:** Failure may indicate a bad handoff, a bad primary unit, insufficient resources, unstable identity, or a poor metric. It should not automatically reject all relation-aware methods.

## Discriminating Tests

### 1. Frozen Development/Audit Test

Use a predeclared sampling frame and denominator hierarchy. Develop the codebook only on a calibration set. Before opening the audit set, freeze the primary direct-support object, exclusions, strata, identity dimensions, boundary rules, thresholds, and failure actions.

- **Supports small-core feasibility:** audit reliability and retained coverage both meet the prospectively justified gate.
- **Supports coverage failure:** reliability passes only after large exclusions or with low role relevance.
- **Supports metric construction:** pass/fail changes substantially across predeclared reasonable metrics.

### 2. Upstream-versus-Downstream Bottleneck Decomposition

Score upstream claim atomicity, evidence boundaries, and direct-support relation separately from downstream content, attribution, scope, qualification, relation polarity, split/merge structure, and epistemic status.

- **Supports identity bottleneck:** upstream agreement is high while downstream lineage dimensions are unstable.
- **Supports sparse-reuse null:** few upstream pairs are role-relevant before downstream coding begins.
- **Supports general infeasibility:** both upstream and downstream judgments remain unreliable.

### 3. Coverage and Role-Fit Audit

Report the full denominator, exclusions by reason, qualification density, multi-source dependence, factual versus nonfactual downstream unit distribution, and the fraction of Alternative Theorist work covered by the retained object. Obtain a separate role-appropriateness or usefulness judgment that is not reducible to factual reuse.

- **Supports coverage failure or role mismatch:** high reliability coexists with low retained coverage and good role performance outside the retained core.
- **Weakens it:** the core covers a substantial predeclared fraction of role-relevant factual work.

### 4. Resource and Privilege Ablation

Within a later authorized pilot, vary or explicitly compare source context, coder expertise, verification passes, and adjudication while holding the frozen object constant and recording full cost.

- **Supports resource/privilege theory:** feasibility drops sharply without full-source access, experts, repeated judgments, or adjudication.
- **Supports schema robustness:** the apparatus remains reliable and sufficiently representative under a lower-resource protocol.

This is not a role-separation test unless roles, access, prompts, sampling opportunities, and full resources are independently controlled.

### 5. Metric-Robustness Grid

Predeclare strict and relaxed span measures, positive- and negative-class agreement, prevalence, clustering treatment, and at least one relation-aware distance. Report all without selecting the most favorable after inspection.

- **Supports metric-construction theory:** conclusions reverse across reasonable predeclared metrics or are driven by sparse prevalence.
- **Supports robust feasibility:** the pass decision is stable across the metric family and disagreement composition remains acceptable.

### 6. Selection/Output Correspondence Test

Use a common, separately costed selected-subset checkpoint and classify recorded selection and observable incorporation independently. Do not infer causal use.

- **Supports selection-output dissociation:** substantial selected/not-incorporated and not-selected/incorporated categories appear.
- **Weakens a simple direct-transfer model:** final correspondence cannot be predicted from recorded selection alone.

A later no-checkpoint comparison would be needed to estimate whether the checkpoint itself changes behavior.

### 7. Prospective Relation-Class Replication

If direct support fails and qualification appears promising, freeze a new qualification-centered protocol and validate it on a new held-out sample.

- **Supports qualification dominance:** the new prospective audit shows better retained coverage and role relevance without sacrificing reliability.
- **Supports post hoc selection concern:** the apparent advantage disappears outside the development sample.

### 8. Reporting and Selection Transparency Test

Require complete reporting of all drafted codebook variants, excluded strata, failed unit definitions, coder retraining, regenerated items, and threshold changes.

- **Supports selection/reporting explanation:** feasibility depends on undisclosed or extensive iterative filtering.
- **Weakens it:** the prospectively frozen procedure succeeds without favorable-subset substitution.

## Remaining Uncertainty

The cycle still cannot answer the central empirical measurability question because no authorized pair-level corpus was coded. The external literature establishes operational precedents and failure modes, not prevalence or feasibility in B001.

The following remain unresolved:

- whether the selected handoff contains a nontrivial factual direct-support core;
- whether that core represents enough of the Alternative Theorist’s function to justify a primary pilot;
- whether upstream relation reliability or downstream identity is the dominant bottleneck;
- whether qualification preservation should remain an attribute or become a future primary relation;
- how much full-source access, expertise, repeated coding, adjudication, and prompt formalization are required;
- which strict and relaxed identity metrics preserve B001-relevant scope without hiding meaningful changes;
- what branch-specific thresholds and false-passage costs should govern the gate;
- whether a selected-subset checkpoint improves observability enough to justify its behavioral and compute costs;
- whether reporting and favorable-subset selection materially inflate apparent feasibility;
- and whether any apparatus result transfers beyond the tested handoff, task strata, source types, coder population, and resource rule.

No available theory is yet adequate as a complete explanation. The strongest interpretation and the alternatives concern different checkpoints and can all be true in different strata.

## Handoff to Integrator

Preserve the corrected apparatus frontier and do not convert C0005 into an effect claim. The most defensible integrated conclusion is that a small atomic direct-support apparatus is prospectively testable, while three serious rival explanations remain live: the retained core may be unrepresentative of the role, downstream identity and causal observability may be the real bottleneck, and apparent feasibility may depend on privileged resources, repeated verification, filtering, prompt formalization, or metric construction.

The next branch decision should be a prospective pilot specification, not a declaration that the handoff is measurable. It should freeze a development/audit split, denominators, direct-support object, qualification attributes, identity dimensions, metric family, role-fit outcomes, resource ledger, thresholds, and failure actions. The Integrator should preserve the sparse-reuse null and explicitly state that any successful apparatus result would validate only a local measurement object. It would not establish relation benefit, typed-representation benefit, role separation, fresh invocation, or seven-agent superiority.

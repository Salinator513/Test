# Critic — C0005

## Fair Reconstruction of the Explorer’s Direction

The Explorer proposes a deliberately pre-effect frontier: determine whether the External Evidence Researcher → Alternative Theorist handoff contains enough narrowly defined claim–evidence relations to support a later apparatus experiment. The intended sequence is to identify candidate direct-support pairs, define and reliability-test a narrow codebook, trace claim identity through downstream transformations, distinguish written-package presence from selected-subset inclusion and final output correspondence, and use a predeclared measurability gate before attempting any relation-availability or representation comparison.

The favored object is a specific upstream factual claim linked to a specific evidence unit, with preserved scope and qualification, followed into downstream Alternative Theorist text. The Explorer also keeps open three important failure possibilities: direct-support reuse may be sparse, qualification relations may fit the handoff better, and claim identity or selection-to-output transfer may be the actual apparatus bottleneck. The proposal explicitly does not claim that the handoff is uniquely sensitive, that explicit relations improve performance, that typed representation is superior, or that role separation is beneficial.

That reconstruction is fair and substantially aligned with `C0004-INTG-02`, `C0004-INTG-04`, `C0004-INTG-05`, `C0004-INTG-08`, and `C0004-INTG-10`.

## What Is Strong or Worth Preserving

1. The frontier is correctly narrowed to measurability and apparatus fit rather than effectiveness. This is already adequate and should be preserved.
2. The Explorer correctly separates package presence, selected-subset inclusion, and downstream output correspondence. This preserves the accepted write–select–use distinction.
3. The proposal recognizes that Alternative Theorist outputs legitimately contain hypotheses, speculation, objections, and unresolved questions. It therefore avoids treating all non-reuse as unsupported output.
4. The direct-support codebook is not silently pooled with contextualization, qualification, contradiction, exemplification, motivation, or ambiguous relevance.
5. The proposal treats low reuse density, identity instability, qualification dominance, and a small reliable core as possible outcomes rather than assuming the apparatus will succeed.
6. The proposed failure gate is directionally correct: a failed screen should revise the handoff, relation class, or schema rather than generate an effectiveness claim.
7. The Explorer explicitly preserves locality and does not infer role separation, fresh-invocation benefit, or full-loop superiority from a successful measurability result.

## Fatal Problems

### 1. “Actual semantic use” is not identifiable from the proposed observations

The proposal repeatedly asks whether a relation was “actually semantically used,” whether an unselected relation was “reconstructed,” and whether a cited relation was merely post hoc rationalization. Final text, copied identifiers, and a selected-subset checkpoint can show observable correspondence or non-correspondence, but they cannot establish the reader’s latent causal reliance. A downstream statement matching an upstream relation may have been independently generated; a selected relation absent from the final text may still have influenced reasoning; and a citation-like reference cannot distinguish genuine reliance from rationalization.

This is fatal to the endpoint as currently named, not to the overall frontier. Replace the construct with an observable endpoint such as **downstream relation-preserving incorporation**. Define it only in terms of output content, attribution, scope, qualification, and explicit lineage markers. Use labels such as “recorded selection with output incorporation,” “recorded selection without observable incorporation,” and “output incorporation without recorded selection.” Do not call the last category reconstruction or causal use.

### 2. The measurability gate is circular unless development and evaluation are separated

The Explorer defers exact thresholds, allows relation-class comparison, proposes finding usable task strata, and asks what minimum retained coverage is acceptable. If the same sample is used to define the codebook, choose the relation class, identify favorable strata, set thresholds, and decide whether the gate passes, the apparatus can be made to succeed by retaining only easy pairs or favorable subsets.

The correction is mandatory: use a development/calibration sample to draft rules and a separately held-out audit sample to evaluate the frozen rules. Freeze the sampling frame, primary relation class, denominators, exclusions, identity rules, gate dimensions, thresholds, and failure actions before opening the held-out audit set. Any switch from direct support to qualification must start a new prospective validation, not be declared successful on the sample that motivated the switch.

## Material Problems

1. **No fixed denominator or sampling frame.** “Enough candidate pairs,” “prevalence,” and “downstream-reuse density” are uninterpretable without a predeclared corpus, output unit, claim unit, evidence unit, and denominator. Pair counts can be inflated by splitting claims, excerpts, or many-to-many links. Report claim-level and output-level denominators, exclusions, and clustering by task, source, and document.

2. **Archived outputs cannot reveal write loss without a reference inventory.** Existing External Evidence Researcher text can support a visible-package screen, but it cannot show which valid relations were never written unless a separate reference inventory exists. The current cycle may estimate package-visible relation prevalence and package-to-output correspondence; it must not claim a full write–select–use decomposition from archival outputs alone.

3. **The relation-class comparison invites outcome-driven substitution.** Direct support, qualification, contradiction, and contextualization are not necessarily parallel, mutually exclusive classes. A qualification can be part of a factual claim and itself be directly supported. Keep direct support as the primary screen, treat scope and qualification preservation as attributes, and report other relation classes descriptively. A different primary relation requires a new frozen protocol.

4. **Independent annotation is assumed rather than provisioned.** The two research roles can identify methods and branch-local objects, but they do not by themselves constitute a blinded duplicate annotation study. No reliability result should be claimed unless multiple genuinely independent coders, blinding, adjudication, and a defined unit of analysis are actually authorized and executed.

5. **Agreement is not construct validity.** A narrow codebook can achieve high agreement by excluding difficult but important cases. The screen must report retained coverage, exclusion composition, role relevance, and disagreements, not only an agreement statistic.

6. **Task-stratum concentration is vulnerable to post hoc cherry-picking.** Strata must be selected from branch objectives and observable task properties before audit coding. “Which strata work” is exploratory on the development set only; it cannot justify a general pilot on the held-out set without prospective validation.

7. **Identity coding currently mixes several dimensions.** Exact preservation, paraphrase, split, merge, generalization, narrowing, scope change, qualification weakening, contradiction, epistemic-status transition, and derived synthesis should not be forced into one flat mutually exclusive label. At minimum, separate content identity, scope/qualification change, relation preservation, and epistemic-status transition.

8. **The gate lacks decision-specific thresholds.** Literature conventions alone cannot decide what pair count, agreement, or reuse rate is sufficient for this branch. Thresholds should be tied to the intended next experiment, expected uncertainty, evaluator burden, and the consequence of false passage. Report confidence intervals or uncertainty ranges rather than only point estimates.

9. **The selected-subset checkpoint remains an intervention.** The Explorer acknowledges this, but the dry run risks treating it as an ordinary observation. It can improve observability of recorded selection, but it cannot recover natural latent retrieval or prove use. It must remain common, separately costed, and excluded from claims about unprompted behavior.

10. **The current questions exceed the likely evidence available from one pass.** Questions about annotator disagreements, reliability, and minimum retained coverage require an executed coding exercise, not merely repository review or external literature search. Researchers should distinguish what they can directly establish, what they can only design, and what remains for a later pilot.

## Minor Problems

1. “Stable pair” is used for both stable relation judgment and stable downstream identity. These should be named separately.
2. “Specific evidence excerpts or locators” combines content-bearing evidence with pointers. A locator alone may not permit support adjudication without source access.
3. “Source interpretation” is grouped with factual assertions as requiring upstream support, but some interpretations may be derived conclusions whose support structure differs from direct factual support.
4. “Realistic outputs” is undefined and can conceal convenience sampling.
5. “Small reliable core” needs a nontriviality criterion stated before audit rather than after exclusions.
6. The identity stress test compares semantic matching with human coding without specifying a gold standard. Agreement between methods does not by itself establish correctness.

## Hidden Assumptions

- The sampled prior handoffs are representative of future handoffs built under a new apparatus.
- The Internal Evidence Researcher will be explicitly authorized to inspect the required prior-cycle outputs; this cannot be assumed from the current prompt.
- Upstream claims are sufficiently atomic to define candidate relations without imposing the evaluator’s preferred decomposition.
- Evidence excerpts contain enough context for direct-support adjudication.
- Annotators have adequate domain and source access.
- Pair-level observations can be treated as independent despite clustering within outputs, sources, topics, and claims.
- A high-agreement narrow class remains relevant to the Alternative Theorist’s actual function.
- Claim identity can be coded without privileging one preferred downstream reasoning path.
- Explicit pair references improve observability without materially changing the behavior being observed.
- External precedents can supply branch-specific pass thresholds.
- A single scalar notion of “best combination” can balance reliability, coverage, downstream relevance, and role fit without normative weighting.

## Scope and Definition Problems

The following terms must be operationally fixed before audit coding:

- bounded sample and sampling frame;
- atomic factual claim;
- evidence item, excerpt, locator, and source-level unit;
- direct support and partial support;
- relation correctness;
- scope and qualification preservation;
- candidate pair and deduplication rule;
- stable relation versus stable identity;
- downstream relevance;
- observable downstream relation-preserving incorporation;
- citation-only correspondence;
- split, merge, generalization, narrowing, derived synthesis, and epistemic-status transition;
- retained coverage and its denominator;
- nontrivial pair count;
- reliability statistic, unit, adjudication rule, and threshold;
- task stratum and the rule for assigning it;
- gate failure action.

The first pilot should avoid claiming complete many-to-many lineage. It may retain bounded many-to-many links in the schema while predeclaring a narrower primary unit for reliability testing.

## Risks of Misleading Research

1. A high pair count could be manufactured by over-segmenting claims or evidence.
2. High agreement could be achieved by excluding qualification-rich, synthesis-heavy, or ambiguous cases that dominate real research work.
3. A favorable task stratum could be selected after inspection and then presented as if prospectively chosen.
4. Output similarity could be mislabeled as evidence use, reconstruction, or causal influence.
5. A selected-subset checkpoint could create the behavior later attributed to the handoff’s natural structure.
6. Sparse factual reuse could be misread as role failure despite the Alternative Theorist’s authorized nonfactual work.
7. A switch to qualification could appear empirically justified when it was selected because direct support failed on the same data.
8. Literature-reported reliability thresholds could be imported without regard to different units, prevalence, annotator expertise, or decision costs.
9. Archival outputs could be treated as evidence about future standardized packages even though the proposed apparatus changes representation and observability.
10. Pair-level percentages could overstate precision by ignoring clustering and repeated sources or tasks.

## Questions That Would Not Actually Discriminate

1. “Are there enough candidate pairs?” does not discriminate without a fixed denominator, confidence interval, and predeclared sufficiency threshold.
2. “Can semantic use be identified from text alone, or is a selected subset required?” presents a false resolution. A selected subset records selection but still does not identify latent causal use.
3. “Which task or claim strata show enough density?” is non-discriminating if strata are chosen after seeing results.
4. “What minimum retained coverage is acceptable?” cannot be answered empirically from the same sample used to optimize the codebook and cannot be imported mechanically from external convention.
5. “Which relation class yields the best combination?” does not discriminate among hypotheses unless the dimensions and weights are fixed in advance; it otherwise selects a winner by flexible scoring.
6. “What reliability thresholds are used in prior work?” does not establish which threshold is valid for this branch.
7. “Are there analogous handoff studies?” may provide methods but does not establish measurability in B001 and risks repeating the accepted exact-handoff evidence gap.
8. Comparing semantic matching with human coding does not identify the better identity method without an independent adjudicated reference.
9. Selected-but-unused and unselected-but-used categories do not identify causal selection–use dissociation; they identify only recorded-selection/output-correspondence patterns.

## Required Corrections to the Research Direction

1. Preserve the frontier as apparatus measurability only.
2. Replace “actual semantic use” with **observable downstream relation-preserving incorporation** and prohibit causal reliance claims.
3. Define a fixed sampling frame and explicit denominators before coding.
4. Separate a development/calibration sample from a held-out audit sample.
5. Freeze the primary direct-support object, codebook, exclusions, identity rules, strata, gate dimensions, thresholds, and failure actions before the audit.
6. Use this minimal primary object: one upstream atomic factual claim; one specific evidence unit with source identity; an adjudicated direct-support relation; preserved scope or qualification fields; and one downstream unit assessed for content, attribution, scope, qualification, and epistemic-status correspondence.
7. Treat qualification preservation as a required attribute of the direct-support object. Report qualification-only and other relation classes descriptively; do not switch the primary estimand on the audit set.
8. Separate identity dimensions: content correspondence, split/merge structure, scope or qualification change, relation preservation, and epistemic-status transition.
9. Distinguish package-visible prevalence from unobservable write loss when no reference inventory exists.
10. Treat selected-subset inclusion as recorded selection only. Keep the checkpoint common and separately costed; do not infer natural retrieval or causal use.
11. Require genuinely independent duplicate coding, blinding, adjudication, and unit-specific reliability before claiming the apparatus is reliable.
12. Report retained coverage, exclusion reasons, clustering, uncertainty, and role relevance alongside agreement.
13. Tie gate thresholds to the requirements of the intended next experiment rather than literature popularity.
14. Predeclare that failure can mean: revise the codebook, change the handoff, change the primary relation in a new prospective pilot, or stop the relation-effect direction. Do not choose among these after seeing the audit without labeling the choice exploratory.
15. Do not infer handoff sensitivity, relation benefit, role benefit, or fresh-role benefit from any measurability result.

## Internal Research Brief

The Internal Evidence Researcher should remain branch-local and use only files explicitly authorized in that role’s prompt. If prior External Evidence Researcher and Alternative Theorist outputs are not allowlisted, the researcher must report that the proposed archival screen cannot be executed rather than reconstructing it from accepted summaries.

The internal task is to determine whether the branch contains enough **observable material and fields** to design the corrected apparatus, not to manufacture a reliability result from one coder.

Required outputs:

1. Specify the exact authorized corpus and a prospective sampling frame. Distinguish development material from material reserved for a later held-out audit.
2. Define the denominator hierarchy: outputs, upstream factual units, evidence units, candidate relations, retained direct-support relations, and downstream units.
3. Draft the minimal direct-support object using only existing branch conventions where possible. Mark every field that would require new annotation or source access.
4. Separate package-visible relation prevalence from write loss. Do not estimate omitted upstream relations without a reference inventory.
5. Draft explicit inclusion and exclusion rules for direct support, partial support, contextualization, qualification-only, contradiction, exemplification, motivation, source-level evidence, ambiguous relevance, and many-to-many cases.
6. Draft a multidimensional identity and lineage codebook separating content, split/merge, scope or qualification, relation preservation, attribution, and epistemic-status transition.
7. Define observable downstream relation-preserving incorporation. Prohibit labels that imply causal reliance, reconstruction, or post hoc rationalization unless independently observed.
8. Segment downstream units by epistemic role so factual support requirements are not imposed on legitimate hypotheses, speculation, objections, or unresolved questions.
9. Identify prospective task strata from branch-defined properties before looking for favorable results. Report clustering risks.
10. Propose a development/audit split, duplicate-coding procedure, blinding, adjudication, uncertainty reporting, and gate failure actions. Do not claim reliability unless independent duplicate annotation is actually performed.
11. Identify which C0005 questions can be answered now, which can only be designed, and which require a later authorized pilot.
12. Report evidence against the favored direction, including sparse visible pairs, unstable identity, low role relevance, excessive exclusions, qualification dominance, or inability to observe downstream incorporation.

## External Research Brief

The External Evidence Researcher should not repeat the accepted finding that no exact matched-resource study tests the target handoff, and should not re-establish merely that claim–evidence schemas, relation annotations, or selection–use problems exist. The search should be methodological and discriminating.

Prioritize primary sources that provide operational details for:

1. narrow direct-support inclusion and exclusion rules;
2. evidence-unit boundary selection and source-context requirements;
3. held-out codebook development and validation;
4. claim identity under paraphrase, split, merge, scope change, qualification change, contradiction, and synthesis;
5. output-level evidence incorporation distinct from citation presence, rationale generation, or recorded selection;
6. many-to-many relation annotation and multidimensional lineage;
7. independent annotation, blinding, adjudication, prevalence-sensitive agreement, and uncertainty reporting;
8. the reliability-versus-retained-coverage tradeoff in long-form or open-ended synthesis;
9. consequences of post hoc stratum or relation-class selection;
10. methods for setting decision-specific feasibility thresholds rather than copying conventional cutoffs.

For every retained source, extract the annotation unit, sampling frame, prevalence, annotator expertise, source access, codebook-development procedure, held-out validation status, agreement metric, adjudication method, construct-validity evidence, retained coverage, known failure modes, and transfer limits.

Seek evidence that could defeat or narrow the Explorer’s favored direction: narrow direct-support classes that remain unreliable; high agreement with poor validity; identity methods that fail under synthesis; output correspondence that cannot distinguish reliance; qualification-rich cases excluded by reliable codebooks; or methods whose apparent success depends on privileged annotators, source access, or task-specific schemas.

Do not use prior-study threshold frequency as proof of an appropriate B001 gate. External evidence may inform candidate procedures and consequences, but the branch must justify its own thresholds prospectively.

## Handoff

Proceed to Internal Evidence Research only with the corrected frontier. The direction itself is worth preserving, but the researchers must not treat output correspondence as causal semantic use, must not optimize and evaluate the apparatus on the same sample, and must not claim reliability without genuinely independent duplicate coding. The immediate goal is a frozen, prospectively testable apparatus specification and an honest account of whether the authorized branch corpus can support it.

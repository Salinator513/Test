# Integrator — C0005

## Research Question Addressed

Does the External Evidence Researcher → Alternative Theorist handoff contain a sufficiently large, stable, role-relevant, and reliably codable set of narrowly defined direct claim–support relations to justify a later relation-availability or representation experiment?

C0005 does not answer that empirical question. The authorized branch-local corpus contained no raw prior handoff outputs suitable for pair counting or independent coding, and no prospective B001 audit was executed. The cycle instead determines what a defensible measurability apparatus would require, what can be learned from adjacent primary-source methods, and which interpretations must remain unresolved.

## Evidence Assessment

### Branch-local evidence

The internal record establishes an important negative result: the authorized C0005 corpus did not include raw External Evidence Researcher and Alternative Theorist outputs, a reference inventory, independent duplicate annotations, or a held-out audit set. Therefore C0005 could not estimate candidate-pair prevalence, exclusion rates, identity-transition rates, downstream incorporation rates, retained coverage, role relevance, or annotation reliability. This is stated directly in `03_internal_evidence.md`, especially Internal Evidence Gaps 1–9, and is consistent with prior accepted gaps under `C0003-INTG-04`, `C0004-INTG-02`, `C0004-INTG-05`, `C0004-INTG-08`, and `C0004-INTG-10`.

The accepted branch state does contain enough conceptual material to draft a prospective apparatus: claim IDs, epistemic status, provenance, scope and limitations, objections, contradictions, rejections, and unresolved questions are already represented. However, these conventions are dispersed and do not constitute a validated canonical claim–evidence, selected-subset, downstream-unit, or lineage schema. The internal evidence therefore supports apparatus design, not apparatus validation.

### External evidence

The sixteen-source C0005 external ledger provides operational precedents rather than a direct target-handoff result.

- `C0005-EXT-01` and `C0005-SRC-01`–`C0005-SRC-02` show that bounded fact-verification tasks can define atomic claims, relation labels, and evidence rationales with useful but imperfect agreement.
- `C0005-EXT-02` and `C0005-SRC-03`–`C0005-SRC-04` show that content-bearing evidence often requires full-source access, multiple sentences, multiple paragraphs, tables, figures, or multiple acceptable evidence units; a locator or source-level citation alone is not enough for adjudication.
- `C0005-EXT-03` and `C0005-SRC-01`–`C0005-SRC-04` show that separate writers, annotators, verifiers, and adjudicators are feasible, but those designs add expertise, source access, labor, passes, and decision opportunities.
- `C0005-EXT-04`, `C0005-EXT-06`, `C0005-SRC-05`, `C0005-SRC-09`, and `C0005-SRC-10` support observable output-content and source-attribution coding. They do not identify latent causal reliance on an evidence item.
- `C0005-EXT-05`, `C0005-SRC-07`, and `C0005-SRC-08` support linked, overlapping, and multidimensional relation objects rather than one flat identity label.
- `C0005-EXT-07` and `C0005-SRC-14` support separating codebook development and favorable-subset selection from held-out evaluation.
- `C0005-EXT-08`–`C0005-EXT-12` and `C0005-SRC-02`–`C0005-SRC-13`, `C0005-SRC-15` show material adverse findings: one-to-one relations are often not the natural unit; exact boundaries are unstable; high percentage agreement or one chance-corrected statistic does not establish validity; lexical or embedding similarity is insufficient for identity; and filtering can manufacture a high-agreement residue while discarding qualification-rich and synthesis-heavy work.
- `C0005-EXT-13` and `C0005-SRC-01`–`C0005-SRC-05`, `C0005-SRC-09`, `C0005-SRC-10` establish a search-bounded measurement limit: none of the retained observable endpoints identifies counterfactual or causal evidence use.

### Reliability, independence, and transfer

All sixteen retained sources are primary, but they are not sixteen independent validations of one apparatus. They cluster into fact-verification, full-document evidence selection, summarization and attribution, span and overlapping-relation annotation, paraphrase stress testing, and general methodological families. Pyramid and QAPyramid are directly related; SciFact, FEVER, and Evidence Inference share verification assumptions; AIS and FRANK share long-form output-evaluation concerns; biomedical sources share domain structure and expert-access requirements. Same-model or same-method agreement is not independent validation under `AGENT_PROTOCOL.md` and prior `C0001-INTG-03` and `C0004-INTG-01` reasoning.

Transfer to the B001 handoff remains unestablished. No retained source studies an External Evidence Researcher → Alternative Theorist transition, no source measures the prevalence of direct-support incorporation in B001, and no source supplies a branch-specific gate threshold or failure policy.

### Role separation and compute

The evidence does not isolate role separation from added compute or privileged resources. Positive annotation precedents use separate human roles, experts, full-source access, multiple judgments, verification, reannotation, adjudication, qualification tests, majority voting, or reference construction. These support independent annotation procedures, not superiority of fresh conversations or a multi-role inference architecture. `C0005-EXT-03`, the C0005 Fixed-Compute Versus Added-Compute Evidence section, and `C0005-ALT-03` reinforce prior `C0001-INTG-02`, `C0002-INTG-05`, `C0003-INTG-06`, and `C0004-INTG-09`.

## Main Interpretation

The strongest defensible interpretation is narrower than the Alternative Theorist’s “small-core feasibility” label: a small atomic direct-support apparatus is **prospectively testable**, but its feasibility, prevalence, representativeness, reliability, and value at the target handoff are unresolved.

The prospective primary object should be one upstream atomic factual claim, one content-bearing evidence unit with source identity and sufficient context, an adjudicated direct-support relation, explicit scope and qualification attributes, and one downstream unit assessed on observable correspondence dimensions. The downstream endpoint must be called **observable downstream relation-preserving incorporation**, not “actual semantic use.” It should separately record content correspondence, attribution, scope and qualification, relation polarity, epistemic-status transition, split or merge structure, contradiction, and unsupported extension.

The Critic’s two fatal objections are accepted. First, final text, citations, rationale, copied IDs, and selected-subset records do not identify latent causal reliance, reconstruction, or post hoc rationalization. Second, apparatus optimization and evaluation on the same sample would be circular. A development/calibration set must be separated from a held-out audit set, and the corpus, denominators, primary relation, codebook, exclusions, identity dimensions, strata, metrics, thresholds, and failure actions must be frozen before opening the audit set.

Direct support remains the primary candidate for the first prospective audit because that is the accepted C0004 direction. Scope and qualification preservation are required attributes of that object. Qualification-only, contradiction, contextualization, and other relations may be reported descriptively, but a switch in primary relation must begin a new prospectively frozen validation rather than being declared successful on the sample that caused direct support to fail.

No effectiveness claim follows from successful measurability. A passing audit would validate only a local measurement object under the tested corpus, task strata, source access, coder population, codebook, metrics, and resource rule. It would not establish that relation metadata improves output, that typed fields outperform prose, that the handoff is uniquely sensitive, or that role separation, fresh invocation, or the seven-agent loop is superior.

## Alternative Interpretations

### Coverage failure and role mismatch

`C0005-ALT-01` is a serious evidence-backed alternative. Atomic direct-support coding may achieve useful agreement only by excluding multi-source, qualification-rich, synthesis-heavy, ambiguous, or nonfactual work central to the Alternative Theorist role. `C0005-EXT-08`, `C0005-EXT-10`, `C0005-EXT-12`, `C0005-SRC-02`–`C0005-SRC-04`, `C0005-SRC-06`, `C0005-SRC-10`, and prior `C0004-INTG-04` support this risk. It remains unresolved because no B001 retained-coverage or role-relevance audit exists.

### Identity and observability bottleneck

`C0005-ALT-02` is also evidence backed. Upstream support may be clear while downstream split, merge, scope change, qualification change, contradiction, synthesis, attribution change, or epistemic retyping defeats a flat lineage code. `C0005-EXT-04`–`C0005-EXT-06`, `C0005-EXT-09`, `C0005-EXT-11`, `C0005-EXT-13`, and `C0005-SRC-05`, `C0005-SRC-07`–`C0005-SRC-11` support multidimensional coding and reject simple similarity or causal-use labels. Whether this is the dominant target-handoff bottleneck is unresolved.

### Resource, prompt, and metric construction

`C0005-ALT-03` is accepted as a confound family, not as a measured dominant cause. Full-source access, expert coders, repeated verification, adjudication, decomposition prompts, qualification tests, filtering, boundary tolerances, prevalence, clustering, and distance functions can change apparent feasibility. `C0005-EXT-03`, `C0005-EXT-09`, `C0005-EXT-10`, the C0005 alternative-explanation and fixed-compute sections, and `C0005-SRC-01`–`C0005-SRC-16` support this family. Relative contributions are unestimated.

### Sparse-reuse minimal null

`C0005-ALT-04` remains unresolved. The target handoff may contain too few role-relevant factual direct-support incorporation events even when the Alternative Theorist performs well. No authorized C0005 corpus can confirm or reject this null. Sparse reuse must not be interpreted as role failure because the role legitimately generates hypotheses, speculation, objections, hybrids, and unresolved questions under `AGENT_PROTOCOL.md` and `C0004-INTG-04`.

### Hybrid account

The alternatives are compatible. A small reliable core may exist while having low role coverage; upstream relation coding may be reliable while downstream lineage is unstable; expert access and repeated adjudication may create an expensive upper bound; and a selected-subset checkpoint may improve observability while changing the process and cost. No evidence estimates the relative contribution of these mechanisms.

## Contradictions and Tensions

- **Narrow reliability versus ecological validity:** Atomic and filtered units can improve agreement while excluding the work most relevant to alternative generation.
- **One-to-one tractability versus natural many-to-many evidence:** A narrow primary unit may be useful for reliability testing even when the broader schema must permit multiple evidence spans, sources, and claims.
- **Exact versus relaxed boundaries:** Strict matching detects boundary instability; relaxed overlap can improve agreement while hiding scope or qualification changes.
- **Independent coding versus resource neutrality:** Independent annotators and adjudication improve audit quality but add labor, source access, passes, and selection opportunities.
- **Observable incorporation versus causal use:** Output correspondence is measurable, but causal evidence dependence remains unobservable without a separate intervention.
- **Selection observability versus behavioral contamination:** A selected-subset checkpoint records selection while potentially changing deliberation, exposure, stopping, and compute.
- **Direct support versus qualification:** Qualification can be an attribute of direct support and a separate relation class; forcing a winner on the same audit creates circular substitution.
- **Agreement versus construct validity:** Reliability can rise because prevalence is imbalanced, difficult cases are excluded, or permissive distances are chosen.
- **Decomposition versus target preservation:** Systematic claim or QA decomposition can improve reproducibility while changing the object and potentially encoding an evaluator-preferred reasoning path.
- **Full-source access versus scalable apparatus:** Full context may be necessary for correct adjudication but increases cost, ambiguity, and selection burden.
- **Successful measurability versus effectiveness:** A local measurement object can be reliable without relation metadata improving any downstream outcome.
- **Successful apparatus versus role separation:** Even a valid direct-support apparatus would not establish a fresh boundary, role-specific benefit, or seven-agent superiority.

## Remaining Hypotheses

- **Prospective small-core feasibility:** A frozen atomic direct-support object may pass a local audit on reliability, retained coverage, identity, role relevance, and uncertainty.
- **Coverage-failure and role-mismatch:** The reliable residue may cover too little qualification-rich, multi-source, synthesis-heavy, or nonfactual work.
- **Identity-and-observability bottleneck:** Upstream relations may be clear while downstream multidimensional lineage is unstable; causal use remains unidentified.
- **Sparse-reuse minimal null:** The selected handoff may legitimately contain too few role-relevant factual incorporation events.
- **Qualification-centered future apparatus:** Qualification may prove a better primary relation, but only through a new prospective protocol and held-out audit.
- **Resource and privilege dependence:** Feasibility may depend on full-source access, domain expertise, repeated coding, adjudication, and reference construction.
- **Metric-construction sensitivity:** Pass or failure may depend on exact versus relaxed boundaries, positive-class prevalence, clustering, distance functions, and filtering.
- **Task-stratum interaction:** A predeclared factual stratum may be measurable even when the handoff as a whole is not; no favorable stratum is currently identified.
- **Selected-subset intervention:** The checkpoint may improve observability and alter behavior; a later no-checkpoint comparison would be required to estimate its effect.
- **Hybrid account:** Small-core feasibility, low coverage, identity instability, resource privilege, and metric sensitivity may coexist in different strata.

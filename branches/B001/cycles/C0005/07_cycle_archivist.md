The source ledger shows that evidence is often multi-sentence, multi-paragraph, multi-page, multi-answer, overlapping, or many-to-many. The broader apparatus must preserve bounded multi-unit and many-to-many structure and must report how much material is excluded by the one-to-one primary unit.

2. **Boundary-instability statement corrected.** PICO directly supports a large exact-versus-relaxed span-agreement difference. PARC supports field-specific span reliability and low agreement for an underspecified supplement field, but it does not by itself establish the same exact-versus-relaxed evidence-boundary result. The accepted fact is therefore that boundary stability is metric- and field-dependent, with PICO supplying the direct strict-versus-relaxed comparison.

3. **`C0005-SRC-16` provenance narrowed.** Sim and Lewis supports decision-specific pilot sizing, precision, and false-passage considerations by analogy. It does not support agreement metrics, class-specific reliability, or a numeric B001 threshold. It is retained only for prospective sizing and gate-justification principles.

4. **Anti-circularity provenance qualified.** `C0005-SRC-14` is a general methodological analogy from neuroscience. It supports independence between selection/development and evaluation, but it does not validate any specific B001 sampling frame, audit split, or threshold.

5. **Source-context requirement qualified.** The apparatus must record source identity, locator, content-bearing evidence, and the amount of context available for adjudication. Full-source access is sometimes necessary in cited tasks, but it is not imposed universally without prospective evidence-unit rules.

6. **Broad source ranges narrowed in accepted state.** Several Integrator support lines cite `C0005-SRC-01` through `C0005-SRC-16` for composite claims. The final state uses narrower provenance by claim component so that indirect statistical or methodological sources are not presented as direct claim–evidence apparatus evidence.

7. **“Same-method agreement” wording qualified.** `AGENT_PROTOCOL.md` directly rejects same-model repetition or agreement as independent evidence. The C0005 Source Reliability section, not the protocol alone, supports the separate conclusion that overlapping method families are not independent replications.

- Small-core feasibility, coverage failure, identity/observability bottlenecks, sparse reuse, resource dependence, and metric sensitivity remain unresolved.
